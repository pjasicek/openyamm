Chedian in a MAW game

  • The fourth continent: its own level bank, its own ladder (bands up to 100, Horizontal up to about 210, Beyond Madness to 500) and a fourth continent for Elder and Ultimate.
  • Start: a Chedian game starts at Ravensford (Bootcamp). Sturmford City's dimensional pedestal is the continent teleporter, like New Sorpigal's, Harmondale's and Ravenshore's.
  • Loot from other worlds (Rules panel, new game, fixed for the adventure): on, MM9 items drop on every continent and MM6–MM8 items in Chedian. Off, a game that never goes to Chedian plays as before MM9.
  • Quests, monsters, shops and every map's points of interest are in the MM9 guide ↗; this section is what MAW changes.

Hjordisa the Hag: skill reset and Dimensional Maps

Hjordisa the Hag, out in Guberland, is Chedian's Oracle, Judge Grey and Seer in one. Her first page gains three topics in a MAW game:

  • Skill reset / Reset skills: MAW's respec, as on the other continents: every trained skill point back, for gold by level. Alchemy stays; masteries return once a skill meets their level again.
  • Dimensional Maps: the same quest as the Oracle's (any character at level 80 starts it; see Dimension maps). Her maps open completed MM6–MM8 dungeons: Chedian's own dungeons are not in the pool yet.

Bounties: the Jarls

MM9's towns are maps of their own, separate from the region around them, so a Chedian bounty is asked for in the city and hunted outside it:

  1. Clear the region first. Complete the region's map (Sturmford, Drangheim…) since its last refill. Until then the Jarl answers "You need to clear Sturmford COMPLETELY first."
  2. Ask the Jarl in the city's throne room for the Bounty hunt.
  3. Go back out. The target, a boss of a random monster family with its escort, appears the next time you enter the region, marked on the map.
  4. Kill it yourself and return to the Jarl for the reward: (the target family's level + all your banked levels) × 100 gold, as on the other continents.

Monsters and maps

  • More monsters by difficulty. MM9's packs are part of its maps, so MAW adds fixed extra monsters instead of larger spawn groups: × 1.2 at Hard and Hell, × 1.4 Nightmare, × 1.6 Doom, × 1.8 Road to Insanity, × 2 Beyond Madness. Some maps multiply that again: Thjorad Mine × 3, Drangheim × 2.8, Guberland × 2.5, Thjorgard × 2, Frosgard × 1.8, Thronheim and Lindisfarne × 1.6, Ta'Sar Academy × 1.5. The extras are the same every time, never pile up (at most six others close by), and never stand near where you arrive or teleport.
  • Map completion never counts sea monsters and Jellyspores.
  • Open-air maps count as outdoors (the regions, built as indoor maps): outdoor save cost, no Nightmare revival, Hell's ranged melee monsters.
  • Frosgard City has its region's band until the Battle of Frosgard, then a late band: the Horde is about 45 / 55 / 65 for a party that banked nothing elsewhere.
  • Drangheim Prison's alarm calls guards of every rank from posts away from the party.
  • The Arena follows MAW's arena rules: no level bonus from banks, no rewards in Beyond Madness.
  • Scripted monsters (the Dragon King, Njam, Yanmir and the like) never become bosses.

Gear, shops, hirelings and quests

  • Gear: MM9 equipment rolls MAW rarity and power; the strongest piece of each kind and quality is 10–15 % stronger than its MM6–MM8 match. No black chest guarantees an artifact.
  • Shops sell their own items at MAW power. Apothecaries sell crafting gems; spellbook shops do not. Spellbooks are sealed and priced as MAW's are.
  • Teachers are MM9's own lessons with MAW's requirements and prices (and MM9's rank requirement): see Teachers, filter MM9.
  • Hirelings scale with the party's level. From Doom up, paid hirelings refuse to join; Forad Darre and Tamur Leng always join.
  • Quests pay MAW's quest gold bonus for levels banked elsewhere; MM9's quest experience stays capped per chapter.
  • Used barrels stay empty through refills. Drowning keeps MM9's damage (a sixth of maximum HP a tick).

Map levels

MAW's bands for Chedian (its first balance pass for MM9; they may still move). Under Free, levels banked on the other continents are added; Where to go works them out for your party.
