Stats

From 25 up, every 5 points of a stat give +1 of its usual effect, with no cap. Each stat also has a percentage effect.

Armour and resistances

Armor Class and resistances cut damage against the attacker's level:

  • Damage taken = hit ÷ (AC ÷ (200 + L) + 1): armour of 200 + the monster's level halves a physical hit.
  • Damage taken = hit ÷ (resistance ÷ (100 + L) + 1): a resistance of 100 + the monster's level halves its element.
  • A hit is never cut below 10 % of its size. Grandmaster Shield takes a further 15 % off every hit.
  • Light uses the lower of your Mind and Body resistance, Dark your lowest elemental one, Energy your lowest of all.

Example: AC 600 against a level-100 monster: 600 ÷ 300 + 1 = 3, so a third of the hit gets through (67 % cut).

  • Your hits compare your Attack with a typical character of the monster's level: 75 % at par, between 25 % and 100 %. Spells always hit.
  • Their hits do not roll against your AC; Speed decides whether a blow misses.
  • Monster resistances: each 100 halves damage, capped at 200. Monsters never resist Energy.

Criticals and speed

Crit chance = 5 % + Luck ÷ (500 + 5 × monster level); crit damage × (1.5 + Accuracy ÷ (250 + 2.5 × level)). Heals never crit.

Base recovery by weapon (lower is faster): Blaster 40, Dagger 60, Sword 80, Bow, Mace and Club 100, Staff and Spear 120, Axe 140. Higher-level weapons are slower but hit proportionally harder; two weapons average their recoveries. Armour you lack the skill for slows attacks and casting.

Auras

Buff spells do not run out: they are auras. Casting one costs no mana; it reserves part of the caster's maximum mana while held, and the reserved part cannot be spent or regenerated.

  • Cast it again with the same character to release it (free, instant); cast it with another to move it. R in the spellbook releases every aura.
  • Auras do not expire: resting, travel and monster Dispel do not end them. A knocked-out holder's aura pauses but keeps its reservation.
  • One holder per aura for the party, and auras never stack: the strongest source wins. A potion of the same buff gives its drinker whichever is stronger.
  • Reserved share = the aura's weight ÷ (class SP per level + Enlightenment mastery ÷ 2), as a percent of maximum SP. Torch Light, Wizard Eye and Water Walk reserve a flat 5, 5 and 20 SP instead.
  • Death Knights and the Thief line hold auras for free. Temple, scroll and pedestal blessings are weak fixed auras that reserve nothing.
  • Fly, Invisibility, Feather Fall, Detect Life and racial buffs stay timed spells.

Crowd control

Control spells always land, but a monster that has been controlled lately gets less of it. The game looks at how much of the last 20 seconds the monster spent under the same kind of control:

Time under control in the last 20 sNext effect lasts
under 15 %full
15–30 %75 %
30–45 %50 %
45–60 %25 %
60 % or moreimmune
  • Two kinds share a history: fear and control (Fear, Mass Fear, Charm, Berserk, Enslave, Turn and Control Undead), and paralysis (Paralyze and weapon stuns). Slow, Shrinking Ray and Dark Grasp never wear thin.
  • A cast's base length grows with mastery (½, 0.65, 0.8, full from Novice to Grandmaster) and Ascension, and shrinks with the target's resistance (÷ (1 + resistance ÷ 100)). Bosses take half. A party of one gets twice as long.
  • So a Fear lasts its full time once, about half the second time, and the target is soon immune: control one monster briefly, then the next.

Leech

Leech heals by how much of a monster you took, not by the damage number: a hit that deals a normal monster of that family its whole HP is worth your whole HP, times your leech share. Overkill does not count.
