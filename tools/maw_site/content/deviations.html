About this list

OpenYAMM reimplements MAW; it does not run MAW's scripts. It follows MAW commit 8fbb48d1 (4 November 2026). The project's full record has about 130 entries; below are the ones a player notices. Most fix bugs in the original, the rest replace interface MAW built for its engine.

Rules and balance

Choosing rulesOne ruleset switch and a Rules panel at New Game; Nightmare is a row that says it cannot be lowered, no typed "yes"
Map bandsTwo maps that shared a display name (Jadame's Temple of the Sun, zdtl02) keep the bands MAW wrote for them

CoverOne rule for every single-target monster attack; only conscious members cover; Master covers every spell
RetaliationSpent on the next melee hit
PromotionsThe final-class draw is fixed per character; any member's Jadame award counts; Elder and Ultimate multiply the per-level gains without renaming tables
Assassin energyOnly blows that land pay energy

Town Portal, Lloyd's BeaconRefused near enemies on Nightmare and in every Doom mode, Grandmaster too

Antagarich experienceThe double experience applies on Antagarich's maps, wherever the party trained

Monster missilesHome at 300° a second at any frame rate, so strafing on foot does not dodge them
Celestial itemsScale with the wearer up to level 600 (1,000 in Beyond Madness)

Items and crafting

AreaIn OpenYAMM
LootA corpse's drop is fixed by the save, the map visit and the monster: reloading does not reroll it
Chest artifactsPlaced artifacts stay (MAW re-rolled them on every load, quest rewards included)
Loot filterFive presets with a level rule; filtered items pay their gold at once with a combat log line; priceless items are never filtered
Shop rerollA "New stock" button with its price in the weapon, armour and magic shops of all three continents (MAW: a hidden R key)
WorkbenchEvery craft on one bench with an exact preview; reloading does not change results; risky crafts ask first; every refusal says why
Pandora's CubeRefuses rings and amulets (MAW took the cube for nothing)
Archibald's MirrorBase items only; the copy stays identified
Gem mergingOn the Workbench, any tier, every bag, many at once
Crafting in shopsEvery magic shop and every alchemist sells materials
CatalystCannot be drunk (a drink with no effect)
Dimension mapsDropped maps have 2–4 active affixes like the quest's; maps open their target in every mode

Interface

AreaIn OpenYAMM
KeysMAW's keys are rebindable in the Keyboard settings: Gather Loot on G (MAW's K is Fly Down here), Alchemist on A; Unstuck, Open Workbench and Knight Charge unbound
BagsFour bags a character, shown together; a party of one has five tabs of four (MAW: five, and 25 solo)
Map progressA bar in the party frame with the share and count, gold when complete
Stats pageMelee, Ranged and Spell Power and Vitality in four rows; MAW's coloured stat names
AurasAn aura panel with holders, reservations and states
TeleportersOne pedestal under a glowing orb in New Sorpigal, Ravenshore and Harmondale
TrainersA journal trainer finder and a Ledger of Teachers in every tavern; MAW's trainer hints name only teachers you know

Not ported

  • MAW's Randomizer (shuffled artifacts and monsters).
  • The Saving Goobers quest (OpenYAMM has no base quest for it yet).
  • The damage recount window and MAW's own settings page: their pieces live where they belong (Workbench, Rules panel, combat log).
