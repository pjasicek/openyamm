Xeen in a MAW game

  • The fifth world: the World of Xeen (Clouds of Xeen and Darkside of Xeen, 82 maps) plays under MAW with its own level bank, its own ladder (bands up to about 110, Beyond Madness to 500) and a fifth world for Elder, Ultimate and Supreme.
  • One bank for both faces. Cloudside and Darkside are one route, so their levels go into one bank: the journal's Progress tab and the Level popup show it as "Xeen". A Darkside map does not grow with what you earned on Cloudside.
  • Start: a Xeen game under MAW starts in Vertigo, as in Standard. Xeen's new-game card adds MAW's four new class lines (Seraphim, Death Knight, Shaman, Elementalist); Xeen's races stay its own.
  • Quests, monsters, shops and every map's points of interest are in the Xeen guide ↗; this section is what MAW changes.

Levels and bands

Each Xeen map's band comes from its act: the party level the map was built for, put on MAW's MM7 stages (the curve Chedian's acts use), is the level the map's own monster families meet you at. With one family that is the band's low end; with two or three, the middle. Low and high keep the spread of the map's own monsters. (Balance version 2, 2026-11-21: maps with a single family, such as Castle Xeen, Castle Blackfang, Castle Alamar, the Alamar Depths and Dragon Cave, read about a fifth too low in version 1.)

  • Horizontal starters: Vertigo Meadows (X1), Joe's Warehouse (XD-01), Red Dwarf Mines (XD-02) and Orc Caves (XD-05): their monsters grow with you, as on the other worlds' starters.
  • Beyond Madness: one ladder over both faces, in route order, from 4 (Vertigo and Joe's Warehouse, the start maps) to 500 (the Dragon Tower and the Dungeon of Death).
  • Mount Firestone (X7) is an Act V map under MAW. Its band follows the north's Hydras, Dragons and Elemental Golems; the orcs on the south slope by Vertigo, which Xeen counts in Act I, are pulled halfway up to it, to about level 20. Do not wander north from Vertigo early.
  • The Warzone (XD-20) follows MAW's arena rules: fighters by the party's level, no level bonus from banks, no rewards in Beyond Madness.
  • Map completion never counts the octopods (Lakeside's lake, the Plane of Water's sea) or the dragons of Lake Darzog: monsters that guard water are left out, as on Chedian.
  • Scripted monsters (Lord Xeen, the Dragon King, Sheltem and the like) never become MAW bosses.
Xeen's monsters are stronger per level than MM6–MM8's at the start: Vertigo's orcs sit at 3–5. As on Chedian, a band is a level, not a promise of an easy fight.

Monsters and density

  • Outdoor maps and 15 of Cloudside's dungeons (Joe's Warehouse, the mines, the Witch Tower, the Orc Caves, Castle Burlock, the Tomb of a Thousand Terrors and the others built on spawn points) grow by MAW's group sizes, as on MM6–MM8 (see Difficulty).
  • The other dungeons place every monster by hand; under MAW they gain copies of their own monsters beside them, as Chedian's do:

  • Open-air maps (Cloudreach, the Clouds of Xeen, Shangri-La, the Skyroads, the Elemental Planes) count as outdoors for MAW.

For MAW, each line's final step is the closest Xeen second-tier quest of its theme, Light or Dark (either counts); the racial lines take Xeen's racial quests. Gear and loot

  • The four gem pieces roll MAW rarity and power on bases by their Xeen tier: the Ruby Broadsword one step below the top MM6–MM8 swords, the Emerald Leather and Sapphire Staff between that step and the top, the Diamond Plate at the top; each 10 % ahead of an item of its step (the Diamond Plate 15 %).
  • Xeen's artifacts keep their table values and fixed effects.
  • Loot is Xeen's own pool plus the MM6–MM8 items, rolled with MAW's rules. Xeen's shops are MM6–MM8 house types, so MAW's shop rules apply as on MM6–MM8.
  • Teachers are Xeen's own lessons with MAW's requirements and prices: see Teachers, filter XEEN.

MAW's people and the teleporter

  • Seers: Zephyra, the Court Magician of Castle Burlock (Burlock Savannah, The Weathervane), and Madame Oorla in Castleview offer MAW's skill reset and Dimensional Maps, as the Oracle, Judge Grey and the Seer do on the other worlds. Both towns are Town Portal stops. Dimension maps open MM6–MM8 dungeons only.
  • Bounty hunts: Xeen's town halls (Vertigo, Castleview and the others) offer MAW's Bounty Hunt: once the town's map is cleared, a named boss with its kin and a "Bounty" note on the map; the mayor pays when it is dead.
  • Dimensional travel: a pedestal under a glowing orb on Vertigo Square, south of where a new game starts, opens the continents' portals in every game (Xeen's own, not MAW's), as New Sorpigal's, Ravenshore's, Harmondale's and Sturmford's do; their Xeen portal lands in Vertigo. With Horizontal progression this is how you move between Xeen and the other worlds.
