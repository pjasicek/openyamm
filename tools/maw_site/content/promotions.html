How promotion works

Every class belongs to a line (Knight → Cavalier → Champion or Black Knight is the Knight line). Lines have two or three tiers; the last is the final tier.

  • Any continent works. Every continent has promotion quests for every line: each line uses the quests of one classic line there. The new lines borrow the Cleric's (Seraphim), Knight's (Death Knight), Druid's (Shaman) and Sorcerer's (Elementalist) quests; the table below lists each line's quests.
  • The final quest promotes the whole line. Finish your line's final promotion quest on any continent and every member of that line goes straight to the final tier, from any earlier tier. That includes characters who join later.
  • Anyone can finish it. Members of other classes get the "Honorary" version, and it still counts for your line. On Jadame, an award given to any member counts.
  • New lines' middle tier. For Seraphim, Death Knight, Shaman and Elementalist, the first-tier quest of the borrowed line on Enroth or Antagarich promotes tier 1 to tier 2. On Chedian, MM9's first-tier quests do it: Healer (Seraphim, Shaman), Mercenary (Death Knight), Scholar (Elementalist).
  • Chedian (MM9) counts as a fourth continent. Each line's final step there is the closest MM9 second-tier quest: Gladiator (Knight, Monk, Minotaur, Troll, Dragon, Death Knight), Assassin (Thief line, Vampire), Paladin, Ranger (Ranger, Archer), Priest (Cleric, Seraphim), Druid (Druid, Dark Elf, Shaman), Mage or Lich (Sorcerer, Necromancer; Elementalist Mage only). MM9 offers every promotion quest at any time, in any order.
  • Two final classes? A character the quest itself promotes gets that quest's class. A character promoted by the line rule gets one of the line's final classes by a draw. The draw is fixed per character, so reloading does not change it.
MAW drew the final class again every time it checked, so its players saved and reloaded for the class they wanted. OpenYAMM fixes the draw per character and counts any member's award.

Stacking: Elder and Ultimate

Completing your line's final promotion on more than one continent raises its final classes' HP and SP per level. The line's class name gets the prefix.

Final promotions done onTitleHP and SP per level
1 continent—× 1
2 continentsElder× 1.25, rounded down
3 or 4 continentsUltimate× 1.5, rounded down (a fourth adds nothing)
  • Only the per-level gains change. Base HP and SP, stats and skills do not.
  • It works on the whole line at once and applies immediately, also to characters already at level 100.
  • It counts continents, not quests: two lines that share a continent's quest both count it.
  • All four continents count, Chedian included. MM9's first-tier quests (Mercenary, Crusader, Scholar, Healer) promote only whoever they promote, except for the four new lines above.

Worked examples

HP = base HP + HP per level × (level + Endurance effect + Bodybuilding bonus). SP = base SP + SP per level × (level + 2 × Personality effect). A stat's effect from 25 up is the stat ÷ 5.

Rounding down hurts small numbers: a class with 3 or fewer per level gains nothing from Elder in that pool. Classes with 4 or 8 per level gain the most from Elder.

What a promotion gives

  • Higher HP and SP per level (the class table lists each tier).
  • Higher mastery caps: most lines open Grandmaster in their main skills only on the final tier.
  • For some lines, class perks tied to the final tier (Supreme Armsmaster for Black Knights and Champions, the Elementalist's 8 SP per level as Master of Elements).
