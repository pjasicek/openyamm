What MAW is

MAW is a rebalance of the merged Might and Magic 6–8 world by Malekith, Rawsugar and the MAW team. Monsters scale with a model of a typical party at each map's level, buffs become auras that hold mana instead of running out, and every drop is rolled with a rarity, an item level and enchantments. It adds four class lines (Seraphim, Death Knight, Shaman, Elementalist), reworks the Thief line into the Assassin and adds crafting.

OpenYAMM plays MAW as an optional ruleset, based on MAW commit 8fbb48d1 (4 November 2026). The Deviations page lists where it knowingly plays differently.

Turning it on

  1. New Game → Choose Starting Continent: switch the ruleset from Standard MMMerge to MAW.
  2. Open Rules… and pick difficulty, progression and (optionally) a challenge mode.
  3. Each save remembers its ruleset. A MAW save does not load without the MAW package.

MAW plays on four continents: Enroth (MM6), Antagarich (MM7), Jadame (MM8) and Chedian (MM9). A Chedian game starts at Ravensford (Bootcamp); Sturmford City's dimensional pedestal links it to the others.

Nightmare adds: saving costs food; Town Portal and Lloyd's Beacon are refused near enemies; a dungeon left below 65 % cleared raises its dead; a member knocked below minus their full HP dies outright, below twice that is eradicated. Difficulty can be changed mid-game in Settings → Rules, except out of Nightmare.

Normal uses MAW's in-between numbers; in the original mod Easy and Normal play the same by mistake. Nightmare needs no typed "yes".

Take the challenge modes in order: Road to Insanity only once Doom feels comfortable, Beyond Madness after that. Austerity is balanced around MAW difficulty; on Nightmare or higher it gets harsh fast. From Nightmare up you cannot kite as before: pull a few monsters at a time.

Playing solo

A party of one is not a mode: these rules switch on while one character is in the party.

  • Quest and map-completion experience × 5 ÷ party size: a lone hero gets five times as much.
  • Permanent Wizard Eye and Torch Light, no mana.
  • Jump, Fly, Water Walk, Town Portal and Lloyd's Beacon scrolls are not used up. A Fly scroll lasts seven hours.
  • Identify Item, Merchant, Repair, Perception, Disarm Traps, Identify Monster and Alchemy reach Expert, Master and Grandmaster on their own at 4, 7 and 10, whatever the class allows.
  • Crowd control lasts twice as long; a successful Cover halves the hit; poison and disease hurt half as much.
  • Five tabs of four bags instead of four bags.

Preferences

In Settings → Rules, changeable any time: homing projectiles (on; yours and theirs), friendly fire (off), the loot filter (see Loot), trainer hints in skill tooltips (off), and in Road to Insanity and Beyond Madness, disintegration.
