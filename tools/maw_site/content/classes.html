Rules every class shares

  • Any race, any class. Any portrait and race may take any starting class, the four new lines and the Peasant included. Race adds its own perks (resistances, skill bonuses, a racial ability).
  • HP = base HP + HP per level × (level + Endurance effect + Bodybuilding skill × mastery). A stat's effect from 25 up is the stat ÷ 5, with no cap.
  • SP = base SP + SP per level × (level + 2 × Personality effect). Every caster's pool comes from Personality, counted twice. Intellect gives no SP: it raises spell damage (only the higher of Intellect and Personality counts) and spell critical damage. Meditation adds no SP of its own.
  • Mastery needs (weapons, armour, Shield, magic, racial abilities, Bodybuilding, Meditation, Regeneration, Dodging, Unarmed, Armsmaster, Ascension): skill 4 / 7 / 10 in Free, 6 / 12 / 20 in Horizontal and Doom, 8 / 16 / 25 in Road to Insanity, 10 / 24 / 40 in Beyond Madness. The other skills keep 4 / 7 / 10. The class must allow the mastery, and Master Merchant, Bodybuilding and Ascension still need 50 Personality, Endurance and Intellect.
  • Teachers cost 1,000 / 4,000 / 20,000 gold for Expert / Master / Grandmaster (Doom 2,000 / 10,000 / 50,000; Road to Insanity 10,000 / 50,000 / 250,000; Beyond Madness 15,000 / 300,000 / 1,250,000), times 1 + the number of your other such skills already at that mastery or higher. A third Grandmaster costs 60,000.
  • The second skill costs less. Raising a weapon skill, magic school or racial ability to level v costs ⌈v ÷ k⌉ points, k = 1 + your other skills of that group already at v or above. A second school next to one at 10 costs 29 points instead of 54. Hybrid lines (Seraphim, Shaman, Death Knight, Assassin) share only the magic schools.
  • No teacher for Cover, Mana Shield, Enlightenment and Retaliation masteries: they come by themselves at these skill levels: Cover 6 / 12 / 20 (8 / 20 / 30 in Road to Insanity and Beyond Madness), Mana Shield 6 / 12 / 20 (8 / 20 / 32), Enlightenment 6 / 12 / 20, Retaliation 10 / 24 / 40. Cover is learned at training halls, Mana Shield and Enlightenment at magic shops, Retaliation automatically when Cover reaches 30.
  • Respec: the Oracle (Ravenshore), Judge Grey (Harmondale), the Seer (Castle Ironfist area) and Hjordisa the Hag (Guberland) reset a character's skills for 1,000 gold × level. Alchemy is kept; former masteries return free once the skill meets the requirement again.
