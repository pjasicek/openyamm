# Online walkthrough notes (paraphrased)

Extracted 2026-11-07 from Lu Richardson's walkthrough, https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/might-and-magic-ix (the
the-spoiler.com copies redirect elsewhere). Its full text covers the prologue to the Connecting Tunnel; Chapter III on
came from a summary only (low confidence). Original MM9; the rework can differ, so check against the map scripts.

## Prologue
- Ravensford: the training centre has a climb, jump and swim course ending in a scroll. Kill the nearby enemies, take
  the money bags, then use the boat to reach the Isle of Ashes.
- Isle of Ashes: Forad is north-west across the bridge and points you to Yrsa the Troll; her cottage is the only
  building in sight besides the castle. Yrsa wants the passage opposite her cottage cleared of beasts, then sends you to
  Forad. Forad mentions a door; Yrsa gives the means to open it. Follow the bridge south, fight to the door, go in, turn
  right for a chest, click the book: a scene takes you to the docks and the boat to Sturmford.

## Chapter I
- Sturmford: the Jarl is in the Jarl's Palace, room to the right of the entrance. Cleanse the altar in the entrance room
  for Town Portal access.
- Anskram Keep (imps): shoot the basilisks from range outside. Destroy the imp portals in the two lower-building rooms by
  standing before the flaming stairs and shooting until the flames die. In the upper building, destroy the portals in
  the first two west barracks rooms, pulling the lever on the wall opposite each door; repeat on the east side; then the
  central wooden partition's lever.
- Ivsar (Drangheim Prison): boat to Drangheim, south to the village, use the well rope, climb the ladders in the cave, up
  to the guard room; pull all the levers by its door. Ivsar is in the last cell on the right. Lead him back to
  Sturmford's tavern, then to the Jarl.
- Drangheim: Jarl Sigmund in the Town Hall, through the door behind the Town Clerk, up a ladder.
- Anskram defenses (second visit): walk around the upper building to a trap door in the floor, go down, shoot the two
  overhead chains, pull the lever under the chain on the wall, climb out.
- Chasm of the Dead: the path north-east of the city. Hug the left wall; undead resurrect. South through the building to
  the last room, mummies and a trap before the last door; the crown (Crona Kiga) on a pedestal between two chests.
- Thjorgard: the Jarl in the castle north of the city.
- Lindisfarne Monastery (Thjorad): climb the chapel ladders to the bell ropes, pull them in the order 1, 5, 2, 4, 3
  (left to right); the monks move away; take the artifact from under the stairs.
- Thjorad Mine: Gudrek sends you in. Follow the crack, fall into the water, north exit, break the boarded doorway. The
  central round chamber's door opens after removing the spikes beside it. A lever opens the machine room: take the broken
  slag extractor. Ketil sends you to a cave: shoot the stalagmite to block the hole. The Fixer lives in the south-west
  house near the church: pay him 2000 gold. A replacement slag extractor from the armour shop south of the Training Hall.
  Install it in the machine room, follow the Fixer.
- Guberland: the Jarl in the Town Hall (he may first ask you to remove the Dook: side quest, talk to Dook at his castle,
  mention the sword).
- Ta'Sar Academy (Lindisfarne, south-west of the village): put the four books on their tables: weapons on the 1st-floor
  Offence table, strategy on the 2nd-floor Strategy table, fortifications on the 1st-floor Defence table, information on
  the 2nd-floor Intelligence table. A bookcase opens a secret room; its coded scroll points to the Book of Rules behind a
  rotating bookshelf in the upstairs lab: stand at the right side of the bookcase and press the book at eye level.
- Ivan the Smart (the Gathering, south-west of Guberland on the beach): answers 3, 1, 1, 2, 3, 3, then Ivan's name.
- Frosgard: Yanmir's fort: leave Frosgard, the ice cave on the left to the teleporter. Inside: left and up to the
  north-west room for the Sage's Diary; east past the throne, south to the trapezoid room with thralls; the bookcase on
  the west wall lets you through; shoot the beams in the beam room and wait at the entrance until the giant falls. Avoid
  the roaming giant.
- Dock ice: Elvissen on the west edge of the city; shoot a test barrel, then he delivers one to the docks; shoot it.
- Thronheim: Kira in the Jarl's room. The dockmaster sends the Honkies away: find the three Honkies in the city and get
  them to the docks (don't kill them). Mountain Pass: east of the city; follow the path to the end, killing everything.

## Chapter II
- War: Kira's envoy reports the Sturmford-Drangheim war. Both Jarls sign only a treaty someone else writes: Kira writes
  it; take it to both; they sign at the meeting.
- The Thing (Thjorgard): west of the city by a large lake. Buy tickets, play the games in order (save before each). The
  last gives the Dragon Prize: take it to Bikki outside Thronheim's south gate.
- Then the Connecting Tunnel to Yorwick (cutscene on arrival).

## Chapter III-V (summary only, low confidence)
- Igrid sends you to the Isle of Ashes with the Capstone; the Gathering's manager explains how to get the Capstone.
- Arslegard: aim the crystal beam through mirrors A, B, C, D back to the crystal, one click at a time.
- Afterworld basin dialogue order: 3, 2, 4, 3, 1, 5.
- Tomb of a Thousand Terrors chess keys: F1, G2, G3, G4, G5, H6, G7, G8, E7, D8; the second board's pieces fight.
