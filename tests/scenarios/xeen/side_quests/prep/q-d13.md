## Fight route

"Fight to the death" (hostile), Hobstadt (actor 12) killed: event 14's kill timer runs `q-d13.hobstadt`
(hobstadt_slain 113264, status "Hobstadt falls across the foot of his throne. The throne is vacant."). The throne
(event 16) then runs `q-d13.throne` (no `once` since D-74): with a Troll member, throne_taken 113265, promote, "Your
troll sits on the throne of Hobstadt. It fits. He stands up a War Troll."; else "The throne is vacant.". The hoard opens
to his killers.
