# Unity Labs VFX flipbooks (CC0)

`flame02_16x4.png`, `flame03_16x4.png` and `smallflame01_16x4.png` are the flipbook sheets (16 x 4 frames,
losslessly converted from the TGA files) of "Free VFX image sequences and flipbooks" by Unity Labs Paris
(Thomas Iché, 2016), released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks

Downloaded 2026-10-09 from
`https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/Flame02/Flame02-flipbooks.zip`,
`.../Flame03/Flame03-flipbooks.zip` and `.../SmallFlame01-temperature/SmallFlame01-flipbooks.zip`.
SmallFlame01 is kept for reference only: several of its frames have hard vertical seams and flames cut at the
frame edge.
