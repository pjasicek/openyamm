Global offset · all generated portraits

All portraits use a 118×158 canvas. Offsets are in image pixels: positive X moves right; positive Y moves down. They persist across families and in the page URL. Gold guides stay fixed at the middle and bottom of the portrait window.

Placement measurements and method

The original is enlarged 2× without smoothing. The new portrait keeps imagegen’s alpha and is downsampled from its master. Vertical fitting matches the original crown and bottom independently of width. Bottom contact is measured across a short lower strip, so one unusual pixel cannot shift the face sideways. Native silhouette estimates measure placement only.

The gold vertical line is x=59 in both portrait canvases. The original and selection ring keep their placement when offsets change. Pixels shifted outside the fixed canvas are clipped. The downloadable PNG includes the current offset.
