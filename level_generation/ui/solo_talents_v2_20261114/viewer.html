Solo Talents v2 Mockups

Two ways to show the proposed talent tree v2 (devdocs/maw/solo_sim/proposals/TALENT_TREE_V2.md) on the solo run's Talents page. Both show Vardis, a level-500 Death Knight on Beyond Madness. Points come only from levels (one per 15 levels, so 33 at level 500, all spent); the three trees are Arms, Archery and Arcana, which a character combines (Archery is bows only, never a Dragon's breath); keystones fill five slots, one per 150 levels (150 to 750), and Warlord, Deadeye and Archon each need 10 points in their tree; Unbroken is for anyone. The other classes each have an Oath keystone of their own; the Death Knight has none since Lichborne was removed. His three open slots hold Unbroken, Warlord and Archon. The mockups are live: hover a row to see it in the detail card, click to keep it there, click keystone sockets.
