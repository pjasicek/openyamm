TYPE

One display face

Fondamento for short button labels, in title case. Optical size is matched to the original tabs. Paragraphs and compact numbers retain their readable text faces.

SURFACE

One reusable frame

Keep a blank, textured button surround and draw the label above it. Longer actions can grow or wrap; each new word does not need a new painted image.

BEHAVIOUR

One state recipe

Warm hover, immediate pressed depth, persistent selected rim, muted disabled ink and a clear keyboard focus edge. No idle pulsing; the button responds to input.

Source evidence and implementation notes

The original samples are the currently mounted restored But20U/H/D textures. Character layout assigns separate U/H/D art to Stats, Skills, Inventory, Awards and Exit; the renderer also holds the active page in D. This study separates selection from a held press.

The proposed faces are comparisons, not an identification of the historical font. Fondamento and Almendra are locally bundled from Google Fonts with their SIL Open Font Licenses; ENDGAME is the existing project font. The viewer makes no external font requests.
