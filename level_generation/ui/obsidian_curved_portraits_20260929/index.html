Each signal has a place

  1. ResourcesHP follows green → amber → red. Mana remains blue. Empty portions stay dark.
  2. SelectionAn ivory-gold inner rim and the top diamond. Both disappear when nobody can act.
  3. Aggro + recoveryThe unmarked lower-left diamond keeps its existing colours; recovery stays around that diamond.
  4. Individual buffsThe lower-right medallion opens the character's buff list. It disappears when there are no buffs.

My preference

A keeps health and mana easy to separate at gameplay size. B is denser, especially beside the selection rim. Both keep the portrait at its current size.
