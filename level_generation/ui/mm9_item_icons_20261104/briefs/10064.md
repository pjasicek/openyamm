# Item 10064 Regimental Halberd (polearm). Folder: items/10064. Size 1024x1536.
A very long halberd standing vertical, head at the top. The shaft is a long slender pale silver-steel pole with banded
segments, ending at the bottom in a gold ferrule with a thin blue band. The head: a straight spear point on top, and
two large swept curved axe blades, one on each side, flaring outward and upward like wings with jagged points (as in the
source), polished steel with bright highlights. The object is very tall and narrow: make it fill the full height.
PROPORTIONS (strict): like the MM7/MM8 reference polearms the whole weapon is about five times taller than its widest
point; the head (spear point plus wing blades) takes only the top fifth of the height, and the bare shaft below the
head is at least three times as long as the head. Keep the head small and the shaft long; do not shorten the shaft
to make the head bigger.
