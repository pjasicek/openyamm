# Item 10123 Dragon Helm (helm). Folder: items/10123. Size 1024x1024.
A closed great helm with a tall rounded bowl of dark blue-black steel with a fine dark stippled/hammered texture.
Polished gold-brass trim forms a bold T-shaped face opening: a horizontal brow bar with small upward-pointed tips and a
vertical nose bar running down the centre, with black empty eye slots on both sides of the nose bar, and gold edging
along the lower rim and the sides of the face plate. View: straight front, perfectly symmetric, the T-shaped face opening
on the centre line, like the MM7/MM8 reference helms. Empty helm, no head, no plume, no horns.
