# Item 10113 Thjoradric Plate (armor). Folder: items/10113. Size 1024x1024.
A heavy plate harness of warm reddish copper/bronze: a smooth rounded breastplate with a gentle centre ridge, broad layered rounded pauldrons,
articulated plate upper arms ending above the elbow, a banded waist, and a skirt of vertical overlapping tasset plates
reaching to mid-thigh. Warm copper with soft orange highlights and darker aged recesses, small round rivets at the
plate joins. No gems, no cloth tabard, no spikes.
