# Item 10210 Soulband (ring). Folder: items/10210. Size 1024x1024.
A unique silver/steel ring: a broad band made of two raised parallel grooved ridges, widening to an oval bezel that
holds a cluster of vivid blue sapphire facets arranged like a small flower, framed in pale silver. Along the outer edge
next to the bezel runs a row of small bronze-gold spiky prongs/teeth as in the source. Cool silver metal with a slight
dark patina in the grooves. View: straight from the front with only a slight downward tilt, the bezel at top centre facing the viewer,
the band symmetric, exactly like the reference rings.
