# Item 10125 Jarl's Helm (helm). Folder: items/10125. Size 1024x1024.
A Norse spectacle helm: a rounded, slightly pointed dome of bright polished silver steel divided into segments by raised
ridges meeting at the top (spangenhelm), on a darker riveted brow band. Below the brow hangs a spectacle guard of darker
steel with two round eye openings and a short nasal between them, covering the upper face. Cool silver with soft dark
reflections. View: straight front, perfectly symmetric, the nasal on the centre line, like the reference helms. Empty helm, no head, no horns, no mail
curtain.
