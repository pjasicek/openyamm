MAW Class Spellbooks

Mockup of the Death Knight's and Assassin's own spellbook pages: their schools' medallions, tabs and spell pictures, drawn in the original book's style. Point at a spell to light it up as the game does; click a tab to turn the page. Rules, spells and skills stay exactly as they are.

Book

What the mockup shows

  • Seven new school pages: Frost, Blood and Unholy for the Death Knight line; Combat, Subtlety, Poisons and Assassination for the Thief line. Each has its own medallion scene and name on the banner.
  • Seven new school tabs, line-drawn when idle and painted when open. A class sees only its own tabs, stacked from the top.
  • Twenty new spell pictures: all twelve Death Knight spells and the Assassin's eight attack spells. The Assassin's thirteen ordinary spells (Fly, Town Portal, Haste...) keep their original pictures.
  • Spells sit in mastery order: the page reads left to right, top to bottom, from unskilled or Novice to Grandmaster.

Still to decide

  • The Assassin attack spells' names. MAW kept the mage names (Fireball, Stun...). The mockup shows proposed names; the panel lists MAW's.
  • Whether the Death Knight's three borrowed spells (Water Walk, Regeneration, Vampiric Aura) keep their names. The mockup keeps them and only redraws their pictures.
  • Pictures, medallions and tabs are first generations. Any of them can be repainted on request.
