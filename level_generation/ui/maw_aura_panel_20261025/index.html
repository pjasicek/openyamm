What the panel shows

Every held aura, its holder and what it reserves (percent of the holder's maximum and points); each member's pool with the same hatched locked cap as the portrait arcs; each aura's strength at its holder's skill and level; each member's actual benefit, including where a stronger source (Hour of Power, Day of the Gods, a potion) makes an aura add nothing; suspended auras of an unconscious holder (effect off, reservation kept); fixed temple, scroll and pedestal auras.
