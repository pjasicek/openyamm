One list per continent

It opens on the continent the party stands on; the tabs switch to the others. Every skill is one block with its Expert, Master and Grand Master teachers in mastery colours.

Search as you type

Any word narrows the list: a skill (“sword”), a mastery (“grand”), a trainer's name or a town. The E, M and G toggles keep one mastery. Previous and Next turn the pages, the wheel scrolls.

Click to see where

A click shows the trainer's outdoor map with a marker on the house door, or on the entrance of the dungeon or castle they teach in. Close returns to the list where it was.

The Ledger of Teachers

Without it the finder lists the trainers the party has found: the teacher notes from talking to them or to their neighbours. Each continent's taverns sell that continent's ledger for 2000 gold as an extra topic; buying it fills every trainer of the continent, and the topic disappears.

MAW's differences

MAW shows its moved trainers where its script places them, and skills it teaches itself read “learned at skill N” with no map. Data rows are generated from the installed tables.
