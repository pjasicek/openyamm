The party line

How strong a solo is against the simulated party of five at the same place in the game. 1 means as strong: both can face equally strong monsters.

MAW alone, no solo rules with the early monster cut with the cut and an ordinary build's talents

The rule the draft is held to: a solo is never stronger than a full party. Under the dashed line it is behind; above it, ahead.

MAW alone crosses the line near level 100 to 150, because a solo gains levels about twice as fast as the monsters grow. The cut (÷2.5 at the start, gone by level 75) lifts the start to the line. Talents then push every class above it, so the last column of the table says how much stronger a solo's monsters would have to be to keep the rule.

The budget, level by level

Points: one every 2 levels up to level 20, then one every 5. A keystone slot every 150 levels. Talent strength is the ordinary build of the class in the planner; its node values are estimates until the simulation has them. Levels past the end of the simulated run (about 500, or 800 on Beyond Madness) reuse the last measured stage.
