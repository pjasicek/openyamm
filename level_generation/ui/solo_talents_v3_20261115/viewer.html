Solo Talent Tree v3 Mockups

A bigger tree for the solo run, with its point budget and what every node does, held against one rule: a solo is never stronger than a full party of five. Three live mockups share one data file, so the planner, the constellation and the chart always agree. Nothing here is in the game, and every strength figure is an estimate until the solo simulation has run the nodes.

    How the tree is built

    • Second Wind is free at level 1. Regeneration that is strong out of combat and weak in it, because everyone would take it first anyway.
    • A path has five tiers. A tier opens with 5, 15, 30 or 45 points in the tiers below it, so deep nodes are earned inside the path.
    • Five kinds of node. Active skills (16 new ones, such as Ground Slam, Rain of Arrows, Stasis, Deflect and Lure), passives, notables, small nodes of 10 to 20 ranks, and skill unlocks that let any class train Bow, Dodging, Armsmaster, Meditation, Shield or a little healing magic.
    • Answers to what you named. Deflect makes missiles miss for a few seconds (homing shots). Lure pulls one monster out of its group. Recuperate and Second Skin replace potion spam.
    • Nobody fills it. A pure melee build finds about 170 ranks worth taking; level 1,000 pays 206 points, so the last points go into a second path.

    It ends at 75, not 100: with the early talents on top, a typical solo already stands at the party's strength there on MAW and Nightmare. On Doom and above the same cut leaves a solo a little under the party, where the quest route is the entry price.

    What needs your decision

    1. The rule against the tree. By the simulation, plain MAW already puts a typical solo at 1.3 to 1.7 times a party from level 150 on, because a solo levels twice as fast as the monsters grow. Any talent with real numbers pushes that higher (the chart shows 2 to 4 times by level 500). Three ways to keep the rule: make a solo's monsters stronger after level 75 by the chart's last column; shrink the nodes to trades with no net power; or accept MAW's lead and hold only the solo run's own additions to the line.
    2. Class spread. A Dragon stands at 4 to 10 times a party and a Priest at 0.2 to 0.5. One curve cannot hold both to the line; class-specific nodes or values would.
    3. Columns or constellation. Same tree, two pages. Columns extend the page that is built; the constellation is a new screen.
    4. The node values. One per cent per small rank is a first value. The simulation should measure the actives, the area hits and the sustain before any number is kept.
