Stable and ship schedule

Mockup of a Schedule topic for stables and boats. It opens a closable popup listing the week's departures, Monday to Sunday, with today highlighted. Clicking a day's row selects that departure and shows the wait, the trip and the cost, and can wait and travel in one step. Routes and weekdays are the real rows from transport_locations.txt; fares are examples.

Mockup controls (not part of the game UI)
Gold Food

What the game already has

  • Every stable and boat route carries Monday to Sunday flags, a travel time and an optional quest bit, loaded into HouseEntry::TransportRoute.
  • The dialogue already filters routes by today's weekday and quest bit, applies hireling day reductions and prices them (game/gameplay/HouseInteraction.cpp, transport options and the TransportRoute action).
  • Day 0 is Monday, the same weekday the save screen shows. Stables and boats are open 5 or 6 to 18, so waiting whole days keeps the house open.
  • Travel itself costs no food in the originals: the party rests and heals for the trip. Only the new waiting step needs a food rule.

How it would work

  1. A new TransportSchedule topic on every stable and boat, listed before today's routes. It stays even when the house says "come back another day".
  2. The popup is built from the same route data and checks as the dialogue: overrides from events, quest bits, hireling reductions, price.
  3. Wait and travel spends the waiting food, advances the clock by whole days (healing like rest), then runs the existing travel action unchanged.
  4. One shared overlay with its own layout YAML, used by desktop and Android, indoors and outdoors.
  5. A unit test for "next departure" and a headless scenario that waits and arrives on the right map and day.

Decisions for you

  • Food for waiting. The mockup charges the map's rest cost per day waited (1 on grass, 2 or more on desert or snow), the same as Rest & Heal. Alternatives: one food per day flat, or free.
  • Row order. Monday to Sunday with today highlighted, as you described. Starting the list at today puts the soonest departures first; easy to switch.
  • Quest routes. Locked routes stay hidden, as in the dialogue today. Toggle "Quest routes unlocked" to see them appear.
  • MM9. Its boats and coaches use a separate route system (Mm9TransportRoute); this proposal covers MM6 to MM8 first.
