Asset: flat surface albedo texture for a 1990s first-person RPG remaster, native 64x96 pixels.
Reference 1 is a soft enlargement of the original low-resolution texture. It is a guide for layout,
colours and materials only and contains no real fine detail. Reproduce EXACTLY its layout:
every block, plank, joint, ornament, figure and border at the same positions and proportions, the same colours,
palette and overall brightness. Do not add, remove, move or resize anything.
Redraw it as new clean high-definition artwork at 4x the original resolution: one complete lid of a weathered blue-grey plank chest: planks inside a rusty iron frame with rivets and iron bands at both ends;
continuous crisp edges and fine natural surface detail invented anew, everything in sharp focus, even diffuse
lighting.
Keep the full canvas, flat orthographic view, full bleed, opaque.

the brightness and material of the reference's edge, because the texture is tiled and its edges touch other faces.
Any fine checkered or crosshatch dither in the reference is a palette artefact: paint smooth continuous material.
