# Meadow plant sheets (Codex image generation brief)

The attached image is the game's current plant atlas (12 cells on a dark background). It is the STYLE reference:
hand-painted, semi-realistic game foliage, soft painterly shading, muted natural greens, readable silhouettes. Match
that style, lighting (soft top light, no cast shadows) and level of detail. Do not copy its plants.

Produce ONE new image with your built-in image generation tool: a sprite sheet with exactly TWO plant variants side
by side, described in the task below.

Hard rules for the sheet:
- Landscape image. The background is a perfectly uniform opaque RGB(255,0,255) magenta everywhere, including the
  holes between blades and leaves. No transparency, checkerboard, gradient, texture, ground, soil, pebbles, shadow,
  border, text or labels.
- Left variant in the left half, right variant in the right half, with a wide empty magenta gap between them in
  the middle of the image (at least 10% of the width). Neither plant touches the image edges.
- Both plants stand on the same invisible ground line near the bottom of the image; their roots end exactly on it.
  Seen from the side at eye level, like the reference.
- Plants must not pick up magenta reflections or pink rims. Clean, lightly antialiased contours.

Save the result as the PNG path given in the task (copy it there from where the image tool writes it). Generate once;
regenerate at most once if a hard rule is broken. Then stop.
