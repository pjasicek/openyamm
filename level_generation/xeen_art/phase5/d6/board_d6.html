D6 The Forbidden Zone: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Phase 5 review (A6, 2026-11-20), for a decider before any geometry. Darkside's north-west corner, Act V trap and endgame map (party 48-51), the world rim on its north and west sides, ash falling on 40 % of days. Castle Alamar (DD-17) stands on a basalt mesa in a horseshoe moat of lava north-west of the middle, every window lit, energy fields on its approach; after M33 a ruin with the sealed stair to FD-01. The moat spills over both rims and down the Burning Channel into Corak's lava lake, where Escape Pod 1 (DD-16) stands nose-up; Sheltem's pod (DD-22, sealed) lies in the Ancient field to the north-east. Necropolis (A4's 12 doors and the mirror, the pass-gate, DD-21's stair) sits walled under the east ridge by the border pass to D5. The long mountain road from Castleview tops Griffin Pass, Ambrose's camp. Castle Alamar (3.6x)

The mesa (to 2,300) in its horseshoe moat (1,550), open to the south: the approach ramp with four energy-field pylons, the double gate (m33.castle_door; DD-17 throws a party without the Soul Box back out here). After M33 (set:alamar): a smoking ruin, fields dark, the stair to FD-01 in the broken gatehouse (m39.depths_door; one POI spot with the gate). Robot patrols on the mesa and the approach.

MU-4 arrival view (from D1)

Camera on Griffin Pass (64, 96), 180 above the saddle, looking north over the Forbidden Zone: the road down to Ash Fork, Corak's lava lake and pod right of centre (about 15,400 away), Castle Alamar on its mesa ringed by lava left of centre (about 26,500), the Northern Lava Falls at the rim, the Ancient field's metal at the right. Ambrose's camp is beside the camera, out of frame. Against the plan: the painting brings the castle nearer and builds it of grey Gothic stone, and lines the energy pylons along the road far in front of it (the plan: four on the approach ramp); L3 keeps the plan's distances and the Ancient kit's dark teal steel. The rim's lava fall, the pod in its crust basin and the field's surfacing metal read as planned.

MU-4 signature view: Corak's pod and Castle Alamar

Camera on the lake's east shore by the Necropolis road (82, 72), about 60 degrees, looking west: the pod nose-up in the glowing lake left of centre (about 5,700), its hatch toward the shore; Castle Alamar's lit walls and energy fields beyond the moat right of centre (about 20,700); ash in the air. The view every party has on the way from Griffin Pass to Necropolis; both of the map's promises (the pod and the castle) in one look (WORLD_DESIGN 4). Against the plan: the castle stands about half its planned distance away and in grey stone (L3: 20,700 and teal steel; at L4 it must still read by its lit windows and fields, never moved nearer, as D-36); the islet's genie lamp sits beside the pod (plan: 6 cells north-west of it); the moat's lava ring is lost behind the far shore. The pod's scorched riveted hull, the round hatch facing the shore, the cyan fields on the ramp and the ash read as planned.
