D5 Desert of Doom and the Great Pyramid: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Phase 5 review (A6, 2026-11-20), for a decider before any geometry. Darkside's top middle, Act V wilderness (party 42-45), the world rim on its north side. A desert basin under the amber day: the Perishing Sands east and south with a salt pan, and rising alone from the dunes the Great Pyramid (DD-15, m28.door); the stony Desert of Doom west (the obelisk, the Last Water's +2 Endurance fountain, the Shrine of Great Power on a mesa). The Griffin Cliffs lift the giants' northern highlands: Gurodel's hold and the great doors of DD-24, Bosco's Hall (DD-25), the Northern Tower (DD-08) at the vertex of the Parabolic Barrier, a bowed line of Ancient frames with a shimmering curtain before the rim. The Quivering Forest of trembling aspens holds a wild cyclopes' lair. The seven Enchanted Boulders stand by the road; the broken travel pyramid's capstone hides the obelisk hoard; DD-18's tunnel ends at a grate in the south-east. Source: devdocs/xeen/briefs/d5/L1/ (project, plan, sketch in Map Studio's schema, NOTES.md; oymap validate clean).

MU-4 arrival view (from D4)

Camera at the D4 arrival (105, 80), 180 above the road, looking west out of the last firs of the Forest of Peril: the road down past the boulder field and its sign, the Great Pyramid far off over the dunes, the broken travel pyramid on the right-hand slope, the Griffin Cliffs and the Barrier's frames faint on the highlands. Against the plan: the road is painted as paving (the plan has a stony earth track, MU-2's soil_stony); the broken pyramid stands nearer the road than its 10 cells; the boulders are fewer and smaller than the seven great Enchanted Boulders. L3 keeps the plan.

MU-4 signature view: the Great Pyramid from the dunes

Camera on the great dune south-east of the pyramid (82, 98), about 60 degrees, looking north-west (11,300 from the apex): the pyramid alone in the sand, its gilded capstone, the robbers' tent at its corner, the pale sun in the amber band, the cliffs and the Barrier beyond. Why this and not the boulder field: the pyramid is the map's name and its story (M28-M30, the Pharaoh), seen from both arrivals; the boulder field is a side quest and gets a tour shot at L3. Against the plan: the courses read as steps and a carved dragon frieze runs round the foot (a good idea for L3: one frieze band, seamless along its axis); the trees on the left are not in the plan.

Open questions (DECIDE)

  1. The People while peaceful. (a) 6 placed at the hold (D-47's rows), not in the 110 at load, their 3,854 per member counted only on the war route (as D-1.2 keeps trap kills out); (b) count them in the 110 and trim the wild areas; (c) only Gurodel on D5. Proposed: (a).
  2. The Quivering Forest's giants. (a) wild cyclopes on the ordinary giants rows (11080-11082) round the source's C1 lair with its "Destroy?" device, not the People; (b) a second People camp (peaceful, then the 27.5 go to lizards and birds). Proposed: (a).
  3. The Enchanted Boulders. (a) one set piece in the census (23 POIs); after the answer each of the other six "disappears and leaves behind..." a treasure item, no chests; (b) seven spots (30 POIs: then cut the three signpost clicks, the dwarf camp and a lamp); (c) six small chests at the boulders. Proposed: (a).
  4. The Parabolic Barrier (the source has only the name, a mirror destination). (a) a landmark: a bowed line of Ancient frames (gn_mtl, xaenergy) with a xafield curtain that cannot be crossed, the rim behind it, no event; (b) also a device: a touch shocks (Air damage, a line); (c) also a mirror destination. Proposed: (a).
  5. Three +2 Endurance places (X6's oasis, X3's spring, DD-18's brew; MAPS gives D5 a fourth). (a) keep; (b) D5's fountain gives another stat (Personality); (c) drop it, the energy fountain's +5 Air resistance is D5's permanent device. Proposed: (a), cheapest to change at L4.
  6. The Great Pyramid's size. (a) 12 x 12 cells, about 3,600 high (Giza's slope), seen from both arrivals; (b) 8 x 8, 2,400. A8's DD-15 inside is independent. Proposed: (a).
  7. The broken travel pyramid moved from the source's D1 highlands to the east valley by the road, so the arrival sees it and the hoard is reachable without the cliffs. Keep, or put it on the highlands. Proposed: keep.
  8. Rescaled devices. Shrine of Great Power +15 all stats until rest, daily (the source's +15 levels; D1's +3 levels became +10 all); Fountain of Might +20 Might until rest, daily; energy fountain +5 Air resistance, permanent (D-4). Accept, or other values.
