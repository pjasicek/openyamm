X8 Cloudreach: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concept

Gate G1 review (A6, 2026-11-20). Act II's sky map (party 18-20), an open-air BLV of walkable cloud islands: the Witch Clouds (east, above Vertigo) and the High Magic Clouds (west, above the lagoon) joined by a long rope bridge. One way up from the Witch Tower's roof (XD-04) and the Tower of High Magic's Stairway to Heaven (XD-11); the only way down is to fall. Fly is not cast here. Source: devdocs/xeen/briefs/x8/L1/ (plan.yml: islands as spaces with position and extent; oymap validate clean).

Same grid as the outdoor maps (cells of 512 units, north up). The footprint is about 76 x 64 cells (39,000 x 33,000 units, about 60 % of an outdoor map's playable square); the islands cover about a fifth of it. Heights in brackets are against the Witch landing; the cloud sea is 2,000 below. Rope bridges are striped, cloud-stone ramps and causeways grey.

Look at, and decide

  • Two archipelagos on one map joined by one long bridge (about 10,000 in two spans with a rest cloud).
  • Falls: east of the middle line land on X1 near the Witch Tower, west on X3 near the Tower of High Magic. The bridge's half-way cloud lies over X2's Lake Darzog in the world: a fall there could land in the lake instead.
  • The explorer's statue (the source's +1 level): an award and experience here.
  • The two Cloud Golems (50) on the high cloud as a visible danger a party of 18-20 should walk round.
