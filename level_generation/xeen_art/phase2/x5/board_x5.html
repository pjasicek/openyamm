X5 Winterkill Tundra: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Gate G1 review (A6, 2026-11-20). Cloudside's white south-west: a frozen mere with the sealed Darkstone Tower on its rock, the ghost town Winterkill on the world's edge, Kai Wu's monastery on its crag, the Troll Forest, the Land of the Giants and a glacier sliding off the rim into the stars. In the east, behind the Frostspine, the Act II pocket (party 16-19) where XD-09 and XD-10 open (D-4); the rest is Act III (party 24-28). No geometry is built until you have seen this. 128 x 128 cells of 512 units, north up; the dashed white square is the playable area (cells 19-109). West and south the land ends in a cliff above the stars at x 24 and y 104; north and east the land, the roads and the river run on to the map's edge.

Winterkill (4x)

A4's 17 doors (x5.yml): Mindmeld Guild 4251 (open from day one, Casper), tavern 4252, temple 4253, Guild of Shadows 4254, Furs and Fittings 4255, twelve homes 4257-4268; the mirror 4256, Mayor Randon and the three gongs in the square; the old strength well. All but the guild open with Q-C6. About 200 cells; 4-5 cells from the rim cliff; the Edge Watch on the brink to the west.

The Act II pocket (2x)

From the X3 arrival: the Golem Dungeon's door 7,300 units, XD-10's north mouth (its arrival) 9,200, its south mouth to the Troll Forest side 13,900. Only tier-A monsters near them: Dark Wolves 16, Ogres 18, Evil Archers 8 (D-4). Halon, a well, a hot spring, the lost sledge; Kai Wu's training (to 40) one road over the pass.

MU-4 arrival view (from X3)

Camera at cell (106, 59), 180 above the road, looking west-north-west. MU-4 signature view: the glacier at the rim

Look at, and decide

  • The Act II pocket behind the Frostspine: big enough, and does the ridge read as "harder land beyond"?
  • Winterkill on the rim's brink (the Edge Watch beside it), Darkstone Tower on a rock in the frozen mere.
  • The mere as walkable ice (a new ice ground material) with open water only where the river runs in.
  • Kai Wu's training (to 40) one pass west of the pocket, so Act II parties use it from day one.
  • The signature view inside the trap corner (earned), not from a safe overlook.
