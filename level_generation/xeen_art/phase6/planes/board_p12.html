P1 Plane of Fire of Xeen: lava field in a crater

MU-4 arrival concept raw/p1_arrival_v1.png (one codex exec, no retry); inset: the blockout from the same camera.

`planes_concepts.py p1`: one `codex exec`, first attempt, 65 s; raw `planes/raw/p1_arrival_v1.png` with its prompt (.txt) and log (.log). References: the arrival blockout, D6's lava concept (this world's lava), MM6's `orfyrlga` and MM8's `gcav2flr`. It shows the Cinder Shelf's cracked crust, the causeway left to the statue of flame on its plinth, the lava lake with crust plates and spurts, the hall's ring of columns at the foot of the tallest spire, the Crucible's black ring right of it, the basalt columns right and a lava fall in the crater wall. Against the plan (the plan wins at L2-L3): the crater wall reads as cut slabs (the blockout's), L3 makes it ragged cliffs; the statue stands nearer and larger than drawn (about 3,700 away); two small gold boxes are the blockout's pedestal and couch, not props; the Crucible's ring is lower than the plan's rise.

The Plane of Fire of Xeen: a lake of lava in a crater of black glass under a sky of red smoke. Causeways of cooled crust run between basalt shelves; vents flare underfoot. On its rise the Crucible, a ring of obsidian where the test of Fire waits; beyond it, in a hall cut into the tallest spire, the Fire Sleeper on his couch.

P2 Plane of Air of Xeen: islands in open sky

MU-4 arrival concept raw/p2_arrival_v1.png (one codex exec, no retry); inset: the blockout from the same camera.

`planes_concepts.py p2`: one `codex exec`, first attempt, 69 s; raw `planes/raw/p2_arrival_v1.png` with its prompt and log. The Plane of Air of Xeen: islands of wind-carved white stone hanging in a sky with no ground under it. Bridges of cloud-stone cross the gaps; the wind sings in a ring of hollow stones. In the south-east the Eye of the Winds, a ring of standing stones where the test of Air waits; from it the Stair of Wind climbs to the Sleeper's Aerie, the highest island, where the Air Sleeper dreams.

Open questions (drawn on the defaults)

Common to all four (asked once, in P1's NOTES)

  1. C1 The plane as a map: size and form. (a) One compact open-air BLV per plane (X9's and P5's route), a room of 26 x 26 cells with about 45-50 M units of walkable floor: a small M dungeon by floor and monsters, one element's form each (Fire a lava crater, Air islands in open sky, Earth the `xeen_plane_earth` bowl, Water a flooded hall). (b) Smaller, S class (at most 20 M units, 15 sectors): the test and the chamber only. (c) MM8's way: whole ODMs (1 ODM each). Evidence: MAPS §3: 'S-M dungeon scale each; MM8's planes were whole ODMs; these are deliberately small'. The source's planes are 16 x 16 squares each, the same as a dungeon level. 30 monsters sit between S (15-30) and M (45-75). Default: (a) Depends: L2 geometry, the texture class (S cap 30), the walking length.
  2. C2 The arrival from D7: falling damage. (a) None: the party lands standing on the arrival shelf (`entrance:d7_fall`); the drop is told by D7's corner text ('You step off.'). (b) A short real fall: the party appears 768 above the shelf and takes the engine's falling damage (Feather Fall none), D-6.2's 'a third of HP at most' rule. (c) Damage by script, one line. Evidence: The source's corner square moves the party to the plane's run position with no damage. D-6.2 and D-56 test every sky fall for survivability; a fall into an unknown arena would add a test per plane. Default: (a) Depends: A3's corner hooks (m38.corner_x) and D7's corner script; the plane's arrival test.
  3. C3 What the test of the element is. (a) 'Accept the test of X?' (A3's m38.test_x) raises the guardians in the arena: the Sleeper's Guardian (75) and two Greater elementals (70), the source's own beast; killing the three passes the test (a map variable) and opens the gate to the sleeper's chamber. Declining does nothing. (b) The source's test: accepting costs every member 2,500 damage of the element (resistance applies; at party level 56-60 that kills); the guardians stand in the arena from the start. (c) (a), and the guardians also stand in the arena before the test (no click needed). Evidence: Source maze0025/26: the test square asks 'Accept the test of Fire?' and deals 2,500 fire (2,500 electric on Air), setting a flag that stops the chest burning. m38.yml: 'Yes calls the hook, the map opens the test (its arena and guardians)'. MAPS: 'sleepers' guardians 75'. Default: (a) Depends: A3's m38.test_x text and the passed flag; A2's guardian rows; the arena's wiring.
  4. C4 What the passed test opens. (a) The gate to the sleeper's chamber, where the couch and the test's chest stand: the test is the way to the sleeper. (b) The chest only, as the source (its chest burns until the test is passed); the sleeper's chamber is open from the start. (c) Both separately: the chamber open, the chest sealed in the arena until the test. Evidence: The source's sleeper does not check the test; only the chest does. The family names the guardians 'Sleeper's Guardian'. m38.sleeper_x needs only Chosen One and M37's four reflectors. Default: (a): the guardian guards the sleeper; one gate, no burning chest to explain. Depends: The chamber gate (a door with a state), A3 (no new hook: the gate reads the map variable).
  5. C5 The way out (corners.yml default_way_out). (a) Two ways, both to D7's `entrance:pN_return` by the corner's rest stop: the way up at the arrival, always open (the source's exit square, a fall back to its Skyroad corner), and the sleeper's return dais, shown once the sleeper is awake (no walk back). (b) The return dais only, after the sleeper (the corners.yml default as written); Town Portal and Lloyd's Beacon before. (c) The way up only (the source). Evidence: maze0025 (13,2) and maze0026 (2,2) are fall squares to Skyroad A1 (3,13) and F1 (12,13): the source's way back is always open. A party that cannot pass the test must not be stuck. Default: (a) Depends: D7's p1_return ... p4_return arrivals; one exit preset per way (D-24).
  6. C6 The guardians and the greater elementals: rows and names. (a) One family per element: A2 adds Air, Earth and Water rows for B (62), C (70) and the Sleeper's Guardian (75) beside Fire's 11621-11623 (11624-11626 exist as XD-19's lesser elementals at 55), named as the family ('Greater Air Elemental'). (b) As (a), with the source's names on the C rows: Fire Blower, Whirlwind, Earth Blaster, Tidal Terror. (c) One neutral Sleeper's Guardian row for all four planes, skinned by element. Evidence: monsters.yml: the family is Fire-typed (11620-11623); XD-19's lesser elementals of the other three elements exist (D-47). XEEN_SYSTEMS: the source's planes hold eight of one greater elemental each. Default: (b): the names are the source's and read well in the Xeen voice. Depends: A2's rows; S's skins (MM6's air, MM7's water and earth elementals as in D-47).
  7. C7 The sleepers and the statues: what stands there. (a) One sleeper model for all four (a giant of the element lying in an Ancient stasis couch, its element in the couch's glow), four skins; one statue model per element on a common plinth with the plaque (S). (b) The sleeper as a peaceful placed figure (an A2 row, unkillable as D-51) on a plain couch. (c) Decorations from shipped MM6-MM8 statues only. Evidence: Source: the same object sprite (46) for all four sleepers and (40) for all four statues; the sleepers speak of a 'Grunfeld field generator' (Ancient technology). Default: (a) Depends: S models (4 statues, 1 sleeper with 4 skins, the couch, the pedestal, the dais).
  8. C8 Points of interest and chests per plane. (a) The sparse-special band: 10-12 POIs (MAP_CRAFT §6: sparse special maps at least 10) and 2 chests (S's 0-3; MM8's planes 0-2): the test's chest and one trapped cache, each with a reason, plus one find (a camp, a wreck) and one buff device. (b) The source: the test, the statue, the sleeper and one chest (4-5 POIs, 1 chest). (c) MM8's planes: 0-3 POIs, 0-2 chests. Evidence: MM8 elemf 3 POIs and 2 chests, elema 1 and 1, elemw 0 and 0 (MAP_CRAFT_CALIBRATION); the source has one chest a plane. Default: (a) Depends: The census in totals; A2 treasure 6.

P1

  1. P1 Q1 The lava: how much it hurts and how the party gets out. (a) The lava is the floor: `lavtyl` faces burn as MM's lava (fire damage while standing in it, Levitate none); every causeway and shelf edge within 64 of the lava has a crust bank to walk out; the raised shelves have ramps. (b) Lava kills (a death trigger, as the world rim). (c) Lava is decoration under invisible walls. Evidence: D-40/D6: `lavtyl`, never water, burns. The engine burns a floor face named lava*/lavtyl (IndoorMovementController) and Levitate lifts the party (as MM8). Default: (a) Depends: L2 banks and ramps; the measure lava_bank_max.
  2. P1 Q2 The vents. (a) Nine small floor faces with the source's rule (1 in 3: 50 fire to each member), marked by a glowing crack texture; five in the Vent Field, four on the loops; none on the critical causeways. (b) Vents erupt on a timer (a set piece every few seconds) with a visible spurt. (c) No vents. Evidence: maze0025: nine squares call one event: random 1-3, on 3 Damage 50 fire. Default: (a) Depends: A3's line; A1's floor-event check; the new texture xpfvent.
  3. P1 Q3 Sky. (a) MM8's shipped `plane_fire` (engine library) by name. (b) A new Xeen preset `xeen_plane_fire` (A6): plane_fire with a lower ember glow and slow-drifting ash clouds. (c) The Darkside amber day (xeendark1). Evidence: WORLD_DESIGN §4: the planes' themes are plane_fire, plane_air, underwater and a new earth preset. plane_fire has no sun and no stars: a timeless plane. Default: (a): shipped and cheapest to reverse. Depends: atmosphere.yml; A6's sky file.

P2

(a) In the map: below -1,500 the wind throws the party back onto the Gale Landing (entrance:d7_fall) with falling damage (D-6.2's rule); the test and the plane are not lost. (b) Out to D7's entrance:p2_return with falling damage (as the way up, the hard way). (c) To the surface under D7's north-east corner (D6), as every sky map falls to its surface. Evidence: The plane is not over the world: the source has no fall from it but its exit square (to Skyroad F1). X8, X9 and P5 fall to the surface because they hang over it. Default: (a) Depends: A3's Xeen.skyFall with an in-map target; P2's fall tests (D-54's shape).
  2. P2 Q2 The storm cells. (a) Nine scorched floor faces with the source's rule (1 in 3: 50 electrical to each member); none on bridges or in the Eye. (b) Lightning on a timer at the storm cairn only (a set piece). (c) None. Evidence: maze0026: nine squares call one event: random 1-3, on 3 Damage 50 type 3 (electrical). Default: (a) Depends: A3's line; xpascorch; A1's floor-event check.
  3. P2 Q3 Sky. (a) MM8's shipped `plane_air` (engine library) by name: below_horizon sky. (b) `xeencloud` (xeen_cloud_sea) as every Xeen sky map. (c) A new Xeen preset `xeen_plane_air` (A6): plane_air with no cloud sea below, only blue. Evidence: WORLD_DESIGN §4 names plane_air for the plane. The plane must not look like X8/X9/D7's cloud sea: there is no world under it. Default: (a) Depends: atmosphere.yml; A6's sky file.
  4. P2 Q4 Only elementals on P2?. (a) Yes: Lesser Air Elementals (55), Air Elementals (62), Greater Air Elementals (70), as the source (eight Whirlwinds); variety from the islands and the storm. (b) Add a pair of Sky Golems (11594, 75) guarding the Stair of Wind's foot (over the band). (c) Add Cloud Golems (11592, 50) as the A tier (under the band). Evidence: monsters.yml: no other Air family at 55-70; rocs and griffins stop at 48. Default: (a) Depends: A2's rows (C6); kill XP.
