P3 Plane of Earth of Xeen: a cavern-like bowl under a dust sky

MU-4 arrival concept raw/p3_arrival_v1.png (one codex exec, no retry); inset: the blockout from the same camera.

`planes_concepts.py p3`: one `codex exec`, first attempt, 62 s; raw `planes/raw/p3_arrival_v1.png` with its prompt and log. References: the arrival blockout, the Darkside day sky (for the manner of dim amber light), MM8's `gwspotwl` and `gwsphlfl`. It shows the Dust Ledge's packed earth, the seated granite giant on its plinth left, a field of standing stones in the middle, the Anvil's cracked bedrock ringed by monoliths right, the Sleeper's Hollow glowing amber under the overhang between them, scree and boulders in the drops, crystals in the walls and a brown dust sky. Against the plan (the plan wins at L2-L3): the bowl reads shallow (the scree lies 1,024 under the arrival, the Hollow 768); the walls read as quarried steps rather than leaning cavern walls; the Pillar Field is squared monoliths, the plan's are rough pillars like petrified trees.

The Plane of Earth of Xeen: a bowl of raw stone under a ceiling of brown dust, its walls leaning in like a cavern's. Terraces step down the pit past a quarry no one finished, a hall of split geodes and a field of stone pillars; at the bottom the Anvil, a floor of bare bedrock ringed by monoliths where the test of Earth waits, and below it, down the Root Stair, the Sleeper's Hollow.

P4 Plane of Water of Xeen: a drowned temple in a shallow sea

MU-4 arrival concept raw/p4_arrival_v1.png (one codex exec, no retry); inset: the blockout from the same camera.

`planes_concepts.py p4`: one `codex exec`, first attempt, 59 s; raw `planes/raw/p4_arrival_v1.png` with its prompt and log. References: the arrival blockout, X9's approved concept (light and stone), MM8's `gtempw1` and `gtempflr`. It shows the colonnade walk with steps down to clear green water on both sides, the statue of Water pouring her jar into the fountain, the Sleeper's Grotto glowing blue under its arch, the Whirlpool Court's broken columns round a turning pool right, the coral terrace and the wrecked ship left, sea stacks and arches in the mist. Against the plan: the sky is painted as a rippling sea surface seen from below (an underwater ceiling): striking, but it says 'under the sea' while the party swims at a surface; question 3 decides the preset; the grotto stands nearer and larger than drawn; the statue stands at the walk's end, the plan puts it mid-forecourt.

The Plane of Water of Xeen: a temple drowned in a sea with no shore, its colonnades standing out of green water under a sky the colour of the sea. Spray blows over the walks; a ship lies wrecked in the south court though there is no coast to wreck on. In the west the Whirlpool Court, where the test of Water turns in the pool, and beyond a falling veil of water, the Sleeper's Grotto.

P3

  1. P3 Q1 The form: a cavern-like bowl or open sky under xeen_plane_earth. (a) A bowl: terraces stepping down a pit whose walls lean in like a cavern's, open to the `xeen_plane_earth` dust sky (open-air BLV); drops into the scree hurt, scree slopes lead back up. (b) A true cavern: a closed BLV with a rock ceiling and no sky (MM8's eleme is a BLV); the preset unused. (c) Floating rock islands in the dust sky, as P2 but heavy (falls caught). Evidence: corners.yml and lanes README give P3 the `xeen_plane_earth` preset (dust, no sun); MM8's eleme is a cave; the source's plane is open ground. Default: (a): the preset, the closure and the way down into the earth. Depends: L2 geometry; the preset; the drop rule.
  2. P3 Q2 Falling rock or quakes. (a) The source's nine rockfall cells (1 in 3: 50 physical to each member) with a short camera shake when one hits. (b) Quakes: every few minutes the whole plane shakes (evt.CameraShake) and rock falls on the nine cells for a few seconds (a timed set piece). (c) No hazard beyond the drops. Evidence: maze0027: nine squares call one event: random 1-3, on 3 Damage 50 type 0 (physical). evt.CameraShake exists (MM9 cutscenes). Default: (a) Depends: A3's line; xperubble; A1's floor-event check.
  3. P3 Q3 Only elementals on P3?. (a) Yes: Lesser Earth Elementals (55, 11626), Earth Elementals (62) and Greater Earth Elementals (70), as the source (eight Earth Blasters). (b) Add two Onyx Golems (11584, 85) as the Root Stair's wardens (over the band). (c) Add Diamond Golems (11583, 50) in the quarry (under the band). Evidence: monsters.yml: no other earth family at 55-70 (material golems stop at 50, then 85). Default: (a) Depends: A2's rows (C6); kill XP.

P4

  1. P4 Q1 How water works for a party without Water Walk. (a) MM9's water volumes, already in the engine for BLVs: the party swims at the surface with no breath lost, dives at will (breath 15 s, then 1/6 of maximum HP every 2 s), no falling damage into water; steps out at every walk's foot; the outer sea's current carries swimmers back; Water Walk does nothing yet (the engine's open item); nothing on the critical path needs swimming. (b) MM6-MM8's rule: deep water drowns without Water Walk (the outdoor tiles' damage); the party keeps to the walks. (c) The sea as decoration under invisible walls: walks only. Evidence: devdocs/mm9_rework/MM9_WATER_SWIMMING_DESIGN.md (done: swim, dive, breath, drowning, current; open: Water Walk on a volume). The source's plane is all water and walkable. Default: (a) Depends: L2 water volumes; A1 only if Water Walk should lift the party onto the sea.
  2. P4 Q2 Diving. (a) No dive anywhere: the wreck's hold is half flooded and waded; diving is only for whoever falls in. (b) One optional dive: the ship's strongbox lies in the flooded hold, 6 s under at most (breath 15 s). (c) The Grotto reached by a dive under the sea arch instead of the Veil. Evidence: MM9's breath is 15 s; drowning is 1/6 HP every 2 s. A plane at party 56-60 can take a short dive; a required one would be the world's first. Default: (a) Depends: The wreck's L2 shape.
  3. P4 Q3 Sky (no shipped plane_water). (a) A new Xeen preset `xeen_plane_water` (A6, data only, after approval): sea-green zenith, aquamarine horizon, no sun disc, a bright diffuse light, slow caustic cloud, below_horizon sky. (b) The engine's `underwater` (no sky drawn, flat fog): reads as the sea bed, at odds with swimming at a surface. (c) Shipped `plane_air` (blue open sky). Evidence: WORLD_DESIGN §4 names `underwater` for the water plane; the engine library has no plane_water; `underwater` draws no sky at all. Default: (a); (c) until A6 writes it Depends: atmosphere.yml; A6's sky file (a new preset).
  4. P4 Q4 Octopods on the Plane of Water. (a) Two groups of Great Octopods (11471, 60, the swimmer profile of D-36) in the open water between the walks: a reason not to swim. (b) Elementals only, as the source (eight Tidal Terrors). (c) Krakens (70) instead. Evidence: monsters.yml: octopods are this world's only swimmers at 50-70; D-36 gave them MM9's swimmer AI. Default: (a) Depends: A2 (the D4 row reused), S (none new).
