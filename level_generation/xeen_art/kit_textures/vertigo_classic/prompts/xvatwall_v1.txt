Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for a high-resolution remaster of Might and Magic IV: Clouds of Xeen (1992), used in a 3D engine like
that of Might and Magic VI-VIII.
The reference images show the original 1992 VGA art (small pixel art shown enlarged) and a smoothed layout guide made
from it: the dark towers of Clouds of Xeen (the towers of witches and wizards and the strongholds of evil): walls of big rough-hewn dark grey stone blocks laid in courses with dark joints and chipped edges, doors of dark planks under a rounded head with a heavy iron ring, between plain stone pilasters under a stone lintel; cold, grim and dark, the original's own colours kept.
Repaint it at high resolution as a modern remaster would: keep the original's design, layout, proportions and colours,
and add the believable material detail its low resolution could not show (one texel is about one centimetre), in a
hand-painted, slightly stylised realism, not a photograph. No pixel-art look, no dithering, no hard pixel steps.
Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast
shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1536 (portrait) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. A wall of big rough-hewn dark grey stone blocks as the original tower wall (References 1 and 2, its blocks repeated): exactly three courses of blocks from the top of the canvas to the bottom, each block about one and a half times as wide as it is tall, the courses' joints staggered (running bond), dark mortar joints, chipped edges, a few darker stains; the same cold dark grey; nothing else.
