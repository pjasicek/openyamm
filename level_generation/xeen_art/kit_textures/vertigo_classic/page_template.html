Vertigo in its original timber and plaster

Clouds of Xeen's town walls repainted at the 4× tier and laid on Vertigo's houses in place of the MM7 Harmondale stand-ins. The walls, the plank door and the colours come from the 1992 art; windows, corner posts, roofs and beams never appear in the original's towns and were painted in its style. Same camera, same time of day, same lighting bake in every shot. Variant A was chosen; this round makes its bays join seamlessly. "A · seamless" is the game as installed, with the roof edges (verges and barge boards); "A · as painted" is the pilot before them.

Notes

  • The seamless kit: a post is 24 texels and a beam 28, half of each at every bay edge, cut from the seamless beam tile (its 256-texel repeat divides the bay), so bays side by side and storeys on storeys meet in one timber. The plaster is the plain bay's own, its cracks and stains evened so the repeat does not show. The door (lintel, jambs and leaf) and the window (frame and sill) are cut from their paintings and set into it. The gable uses the wall, so its posts and beams continue up the gable end.
  • The whole of X1 changes, not only the town: the hamlets' and farms' half-timbered houses use the same kits. Stone service buildings, the town wall and its towers keep their MM7 stone. Shop signs are unchanged.
  • Houses that had thatch (the MM7 poor kit) get the original's golden thatch, the rest wooden shingles like the roof of the original's town well.
  • Every bay has a post at each edge, so the original's post reappears at every 128 units; its panels were wider (about 170 units for 256 high), so ours are a little taller and narrower.
  • The comparison shots above reuse the old lighting bake (the geometry is identical, only texture names change). The installed maps below were re-baked.
  • Made with Codex image generation from the original frames and layout guides cut from them: 23 calls in all, counting the roofs' and beams' seam passes and one retry each for the two beams and A's corner post. Ten textures per variant, seven painted and three cut from those (the right-hand corner, the gable, the ridge).
