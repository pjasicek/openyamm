Asset: a painted sign texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: Harmondale, the home town of Might and Magic VII: half-timbered houses of cream and ochre plaster between dark brown oak beams, grey weathered plank walls, rough fieldstone, thatch and wooden shingle roofs, and the town's hanging shop signs of painted wooden boards in iron-cornered frames; warm, cheerful and bright.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition (Reference 1 is the layout guide: the board covers exactly its flat brown rectangle, at the same position,
size and proportions; the grey around it stays plain grey): on a perfectly flat plain mid-grey (#808080) background, one flat rectangular board seen exactly
straight on, its outline perfectly straight and axis-aligned, centred, 128 wide by 64 tall (aspect 2.00 to 1),
filling about 92 percent of the canvas width. Nothing else on the grey: no shadow, no wall, no nails outside the board.
The board: An armourer's sign: a signboard of three horizontal dark brown oak planks like the first reference, in a thin oak frame with small iron corner plates. At the left end a painted breastplate made of overlapping green snake scales with a brass rim. Beside it, in cream-white painted capitals with a dark green shadow, the large words "SCALE HOUSE" on one line, and under them in small letters "ARMOUR". Slightly worn paint.
