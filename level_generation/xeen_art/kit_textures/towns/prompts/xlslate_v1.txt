Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the stone towns of Might and Magic VIII (Ravenshore's dock quarter and Regna): low houses of grey rounded boulder-stone between dark weathered timber posts, grey dressed ashlar with carved friezes, plank doors with iron straps and dark blue-grey slate roofs; cool, desaturated and dry.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. A roof of neatly cut slate, repeating: overlapping horizontal rows of thin rectangular slates with square-cut lower edges, laid in straight courses, each row offset by half a slate from the one below, eight rows over the height of the canvas. Dark blue-grey and charcoal slate like the first reference, each slate a slightly different cool grey, faint pale lichen spots on a few, dry and matte, a thin dark shadow line under each row.
