Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the wizard towers of Bracada and the towers of Mount Nighon of Might and Magic VII: grey rubble stone and slate-blue coursed stone under black timber friezes and posts, black plank roofs, carved dark oak, a witch's or wizard's tower of dark timber and slate.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1536 (portrait) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. A slate-hung tower wall seen straight on, repeating: small rectangular slates hung in overlapping horizontal courses on the wall, each course offset by half a slate, dark slate-blue and charcoal like the first reference's stones, with a few darker and a few greenish slates, the slates' lower edges slightly lifted and chipped. About twelve even courses from top to bottom. No timber, no window.
