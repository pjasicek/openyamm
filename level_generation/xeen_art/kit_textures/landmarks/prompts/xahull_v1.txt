Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Control Center of Might and Magic VI and the crashed starship of Might and Magic VII, the technology of the Ancients: dark teal-grey and verdigris steel panels with rust streaks, riveted plates, pipes and conduits, glowing green, red and cyan energy grids and light strips, dark glass consoles.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. Large outer hull plating of an ancient machine-fortress, repeating: big rectangular dull teal-grey steel plates of three sizes fitted edge to edge with thin dark seams, rows of round rivet heads along the seams like the third reference, a few recessed square access panels and narrow vents, verdigris and brown rust streaks running down from the rivets, scuffs and dents; the colours of the first reference.
