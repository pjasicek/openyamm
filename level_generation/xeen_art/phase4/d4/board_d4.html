D4 Lakeside and Blackfang: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Phase 4 review (A6, 2026-11-20), for a decider before any geometry. Darkside's north-east corner, Act IV wilderness (party 35-38), the world rim on its north and east sides. Octopod Lake fills the middle with the Isle of Lost Souls (DD-12) a short row off Lakeside, the coven's low town of grey stone and slate on the south shore (A4's 15 doors and the mirror; Naomi in the stocks; the guild's riddle door into DD-20). Across the water Castle Blackfang crowns a black crag in a storm of bats; its gate at the crag's foot never opens. The Snow River comes down its rapids from a snowfield at the north rim; Magic Mountain (the wizards' camp, the gargoyles and DD-23, Ector's woodpile) rises over the Forest of Peril in the west. The lake falls over the east rim. Source: devdocs/xeen/briefs/d4/L1/ (project, plan, sketch in Map Studio's schema, NOTES.md; oymap validate clean).

MU-4 arrival view (from D3)

Camera on Lakeside Brow (88, 102), 180 above the road, looking north: the road down past Well Fork to Lakeside on the shore, the jetty, the Isle with its ruined tower, Castle Blackfang beyond, the falls at the east rim, Magic Mountain far left. Against the plan: the painting brings the crag and the falls much nearer (the plan has Blackfang about 28,000 away on the north shore and the falls behind the Rimward crags) and gives the castle fairy-tale spires; L3 keeps the plan's distances and the towers kit's Darkside stone and cones.

MU-4 signature view: Blackfang over the lake

Camera at the ferry jetty's end (72, 80), about 60 degrees, looking north-north-east: the Isle of Lost Souls ahead, Castle Blackfang on its black crag in the storm of bats across the water, its sealed gatehouse at the crag's foot. The view every party has before the ferry (M20) and again on every return; both of the map's promises (the Isle's dungeon and the unreachable castle) in one look. Against the plan: the painting joins the Isle to the crag and puts the gatehouse on the lake side under the castle; the plan keeps about 2,500 of deep water between them and the gatehouse on the crag's west foot, facing the north-shore track (not the jetty). The pale sun stands behind the castle; the glow over the north-east rim is only a warm horizon here.

Open questions (DECIDE)

  1. The storm of bats. (a) 20-40 decorative bat models (S's xeen_bat.glb) on looping circling paths round the towers (animated decorations, no AI); (b) a particle effect of small bat sprites over the keep; (c) a few real, non-hostile bat actors. Proposed: (a).
  2. The octopods in the water. (a) swimmers that never leave deep water (MM9 AI movement swim, A2's modifiers), a new octopod model; (b) stationary lurkers placed on the skerries; (c) no octopods on D4 (the lake only looks dangerous). Proposed: (a), 5 placed.
  3. The coven's witches after Q-D22. (a) the two placed groups hidden at load once q-d22.done, peaceful to members (q-d22.joined); (b) they stay, hostile, after the break. Proposed: (a).
  4. DD-20's second way in. Keep the source's sewer shaft by the jetty (open from day one, sealed with a line until DD-20 exists) beside the guild's riddle door, or the guild door only. Proposed: keep both.
  5. Leafpoint, a talking tree. (a) a great old tree decoration whose click opens her dialogue (A3's speak event); (b) an immobile actor with a tree model (S). Proposed: (a).
  6. The ferry back from the Isle. (a) a click at the Isle's jetty moves the party to lakeside_dock (A4 writes the line); (b) one-way: Town Portal, the mirror or water walking. Proposed: (a).
  7. D5's crossing at y 80 (a level valley at 1,500 out of the Forest of Peril), or nearer the source's E1/D1 seam (y 60-64, under Magic Mountain). Proposed: y 80.
  8. POIs 35 and chests 10 sit at the tops of their bands. Keep, or drop the third signpost's click and the hunters' hide (33, 9). Proposed: keep.
