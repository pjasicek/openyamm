D2 Sprite Forest and Ogre Pass: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Phase 3 review (A6, 2026-11-20), for a decider before any geometry. Darkside's south centre, Act III (party 21-25): the Gemstone Range walls the north; a spur of it crosses the middle, and the caravan road from Castleview to Sandcaster climbs over it at Ogre Pass between Graalg's two timber forts. South of the road the Sprite Forest runs to the world's rim: glowing caps, the great tree of Bark (DD-09) at its heart with Nibbler's hollow at its roots, Sharla's treehouse village beside it, troll country with three holes (DD-11A-C), and the Glimmerbrook falling off the edge into the stars. East of the spur, the Southern Tower (DD-06) stands in darker woods. MU-4 signature view: Ogre Pass

Camera at cell (60, 44) on the caravan road below the pass, looking east up to the saddle: the west fort on its knoll, Graalg's fire in the saddle, the east fort beyond, the crystal-veined Gemstone Range behind. Why this view: the painting softens the spur's shoulders and brings the east fort level with the saddle; the plan's 3,100 crests and the fort beyond the saddle stand. The arrival already shows the forest and the great tree; the pass is the map's second biome, its gauntlet (both routes of Q-D11 are on screen) and the only view that shows the range that closes the map. The village's form waits on the treehouse question below, so painting it now would fix a form not yet chosen.

Open questions

  1. How are the treehouses and the great tree built? (a) giant hollow trunks as round BModel parts (bark texture), doors at their feet, decks, stairs and rope walkways from the studio's structures, crowns of shipped tree models on top; the great tree the same at 3 cells across with a new crown model; (b) new giant-tree GLBs (S lane) with BModel door facades; (c) doors up on platforms reached by stairs. Proposed: (a), doors at the foot (nothing to climb to a shop), Sharla's stair only to the chest.
  2. Graalg's ogres at load. (a) hostile, as MAPS ("peaceful if Q-D11"), counted in the 135, Graalg and his guards peaceful at the parley ground; (b) neutral until attacked (then 22 short: 9 more markers elsewhere). Proposed: (a).
  3. Sprites below the band. The sprite family is 10/14/18, D2's band A 16-22: (a) keep (24 of 135, the forest's colour); (b) A2 adds a D2 tier (16/22); (c) swap the wild sprites for an in-band family. Proposed: (a).
  4. Monga melons. MAPS lists them on D2; q-d9 puts all four on D1 (the source's A4/B4). Plan: none on D2, Nibbler's own patch as dressing after q-d9.done.
  5. The river. MU-1 paints a river through D1, D2 and D3; the D1 sketch has one on D1's south. D2's Glimmerbrook rises inside and falls off the rim; if D1 runs its river to its east edge, D2 takes it in at y 96-100.
  6. Burned forts outside. Show the forts as charred ruins after q-d11's burned bits (a model swap by qbit), or leave them unchanged outside?
