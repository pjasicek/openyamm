D1 Castleview Vale: MU-3 plan and MU-4 concepts

Phase 3 review (A6, 2026-11-20). The Darkside's hub and its south-west corner (MU-1): the world rim on the west and south, D6 north by a long mountain road (x 64), D2 east by the road at y 60. Castleview, A4's 44 doors inside low grey walls with one gate, sits in a bend of the Vale Water; outside the gate the travel pyramid, Joe, Justine and the gremlins' burrow; north the Dark Grove and its rope into the sky, the Western Tower by the rim, the gemsmiths under the mine hills; south-west Castle Kalindra as a ghost on its rise, the river falling off the world below it. Castleview (3x)

A4's d1.yml: 16 services (4451-4466), Lucio's Jetty 4901 on the west wall's top (D-5.3, opens with m30.done), the mirror 4467 in the square, 27 residences (jail, library, Public Storage, four Drawkcab cells, 20 houses): 44 doors in about 400 cells. Tavern and Adventurer's Inn first inside the gate, smith and armourer across Gate Street, magic and alchemist side by side on the square, five guilds in a row, the temple raised in the south-east corner, Kramer's coach yard inside the east wall; Ellinger's round tower in the north-east quarter; the sewer hatch by the square. Grey t06 boulder-stone houses, t09 ashlar for civic buildings and walls (MU-5 Darkside kit).

Outside the gate (3x)

The pyramid's knoll west of the road; the first fight about 2,600 from it (gremlins in the old trenches, E1), MAPS asks for 3,000 or less. Joe's stall and sign on the road, Justine's camp to the west, the notice by the gate, the court burrow under the wall east of it: all reachable without a pass. The gorge east of the walls with the cut rope bridge and the burned mill; the East Bridge (intact) is the way round until the court is won.

MU-4 (a) arrival: from the travel pyramid

Camera beside the pyramid at (61, 38), 180 above the knoll, looking south-east at the north wall: Castleview's low slate-stone walls and gatehouse with the besiegers' empty trenches and stakes before them, Ellinger's tower above the roofs, the mill gorge with its cut rope bridge and the burned mill on the left; amber sky, small pale sun (A3's m14.arrival text). MU-4 (b) signature: the vale from the south bank

Camera on the prayer shrine's knoll at (52, 90), looking north over the Vale Water: Castle Kalindra as a translucent ghost on its rise (left), the Dark Grove's great tree with its rope into the sky (middle distance), Castleview's walls (right). Why this view: it puts the map's three promises in one look (the castle to restore, the way up, the hub), from a quiet bank an Act III party reaches early by the East Bridge and the south lane, and the ghost castle reads best against the river and the amber sky.

Choices, and questions for the decider

  • POI count with the hub's doors. 44 without service doors (MAPS' "named 20 + 20-30 generic"); 61 if D-11.4's census counts Castleview's 17 service doors. Options: count devices only for a hub (as X5 did), or cut about 11 generic devices (signposts, the west well, the bottle, the wayside shrine, the Edge Stone, the falls).
  • One gate. MAPS' sketch has a south gate; the plan has only the north gate so that A3's single m14 gate hook is the only way in. A south postern would need the same hook. The gate must open from inside unconditionally (temple, stables, Town Portal and mirror set parties down inside).
  • The Vale Water rises inside D1 (the Sardaj Falls) and falls off the south rim. MU-1 paints one river through D1 and D2; only the road's crossing (y 60) is agreed with D2. If D2 wants the river to come in from the east, the crossing y must be fixed for both maps.
  • Hector's Diamond Works stands in the gemsmiths' row with the other three (the source has him in B3, the north-east): d1.yml groups the four as gemsmith#1-4, which the studio places in one district.
  • The broken bridge is the rope bridge over the mill gorge east of the walls (visible from the pyramid, as m14.arrival says); the stone East Bridge stays whole so D2 and the Sardaj lands are reachable before the court is won.
  • Falista's hut (MAPS, source A4) is in the plan as a device (no house in d1.yml): spell points with the Clouds award "Restored Falista". MAPS' "Well of Luck" (sketch) is the sheet's "+2 permanent Might" well.
  • The Dark Grove rope to D7: its condition and D7's arrival are D7's owner's; until then a message.
  • The Sardaj (12) are peaceful at load and not in the 135; hostile they add 12 near the north edge.
