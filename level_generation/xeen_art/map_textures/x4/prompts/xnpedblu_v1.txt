Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the dressed brown sandstone of Might and Magic VII's Harmondale and Castle Harmondale (chb1s, chb1): warm brown and tan dressed blocks with rounded worn edges, fine pitted grain and pale sandy mortar joints.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. The top of a stone pedestal cap, repeating: a grid of exactly two by two square dressed slabs of the references' warm brown sandstone, paler and sun-bleached, the slabs' worn rounded edges and pitted grain as in the first reference's top course. Every joint between the slabs, along the vertical and horizontal centre lines and along all four canvas edges, is a straight inlaid strip of glossy clear mid-blue enamel, as wide as a finger, set flush in the stone with a thin dark line on each side. In the middle of each slab one round inlaid boss of the same bright clear blue enamel, about a third of the slab's width, with a thin bronze rim. The blue is light and clean, like cornflower or lapis glaze, unmistakably blue, never teal, green or grey. All four slabs alike.
