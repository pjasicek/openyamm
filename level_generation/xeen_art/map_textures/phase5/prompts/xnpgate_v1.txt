Asset: a flat single-picture surface texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the cool grey Darkside town ashlar of Might and Magic VIII's Regna and Shadowspire (t09a01): grey dressed blocks with dark mortar, sombre and weathered, hand-painted.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge. A carved stone crest panel set into the first reference's grey dressed ashlar, seen straight on: in the middle, in deep low relief, a human skull seen from the front, and above it, carved across its brow, a single large closed eye with long lashes, lid shut. Round them a plain carved circle. The relief is the same grey stone, shaded only by soft shadow, weathered, a little dark in the hollows; no paint, no gold, no lettering. All round the circle, out to every edge, the plain grey ashlar of the first reference continues unchanged, the same block size, colour and dark mortar, so the crest sits in the wall without a border or frame. Sombre, not gory.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
