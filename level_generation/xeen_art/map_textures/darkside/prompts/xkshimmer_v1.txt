Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the pale cream and white dressed ashlar of Might and Magic VI's Castle Ironfist (hcwall): large smooth cream-white blocks with thin grey mortar joints and soft worn edges.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. The castle's cream-white ashlar of the references, block for block the same size and joint pattern, seen as if the castle were not quite in the world: the whole surface washed in pale lavender and soft violet light, silver white where the light is strongest, and crossed from top to bottom by faint vertical bands of shimmering light like an aurora curtain or a heat haze, some bands brighter, some fainter, slightly wavering. The mortar joints are still readable but faded and softly doubled, as if seen through rippling glass. Luminous, cool, misty and calm; pale violet and silver only, never neon, never pink, never dark.
