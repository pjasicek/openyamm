Asset: a flat single-picture surface texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the drowned temple stone of Might and Magic VIII's temples (gtempflr, gtempw1): blue-grey slate flagstones and pale blue-veined marble, wet and cool, hand-painted in the games' soft painted manner under flat even light.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge. The blue-grey slate flagstones of the first reference seen from straight above, their colour, joints and grain unchanged at the edges of the picture, drenched by a wave that has just broken over them: in the middle a wide irregular sheet of white sea foam and lacy spray about half the picture wide, thinning outward into streaks and bubbles of foam lying in the joints, the stone under and round it darkened and glossy with standing sea water in pale aquamarine pools, a little frost-white rime on the wettest stones. The outer quarter of the picture on every side is plain wet flagstone that matches the first reference, so the face sits in a flagstone floor without a seam. No waves standing up, no droplets in the air, no shells, no ice blocks.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
