# Codex brief: HD dagger for the MM9 Skeleton (mm9_skeleton_claude)

Scope: image generation only. Write ONLY into `level_generation/creatures/mm9_skeleton_claude/references_prop/`. No builds, no Meshy.
Use your built-in image generation tool. Record `generation_runs.json` as `references_hd/generation_runs.json`; keep the raw return in `raw/`.

Inputs (attached, in order): 1. `mm9_dagger.png`: the ORIGINAL Might and Magic IX model of the dagger, flat on its blade plane, design, proportions and
outline authority (its faceting and blur are not the design). 2. `mm9_dagger_skin_x12.png`: the original skin enlarged, surface colour only (the grey background is unused).

Deliverable: `dagger_flat.png`, 1536x1024 landscape, unresized, true transparent background. One complete dagger, exact side-on view of the flat of the
blade, orthographic, the same orientation as input 1: pommel at IMAGE LEFT, tip at IMAGE RIGHT, perfectly level (horizontal). The dagger spans about 88 % of the image
width, generous margins, nothing cropped.

Identity: a faithful high-definition reconstruction of the existing weapon (a magic dagger): a straight double-edged blade with parallel edges and a long tapering point, a raised central ridge
along its whole length; polished pale blue-silver steel with a soft sheen. The base third of the blade is a dark inlaid blue-black patterned panel, with a large red diamond gem in a pale bevel and a smaller red diamond gem towards the point.
A pale silver cross-guard perpendicular to the blade: a faceted diamond boss at the centre and two tapered arms with slightly hooked tips. The grip is dark blue-black ribbed wire, slightly tapering toward the pommel.
A small faceted pale silver arrowhead pommel.

Light: even soft neutral studio light, no cast shadow, no floor, no text, no glow, no hand, no scabbard. Do NOT redesign the dagger, add other gems, engravings of text, or a curve.

Checks: flat side-on view; pommel left, tip right; straight, level; two red gems on the dark panel; silver guard with faceted boss; true alpha; nothing cropped. One corrective regeneration at most.
