# Visual review — natf

Reviewed all native action references, ten generated raw sheets, all 59 registered base images and final mask
overlays, standing palette views, attack/death comparisons and worst contour patches. The native dark-skinned
warrior remains distinct from the civilian families: short curly hair, asymmetrical feather armor, right curved
blade, left pointed shield and barefoot gait. Native shield rotations, overhead attack, recoil and collapse poses
are preserved. The native duplicate fidget image shares one enhanced tile; unused natffib0 is retained as artwork.

The first green-only mask missed olive armor and selected blade fringes. A reviewed olive hue mask with stronger
saturation seeds now protects neutral metal. Connected material regions retain their interior armor highlights;
small isolated warm specks are removed. The final red mirrored-walk capture confirms removal of the red blade
stripe. Shield weathering retains some ochre/neutral patches, while its painted face and colored straps/panels
follow the native grey, olive and red appearances. Skin, hair, pale feathers, blade and gold neck collar remain
visually distinct. All-frame mask contact was rechecked after this correction.

All cleaned, cropped and atlas-extracted frames pass residual-matte checks with zero candidates above the configured
threshold. Worst light/dark patches show no obvious magenta halo. Browser captures show red mirrored profile walk,
grey attack with mask overlay, and green prone body on a light background. Native/restored floor placement agrees.

Remaining approximations: feather layering, hair strands, shield weathering and facial microdetail vary slightly
between poses. Armor is smoother and its highlights clearer than the native pixel rendering; near-black native
shadows are interpreted more softly. Small warm weathered details are intentionally retained rather than forcing
flat recoloring over every shield pixel. Alpha is inferred from a matte. These are accepted offline limits.
