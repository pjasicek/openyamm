# Visual review — pman

Reviewed native references and all ten generated sheets, every registered base frame and final mask overlay,
standing variants, attack/death comparisons and the light/dark worst-edge contact. The adult villager retains
short auburn hair, green tunic, pale leggings and brown shoes, with empty hands outside the native dagger attack.
Walking views, recoil, subtle fidget and the forward collapse remain recognizable. Both unused native artworks
are retained without inserting them into playback.

The dagger acquired green during matte processing and incorrectly entered the cloth mask. Blade-only polygons
preserve alpha and luminance while neutralizing RGB and excluding metal from variants. All six corrected attack
frames were inspected in the red appearance; the final grey-variant viewer mask capture confirms silver metal.
The full mask contact was rechecked after the repair. Native/restored mirrored walk and prone captures show
matching logical scale and floor placement.

All cleaned, exported and atlas-extracted frames have zero residual-matte candidates above the configured threshold.
The worst-edge contact shows no obvious magenta halo on light or dark backgrounds. Crop reconstruction, exact atlas
extraction, base bypass, protected pixels, variant alpha, source hashes and native per-palette sequences pass.

Remaining approximations: face and tunic fold microdetail vary slightly between poses, the restored skin/leggings
are warmer and the native near-black shadows are softer. The tunic's muted red follows the native palette rather
than becoming saturated scarlet. Fine finger shapes and cloth weave are inferred detail. Alpha is matte-derived.
These are accepted offline limitations; no runtime performance is claimed.
