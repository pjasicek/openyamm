# Runtime geometry trial — 30,000 triangles

Derived directly from the repaired dense master using local Blender collapse decimation.
28,732 body triangles + 1,268 back-plate/rivet triangles; sword excluded. No Meshy credits used.
The exported GLB was reimported and measured; eight clay views rendered. Front and quarter views inspected.

The trial retains spikes, fingers and major facial structure much better than Meshy remesh 01, but the
surface is visibly faceted and small eye/mouth detail is softened. It has not passed final appearance,
joint topology, rig, grip, materials or runtime validation. This is an experiment, not a ready-to-rig
recommendation. Detail baking is still required and cannot repair missing silhouette or bad deformation.

Next: examine/repair joint topology, finish left-ear jewelry cleanup and validate a provisional rig's
hand grip and arm bending. Finalize UVs and bake/detail textures after topology is settled.
The target budget remains provisional; compare quality and profile multiple actors before acceptance.
