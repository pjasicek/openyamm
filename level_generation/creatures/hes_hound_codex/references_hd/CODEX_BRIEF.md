# Hellhound HD body masters

Built-in image generation, based on the selected original Hesper concept
`design/kreegan_world/concepts/monsters/fam_hellhounds.png`. This new family has no native sprite; the concept and
NEW_FAMILIES section 2 establish identity. Generate one creature per unresized 1536x1024 transparent PNG:
00_three_quarter, 01_front, 02_back, 03_left, 04_right. Three-quarter first; use it unchanged as the primary
identity reference for all four orthographic views. No floor, shadow, text or extra subject.

Tier A ash-grey bare body; remove ALL collar, tether ring and armour because they are separately built rigid
attachments. Keep two short swept-back horns, small pointed ears, long canine jaw, four legs, four clawed toes per
paw, deep narrow chest, tucked waist, thick tapering tail. Tail straight back and clear of legs. Stand square,
four paws on one plane, feet separated, joints slightly flexed; no walk, crouch or action pose. Mouth slightly open
so lower jaw, teeth and warm orange throat are visible; dark reflective non-emissive eyes. Even soft neutral studio
lighting, detailed leathery skin, coherent anatomy. No fur, spikes on body, exposed lava, new accessories or palette
changes. Left view: creature faces image left, its anatomical left flank visible; right view mirrors the camera,
creature faces image right, anatomical right flank visible. Back is directly from behind, not another front.

Gate every image for anatomy, consistent proportions/pose, exactly two horns/four legs/four main toes per paw,
full framing, true alpha and dimensions. Record original paths, SHA-256 and exact prompts in generation_runs.json.
Do not submit any Meshy job until all five pass. Keep original generations if one correction is needed.
