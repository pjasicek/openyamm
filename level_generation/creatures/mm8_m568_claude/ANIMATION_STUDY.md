# Salamander native study and production plan

Authority: 160 original BMPs, all hashes checked against `../mm8_m568/sources.json`; transparent palette index 0. Descriptor IDs 127/128/129 are Fire Newt / Fire Lizard / Salamander, using palettes 569/570/571 and native groups m569/m570/m571. Stored views 0–4 supply the original front-to-back views; 5–7 mirror 3–1. Native one-view death/remains do not supply back-view reconstruction evidence.

Identity: lean spotted bronze-black reptilian upper body and one continuous J-shaped snake body. A single ribbed sagittal head crest, small yellow eyes, short muzzle, exposed scaled arms. Five open hand digits are inferred from the low-resolution held hands. The rear tail projects roughly 55–65% of upright height in the native side view. Generated detail is an interpretation; native scale, action and equipment remain authoritative.

Required equipment: one two-handed black/steel polearm, long central spear tip with short angular side blades/barbs and a long dark shaft. Two bright green wraps in A, ochre in B, red in C. Right hand grips near the head, left hand lower along the shaft. No shield, armour or clothes. Weapon is generated/constructed separately and must remain seated in both hands during two-hand actions. Fidget frees the left hand; death releases the polearm, which remains beside the corpse. Final assembly is required.

Native timing (every frame held 0.125 s): Standing sa held, Walk wa–wh 1.0 s, Attack/Attack2 aa–ah 1.0 s, Hit na–ne 0.625 s, Death na/nb/da/db/dc/dd 0.75 s, Corpse xa held, Fidget fa–fe 0.625 s. Exact ordered records are in `references/native_actions.json`; unreferenced death views are supplementary art, not playback. All tiers cast Fire Bolt; implement a distinct Cast clip and polearm-head glow/release socket. Native MM8 gameplay applies the attack at its own action end; preserve this and author an impact marker near 1.0 s. No gameplay timing edits.

Observed motion: Walk keeps the torso nearly upright while the grounded rear tail swings laterally and the shaft rocks slightly; no leg movement or large torso bounce. Attack begins with the polearm low and transverse, gathers it near the chest, tilts/lifts and drives the head forward and up while the trunk leans back and the tail tip curls for balance, then recovers the diagonal guard. Hit arches backward and lifts both hands/shaft overhead. Death starts with that recoil, releases the shaft, falls backward while curling the snake body and settles as a coherent grounded corpse. Fidget lifts the left fist while holding the weapon upright in the right hand; it does not throw the weapon.
