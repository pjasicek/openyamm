# MM8 Malevolent Boulder staging restoration

All 95 unique native frames are represented, including 42 referenced and 53 unbound files. The three actors share palette 588 and use native scales 1, 1.299988 and 1.699997. No gameplay assets were replaced.

The runtime export uses 2 pixels per logical pixel, native 256×256 logical canvases, exact native pivots, five stored directions and all eight logical views with native mirrors. Attack and death use the native Image1 front-view binding. Death is na0, nb0, da0, dc0, dd0 at 125 ms per frame, followed by the separately bound xa0 corpse. Unbound files receive no invented playback timing. Per-frame glow radius is retained as metadata.

Six RGBA8 atlas pages and matching zero R8 masks occupy 99,271,680 decoded bytes including packing. Palette 588 uses exact base bypass; no second palette artwork is generated. Every page has 16-pixel gutters, three pixels of hidden RGB bleed, and disabled mipmaps. Placement, animations, variant operations, source hashes and palette hashes are recorded in manifest.json and sources.json. Open viewer.html locally to compare the native art and actual runtime atlas.

Raw imagegen sheets, every prompt, rejected rear-view output and accepted identity checkpoint are retained. Identity determines one frozen stone luminance multiplier (0.8512968755624669). All other sheets reuse its raw exposure as their identity reference, then receive that multiplier exactly once. Enhanced-art warm masks and alpha components correct demonstrated orange/dim spell particles. Alpha and geometry remain unchanged during color correction. Fine mineral grain and individual effect filaments are reconstructed interpretations of the native images. Flat bottom cuts already present in native sprites are retained.
