Findings

1

Textures are the largest and cheapest gap

By the texture-pass rule, 47 models are group A (atlas under 25 %, fragments 30 % or more, or heavy stretch) and 35 are group B. The pass has been done on 10 (Bone Dragon, Water Elemental, Troll, the MM7 Mage and peasants), and the user called the Troll "clearly better". Worst still waiting: Merchant (atlas 13.9 %), Roc 12.9 %, Thief 15.6 %, Gorgon 15.6 %, LizardArch 17.2 %, Ratman 22.6 %, the lizardman peasant (49 % of its surface on specks), Druidess, Robot, zcat, Centaur, Thunderbird, Crystal Dragon.

2

Attacks are slower and stiffer than MM9's

47 of 157 melee attacks reach less than 60 % of the MM9 median for their body type (mean of reach, moving share and peak speed), and 20 fall under 45 %: PeasantF1, Merchant, PeasantF3, Guard, zBlasterGuy, Robot, Juggernaut, land Wyrm, Cleric Sun, Warlock, Ogre Mage, Mage, Serpentmen, Efreeti, Pirate Crossbowman, Salamander, Medusa, Water Elemental, Nightmare. In the frames below, MM9's Guard leans and bends the knees and its Dwarf lunges into a crouch. MM6's Guard swings one arm on straight legs and MM8's Ogre Mage barely moves. 17 peasants play Standing or Walking as their attack, because the native peasant attack reuses the walk frames.

3

Robes and skirts tear in the run clips

The gait rollout of 2026-10-31 gave robed and skirted models Jog, Run2 and Sprint clips that the cloth weights do not survive. Worst: PeasantF3's skirt balloons into an egg shape in Sprint and Jog. The Minotaur's kilt opens holes in Jog, Running and Sprint. The Wight's robe pulls into long sheets in Walking, which plays all the time. Also affected: Sorcerer (Jog and Walking), Necromancer m472 Run2, Monk m290, BMonk Run2, Thief Sprint, Cleric Moon, Necromancer m294 and Cleric m460 in Jog.

4

Hit reactions crumple torsos

On about 20 bipeds the Hit clip folds or stretches the chest, neck or shoulders by 18–42 % of the region. The worst are the Necromancer m294, the Dwarf m190 (chest and beard crumple), the Knight (neck tears), the female peasant m342, the dwarf peasant m302 and the Troll m476.

5

Hands

The two you named. The female peasant is the one the user already rejected ("hands are terrible, especially in standing"; the v4 rebuild and arm fixes were reverted). In Hit her left hand also disappears into the skirt (46 % of hand vertices inside the body). The Titan has rigid open hands with no finger bones (README, Discord OY-27). In Cast, the clip its melee plays, the left shoulder and armpit collapse at 1.6–2.05 s and the hand then sinks into the belly.

Elsewhere. The Dragon Hunter m456 walks with its left hand inside its thigh (62 % of hand vertices, in Walking). The Ratman's chest crushes in Running. Rigid hands with no finger bones: Guard, Titan, Thief, Werewolf, Cleric, PeasantF2. Weak at rest: PeasantM2's right hand is a fingerless lump; the Thief has mittens; the goblin has blobby four-finger hands; the lizardman peasant's are spiky. zBlasterGuy, the LizardArch bow hand, and crumpling fists on the Minotaur, Ogre Mage, Dwarf and Archer complete the list. Most other hands read acceptably as geometry at rest.

6

Smaller clip faults

  • Loop hitches: the Vampire Female Peasant's Running jumps ten times its normal frame step at the loop; the Ghost m242's Walking about twice.
  • Attack and Cast are one clip on the Ogre Mage, PeasantM3, LizardArch, the MM7 dragon m182 and the Unicorn.
  • The Unicorn's Hit equals its Standing, so it has no hit reaction.
  • One loop serves stand, walk and run on m182, the land Wyrm, the MM6 Ghost and the Crystal Dragon.
  • Floor: only the Behemoth's attack sinks 17–21 units, already known. Gait: only Fighter Leather (your choice) and Gog (open).
7

Models on legacy routes

  • Seven MM6 models still use the Meshy auto-rig from before the rig-first route: Demon, PeasantM2, Guard, Cleric, Thief, Titan, Werewolf.
  • The lizardman peasant is the last image-to-3D standard body; its attack is Walk played once.
  • Codex installs that went in with failing gates or on QA hold: Lich, Merchant, Minotaur, Knight, Archer, FighterChain, PeasantM3.
  • Still marked placeholder in their READMEs: Werewolf, Skeleton, goblin, Cleric, Druidess.
  • LODs are fine apart from the effect bodies.

What to focus on

  1. Run the texture pass, group A first. It is already queued in texpass_mm678/HANDOFF.md with a proven recipe: 10–20 Meshy credits and about an hour per creature, with no rig work. It brings the largest visible gain for the least risk.
  2. Fix the verified clip and weight faults in place. That means cloth chains in the run clips (11 models), Hit torso folds (about 20 bipeds, many share one Hit clip), the Dragon Hunter's Walking arm clearance, the female peasant's Hit, the two loop hitches and the Titan's Cast shoulder. These are small edits to installed GLBs.
  3. Re-animate the blandest attacks from donors. Use MM9 clips where the anatomy matches and Legends of Might and Magic clips where it has the body. Otherwise choose library clips that move the legs and torso as well as the arm.
  4. Rebuild where mesh, rig and history all fail. Candidates: PeasantF1, the lizardman peasant, Merchant, Thief, Guard, Werewolf, and the Titan's hands (LoMM has a Titan skeleton with StepOn and Wipe attacks).
  5. Pilot the donor route before queueing families. Try one MM9 skeleton (the unbuilt MM9 Wyvern for the MM7/MM8 Wyvern) and one LoMM skeleton (Bat or Harpy).

How it was measured

  • Deformation (motion_audit.py): every bound clip skinned at 30 fps on LOD0. Each triangle is compared with its bind shape (stretch over 1.6, squash under 0.55, or turned inside out against its main bone). Results are grouped by body region from joint names and measured beyond the same region in Standing.
  • Hand contact: hand vertices more than 2 % of the height behind the torso, head or leg surface. Death clips are left out.
  • Attack energy: the mean of reach (p90 vertex displacement), the share of the surface that moves more than 5 % of the height, and peak speed, each against the MM9 median of the same body type.
  • About twenty flagged moments were checked in rendered frames before going into the findings. One false positive, the Reactor's unfolding arms, is now excluded.
  • Not covered: how the paint itself looks (only its layout), faces, held weapons (separate files) and the in-game look. Slow can be intended for a robed caster, and a high percentage on a small region can mean a few large triangles. Read the frames before acting on a number.
