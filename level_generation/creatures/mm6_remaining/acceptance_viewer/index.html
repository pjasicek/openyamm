Queue scope and controls

    Drag restored art or use arrows; Shift+Up/Down = 10 steps; [ / ] steps frames; Space previews animation. Shift+Left/Right changes native animation/view, skipping mirrored copies. Shift+Space aligns head X and fits head top + bottom for this frame, applying whichever action has the required references. One Undo restores both. At 2×, one atlas pixel = ½ original pixel. Fixups share a frame across palettes/views; arrows follow the screen. Drag the top or vertical line on the original; both panels follow. Auto top / Auto center reset them. Guides stay fixed across frames, actions and views; their positions are saved for each family. The vertical line colors only intersected pixels: red original, pink restored, red/pink together for both, green for neither. Pink uses restored alpha ≥ 128. JSON checkpoints are proposals; sprite files stay unchanged.
