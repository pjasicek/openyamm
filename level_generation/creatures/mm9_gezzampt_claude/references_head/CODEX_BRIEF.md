## Content
- FACE: broad, brutal, bestial. Heavy brow ridge in an angry frown, deep clearly formed eye sockets with lids and open eyes, a big broad flat nose with deep nostrils. A closed snarl: the upper lip is drawn back and the teeth are clenched, upper and lower jaw touching, a row of small blunt teeth, and a pair of thick tusks rising from the lower jaw outside the upper lip at each mouth corner, plus shorter upper fangs. The tusks and teeth are distinct clean forms. A complete strong chin.
- BEARD: one solid smooth mass round jaw and chin, a little thicker than the jaw, with only shallow grooves, no strands.
- MANE: one solid carved mass, like carved wood: it starts at the hairline above the brow, rises up and BACK to a tall pointed crest behind the crown (as input 1's side view), then falls behind the head as a broad thick mass to the base of the neck, ending in a clean rounded lower edge with a few shallow notches. Only shallow combed grooves. No separate locks, no thin strands, no gaps, no spikes thinner than a finger.
- HORNS: two short thick ram-like horns, one each side, rooted in the mane above the temple, curving outward and forward. Clear of the mane for most of their length, with shallow ring grooves. Clear air between horn and ear.
- EARS: two large pointed ears standing out level to the sides below the horns, thick enough to sculpt (about 1 cm), with a simple inner hollow.
- NECK: a thick plain cylinder under the jaw, cut flat below. No shoulders.
- No colour, no texture, no fur strands.
