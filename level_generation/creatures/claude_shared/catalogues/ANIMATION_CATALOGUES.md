# Animation catalogues (Mixamo and Meshy)

Generated by `build_animation_catalogues.py` (rerun to refresh). Full data: `mixamo_motions.json` (2484 entries) and `meshy_library.json` (678 clips). Roles are keyword guesses from names and descriptions (MM actor clip roles: idle, walk, run, attack_melee, attack_ranged, cast, hit, death, fidget, fly); always look at the preview GIF before choosing.

## How to use

- **Owned clips first**: the whole Mixamo library (bulk download 2026-10-11), the humanoid set and stored Meshy
  results are classified in `anim_library/index.json` (`claude_shared/anim_library/build_anim_index.py find`); this
  catalogue is for clips not yet owned (Meshy purchases) and for Mixamo preview GIFs.

- **Meshy library** (humanoid/biped only): applies to a Meshy auto-rig (`rigging` job) as an `animation` job, 3 credits per clip, e.g. `{"rig_task_id": ..., "action_ids": [...], "post_process": {"operation_type": "change_fps", "fps": 24}}` (`mm6_gob_claude/requests/anim_lib_v1.json`). Clips play on Meshy's skeleton without retarget loss; they carry the source body's wrist flex and foot posture (humanoid playbook).
- **Mixamo** (humanoid only): free FBX downloads through the user's Adobe account (Claude cannot download; ask the user with the exact clip names, "Without Skin", 24 fps, In Place where offered). Retarget by direction + twist (`mm6_pmn2_claude/scripts/retarget_fbx.py`); an FBX import switches the scene frame rate, reset it to 24. Mixamo kept two-handed grips better than Meshy clips (mage hammer swing). Several Mixamo motions share a name (variants differ in description); the downloaded FBX is named after the description, and the `id` is the unique key. Motion packs (42) bundle clips and have no single duration.
- Quadrupeds, flyers and spiders use procedural clips (quadruped playbook); neither library has non-biped motion.
