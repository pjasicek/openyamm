# MM6 Medusa native motion study

Source: ../mm6_med/native and references/action_contact.png; original tier palettes 769/250/251. Descriptor IDs 574-576.

Observed: one upright female torso over a continuous rear-trailing serpent tail. Two empty hands remain open; no weapon. Living snake crown; gold collar, wrist and left-arm jewelry, tail rings. Bronze/navy/burgundy torso armour changes by tier; belly brightness changes too.

Standing holds an upright pose. Slither: six frames of lateral snake-body sway, small opposite arm swing, mostly stable head height. Attack: six front-facing frames; head/snake crown brightens and raises before release, with restrained chest motion and no punch/held weapon. Hit leans torso backward with both arms raised, then recovers. Fidget: chest/head glance, small arm and tail adjustment. Death begins with backward recoil/arms raised, falls, and ends in a compact crumpled body with snakes and tail spread on ground. Native clips have only one stored view for attacks, hits and death; unseen rotations/depth are inferred.

Chosen route: locally authored hybrid rig following native choreography, serpent centreline and independently articulated head snakes. Local animation library inspected (cast candidates); biped leg clips cannot describe slither or collapse. Arm frames fitted from source B_* rest conventions; hands and finger chains measured on final geometry. No paid humanoid auto-rig. Geometry stress poses and normal/slow clip review precede texturing. All action timing measured in work/native_timing.json. Distinct attack and cast clips if monster spell data requires them.

Inferences: eight main visible snake locks (native overlap hides exact roots), five digits per human-like hand, full 3D tail and corpse depth. No new equipment.
