# Dark Dwarf HD modelling references

Scope: generated modelling reference images only; the parent production workflow owns modelling and installation.
Authority: references_native/standing.png and original m632 frames with native palette 633 applied (the BMP
embedded preview palette contains placeholder colours and must not guide colour). references_native/tiers.png
shows actual palettes 633, 634 and 635. MM6 dwarf is a secondary rig/proportion reference, not the design.

Five full-body images, unresized returns, portrait 1024x1536 and true alpha: 00_three_quarter.png,
01_front.png, 02_back.png, 03_left.png, 04_right.png. One creature per image; same neutral A-pose, open separated
five-finger hands and feet apart. Empty hands: the native crossbow remains a separate required component.
Left profile means the creature's left side is visible and its face points toward image left; right is the reverse.
No floor, shadow or text. Use the primary plus original native sprites for subsequent views.

Preserve short broad build, dark warm brown exposed skin, white chest beard and rear braid, dark iron two-horned
helmet, spiked heavy shoulders, riveted blackened armour, circular belt buckle, silver chain-mail skirt tabs,
green silver-edged bracers and heavy dark boots. Check count of horns and fingers, unclipped margins, colours,
arm/leg gaps, symmetry, unchanged proportions and side/back consistency. Record every generation and selection.

Also prepare four separate close head views for a 1K head texture: front, three-quarter, left profile and back.
