## v2b: abs and shoulder fix (user review 2026-10-21)

Feedback: "just fix the abs (protruded polygon or what idk) and the shoulder(s)"
([feedback/2026-10-21/user_abs_shoulder.png](feedback/2026-10-21/user_abs_shoulder.png): his right side, Standing).
Diagnosis (`scripts/diag_rest.py`, renders in `review/shots/fix_abs_shoulder/diag/`): rest vs posed, Meshy original
(`orig_co`) vs reshaped, reshape-displacement gradient and the upper-arm weight field.
- **Abs: geometry.** A faceted ledge under the pecs and at the flank above the belt is already in the Smart Topology
  mesh (sharpened by the reduction); the Orc Idle's spine flexion folds it into a protruding corner. The hip slimming's
  piecewise-linear ramp just above the belt added a small crease at the flanks.
- **Shoulders: weights + geometry.** The upper-arm weight fell from 1 to 0 over a narrow band across the back of the
  deltoid, which creased with the arm out; the deltoid's lower/back border is a jagged ledge in Meshy's mesh; and the
  v2 arm slimming (`reshape.arm_apply`) had hard edges (a 16 cm radius cutoff and an |x| selection) that left a
  ~2.5 cm step where the deltoid meets the back. Both sides were affected the same way.
Fix (0 credits; topology, UVs, joints, clips, axe, textures and tiers unchanged), applied after the reduction in
`rebuild.sh` and identically to the textured master (`sync_weights.py`: 0.000 mm rest mismatch):
- `fix_reshape.py`: the reshape displacement replaced by a smooth one (`reshape.apply_smooth`: C1 transitions, the
  waist ramp 0.80-1.04 m; `reshape.arm_delta_weighted`: the arm slim scaled by each vertex's own upper-arm/forearm
  weight instead of a radius/selection edge). Displacement-gradient max 3.87 -> 0.93.
- `smooth_fix.py`: Taubin smoothing of the front torso between the pec line and the belt (126 vertices, median
  11 mm) and of the skin within 0.24 m of each shoulder joint (253 vertices, median 6 mm, jaw/neck excluded);
  Laplacian smoothing of the clavicle/upper-arm/twist/spine2/neck weights within 0.26 m of each shoulder joint.
Before/after (`review/shots/fix_abs_shoulder/`): `clay_abs.png`, `clay_shR.png` (the user's angle), `clay_shL.png`,
`clay_shBack.png`, `clay_front.png`, `clay_back.png` and textured viewer pairs `textured_*.png`.
Gates after the fix: G6, soles, hand stretch and the grip solve identical to v2; G4 arm stretch p99 2.16 -> 2.00;
winding 0/0; viewer 24/24, no console errors; proportions 14/17 within 10 % (shoulders +10.5 %, chest +12.2 %,
chest depth +11.2 %; waist -10.0 -> -5.0 %), IoU front 0.772, side 0.46 (`review/model_proportions.json`; v2 in
`review/model_proportions_v2.json`).

## v2c: muscle definition (user review 2026-10-21)

Feedback: "minotaurs muscles need to stand out more ... chest, abs, arms". Diagnosis per layer (measured on the skin
texels; `review/shots/muscle_definition/raw/` for the renders):
- **Base colour (main cause).** The v2 skin repaint (one albedo × Meshy detail above a 6 px blur) kept only paint grain:
  the torso's muscle-scale shading (luminance band 3-24 px / mean) fell from 0.184 in Meshy's paint to 0.059; the HD
  master has 0.231, the native x2 0.277. Meshy's own mid-scale paint is patchy (keeping it at a 40 px scale brought back
  dark blotches that do not follow the muscles), and a hue window left grey Meshy patches unrepainted on the chest.
- **Normal map.** Meshy's painted grain only (tilt median 1.3°, p90 3°), not baked from the high-poly: no muscle forms.
- **AO.** Median 1.0 on the torso: only deep creases.
- **Geometry.** The forms are soft at 10k triangles; a muscle-relief displacement at a safe amplitude (median 1.3 mm)
  changed the lit look very little and was dropped, so the rig, clips, LODs and proportions are unchanged.
Change (textures only, 0 credits; `textures_v2b/` keeps the previous set):
- `hd_shading_bake.py`: the HD masters' muscle shading (01-04: log of linear luminance blurred 0.6 cm over 6 cm,
  centred) projected onto the skin texels: landmark registration (soles and horn tips; horizontal offset by silhouette
  IoU), z-buffer visibility, facing² weights, faded out above the shoulder line (1.36-1.42 m) where the masters' mane
  frames the neck. `compose_m286.py`: skin × exp(0.8 × that).
- `cavity_bake.py`: band-passed geometry convexity plus a muscle-scale top-light term from the mesh, skin × (1 + 0.6 ×
  that).
- Meshy contributes fine grain only (3 px, clip 0.6-1.6, linear-light ratio); the skin is classified by position (every
  body-shell texel above the belt that is not mane/hoof), and the head set's trapezius skin takes the same repaint;
  AO grade 0.4 → 0.55. Skin albedo now spans sRGB 19-135 (p1-p99): the deepest grooves sit below the guideline's ~30,
  a deliberate sprite-like painted shading (the native frames go darker still).
- The region mask and ramps are unchanged (the kilt and wrist-band padding only; tiers checked:
  `review/shots/muscle_definition/tiers_vs_native.png`).
Shots (`review/shots/muscle_definition/`): `chest_abs_arms_front.png`, `_tq.png`, `_side.png` (native x2 crop at the
same scale | v2b | v2c, tier A, Standing frame 0, textured EEVEE close-ups from `closeup_glb.py`), viewer pairs
`viewer_front/quarter/right/left.png`, `tiers_vs_native.png`. Gates unchanged from v2b (geometry and clips untouched):
winding 0/0, viewer 24/24 with no console errors, G6/soles/hand stretch as v2b, G4 p99 2.00.
For review: on the side and back of the upper arms the projection is less exact (the A-pose arms register less well
than the torso) and leaves a few darker streaks.
