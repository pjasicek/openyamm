# Gorgon m564 HD master brief

Generate five unresized transparent 1536×1024 reference views from references/native_standing_views.png.
Original native sprites are the design authority. Selected 00_three_quarter.png is the consistency reference
for 01_front.png, 02_back.png, 03_left.png (nose to image left) and 04_right.png (nose to image right).
Image-generation scope: references_hd only; this brief does not itself submit Meshy jobs.

Identity: compact green plated bovine, small red-eyed head, two pale swept horns, four cloven hooves,
short copper-brown dorsal ridge, thin tail. Armour is sculptural irregular large plates, not tiny scales.
Square planted neutral pose, near-orthographic camera, soft even light, no floor/shadow/text/cropping.
No saddle, added equipment, spikes, extra horns or limbs. Gate identity, palette, counts, proportions,
view consistency, fully visible extremities, alpha and canvas before selecting any view.

Save exact prompts, input/output SHA256 and selection reasons in generation_runs.json; preserve returns.
After all five pass, create selected_views_review.png and record hashes in creature_3d_progress.json.
