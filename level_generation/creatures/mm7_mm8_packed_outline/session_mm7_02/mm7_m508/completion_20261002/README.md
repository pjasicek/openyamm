The five new complete sheets restore walk view 0, attack view 1 and unused death views 2–4. Attachment paths, roles, SHA-256 hashes and recovered historical dispatch evidence are in `review/actual_attachment_provenance.json` and `review/inherited_actual_attachment_provenance.json`.

All poses have checked 2x physical masters, RGBA/R8 atlases, palette 508 and exact fractional placement and native animation metadata. The new attack sheet has about 1.76x actual generated sampling; the full family minimum is 1.711x. These were accepted after review under whole-cycle priority. No sheet was split or regenerated solely to reach 2x. All 20 unused images remain archival art.

Review evidence is indexed in `review/index.html`. Static poses, independent anatomical features, material detail at 100%, soft alpha edges and every palette/mask pose were inspected. Timed playback was not watched. No gameplay installation or shared-file edits.
