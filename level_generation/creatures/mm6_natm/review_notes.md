# Visual review — natm

Reviewed native multi-view/palette/action references, all ten generated sheets, all 59 registered base images and
final mask overlays, standing variants, native/restored walk/attack/collapse comparisons and light/dark contour
patches. The bald muscular man retains the broad feather mantle, bare torso, loincloth, leg guards, right spear
and left buckler. Native equipment rotation, raised-shield recoil, overhead attack and backward collapse remain
recognizable. Unused natmfib0 is retained without changing the native fidget sequence.

The initial olive selector missed a greener side-view loincloth fold. This family's upper hue limit now includes
green while retaining saturation/component checks that protect neutral equipment. The final standing calibration
and all-frame mask contact were rechecked. Cloth panels, armbands, shield paint and spear bindings recolor together;
skin, pale mantle, wooden shaft and silver blade remain distinct. No AI regeneration was needed.

Browser captures show red mirrored walking, grey equipment attack with mask overlay, and the base corpse on a
light background. Logical height, floor placement and prone width agree with native sprites. All 59 frames pass
unclipped extraction, exact crop/atlas reconstruction, base bypass, protected-pixel and alpha invariants. Source,
palette and generation hashes match. Every cleaned/exported/atlas-extracted frame passes the residual-spill scan,
and the magnified contact shows no obvious magenta outline.

Remaining approximations: restored musculature and feathers are more defined, with brighter midtones and softer
near-black shadows than native sprites. Feather layering, small face details, shield wear and hanging cords vary
slightly between poses. Desaturated weathered patches retain some base material color; fine reflected highlights
are inferred artwork rather than recovered native material IDs. Alpha is estimated from the matte. These are
accepted offline limitations, with no claim about measured in-game performance.
