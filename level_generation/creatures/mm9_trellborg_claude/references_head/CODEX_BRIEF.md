# Codex brief: head sculpt for the MM9 Trellborg (mm9_trellborg_claude)

Scope: image generation only. Write ONLY into `level_generation/creatures/mm9_trellborg_claude/references_head/`. No builds, no Meshy. Use your built-in image generation tool.
The image is a modelling reference for a Meshy Smart Topology head model of a fantasy video-game orc-like brute. Geometry only, so it is a sculpture with no colour.

## Inputs (attached, in order)
1. `references_hd/mm9_head_views_a.png`: renders of the ORIGINAL low-poly head, five views: three-quarter, front, figure's left, back, figure's right. Layout authority: face, tusks, ears, hair mass. Grey is background. Faceting is NOT the design.
2. `references_hd/mm9_head_views_c.png`: the same head with another skin. Same geometry.

## Deliverable (true transparent background, no floor, no shadow, no text, unresized return, 1024x1024)
`00_geometry.png`: one matt, plain light-grey clay sculpture of a head and thick neck, front three-quarter from the figure's RIGHT (he looks toward image right and toward the viewer), camera at eye level, near-orthographic, even soft light.
Keep raw returns in `raw/`. Write `generation_runs.json` (id, prompt, inputs with sha256, output path, reason). At most one corrective regeneration.

## Content
- A broad brutal orc-like MALE face: low heavy brow ridge with a deep frown furrow, deep clearly formed eye sockets with heavy upper lids and open eyes, high wide cheekbones, a short wide flat nose with flared nostrils, a long wide upper lip, a closed mouth with firm thick lips set in a slight underbite.
- JAW: a massive square jaw jutting forward past the upper lip, a complete strong chin. Short solid beard mass only on the chin, carved as one smooth wedge, no strands.
- TUSKS: four teeth rising from the lower jaw over the upper lip, two big blunt tusks at the mouth corners about 3 cm tall and two shorter ones between them, clearly separated, thick at the base.
- EARS: pointed, leaning back and out, clear of the head and of the hair, each with a thick helix rim, like input 1.
- HAIR: one solid chunky carved mass, like carved wood: thick swept-back locks from the brow over the top of the head, a low crown, the mass close to the skull at the sides, ending in about eight thick tapered pointed locks behind the ears and at the nape, the longest reaching the base of the neck. The locks are thick, at least 2 cm at the base, and stay joined to the mass. NO thin strands, no gaps through the hair, no flying tips. The hairline is clear on the forehead and in front of the ears.
- NECK: a very thick plain cylinder, cut flat below at the collar. No shoulders, no collar, no straps.
- No colour, no texture, no ear ring.
