Role counts

“Far” means nonwater terrain-cell centres more than 2,048 units from any initially visible sprite anchor. This excludes BModels, monsters and texture detail. It is not a boredom score or a requirement to fill those cells.

Native decoration palette and usage

Frames are fitted independently; use pixel dimensions × frame scale and collision dimensions when selecting sizes. The role “trees” includes species and bare/snow variants with generic native hints.
