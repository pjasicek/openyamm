## Layout and visual reading

A tightly packed multilevel entrance complex leads to an exceptionally large circular chamber. The contrasting scales create a clear focal destination. A single broad cutaway leaves much of the small front complex obscured and needs a second close view.

lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

The captured section shows torches along galleries and at bedroom edges, with beds and storage made from geometry. Lighting reinforces the repeated private rooms and central stair rather than occupying every open floor patch. Inspect both upper galleries and lower passages.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Separate raw face IDs from logical groups used for facet changes. Plan approach, guarded threshold and major chamber together with faction/party state. Include lifecycle restoration of hidden/untouchable geometry and dialogue access.

## Follow-up review

Exercise allowed/denied entry, guard reaction, transformer prerequisites and completion, and house dialogue in relevant states. Verify changed geometry collision as well as appearance, and test save/revisit without replaying rewards or movies unintentionally.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
