## Layout and visual reading

A central hub connects relatively regular wings, corner rooms and stacked passages, with a cave-like extension. The cutaway shows the architectural rhythm but overlays several levels. Invisible faces and secret faces make the loaded state important to the final appearance.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

The initial hall mixes rubble, refuse, chests, banners and a strong modeled fountain/stair composition. These are state-dependent impressions of a damaged castle, not a neutral furnishing kit. Wall art also exists as faces and can appear detached when the study cuts away its supporting wall.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Use stable identities for wings, stairs, house interfaces and hidden passages. Model a house-facing doorway as an event target, not as proof that a walkable interior exists behind it. Explicit room membership is essential where AABBs overlap vertically.

## Follow-up review

Review floor-separated plans and test house access in each relevant quest state, secret detection, stair collision and portal visibility. The texture key trim1_16 resolves from another mounted world's assets in this merged checkout; a new package must declare/validate its dependencies.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
