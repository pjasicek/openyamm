## Layout and visual reading

Regular corridors, tall chambers and repeated room modules form a compact multilevel industrial complex. Visible lava-textured regions give the machinery a spatial purpose. The vertical span is nearly five thousand game units, so a flat connectivity sketch needs elevation annotations.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Torches complement the forge’s lava channels, bridges and dark masonry. The glowing-looking lava material and the torch decoration are separate from actual light records. Keep prop placement away from narrow moving or hazardous crossings, then test the combined lighting in the game.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Author moving pieces separately from the static shell, retaining shared logical mechanism groups. Inspect motion axes and endpoints in the native door records before calling a piece a lift or bridge. Connect hazard surfaces, controls and safe standing areas in the design.

## Follow-up review

Exercise each coupled control from both sides, test missing-key access and state persistence, and check party carrying/collision for vertical movers. Verify the actual damage behavior of hazardous surfaces rather than assuming a lava texture is sufficient.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
