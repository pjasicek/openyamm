## Layout and visual reading

Curved tunnels form loops with many reward pockets and a more regular central complex. The map supports exploration and returning along alternate passages rather than a single corridor chain. Timber/trim and rock materials give small areas identity without needing a different biome each time.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Torches repeat near timber supports and tunnel turns; occasional statue sprites mark side pockets. Keep the support rhythm and clear mine route, then use a special prop to identify a destination. One bright prop at a dead end can do more than evenly scattered clutter.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Make the same semantic vein own all its target faces, state and depleted appearance. Keep environmental interaction rewards separate from chest templates. Use authored side-pocket placement to reward reading the tunnel layout.

## Follow-up review

Test veins before/after use and after save/revisit, both reward and damage outcomes, and each dwarf's intended state transition. Check loops for actor pathing and portal culling. trim9_32/trim9_32a resolve through the merged mounted-world texture set.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
