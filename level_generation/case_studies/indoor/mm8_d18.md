## Layout and visual reading

A mostly linear progression links polygonal rooms, bent passages and stairs. Repeated chamber shapes create a recognizable rhythm while the turns hide upcoming spaces. Fourteen occupied sectors make this a useful next step after a small cave.

lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Twenty dec56 wall torches form the entire visible decoration-sprite palette. Modeled containers, tables and other fixtures still furnish the rooms. Use corners and passage approaches for light rhythm; keep wall, floor and trim textures continuous across ordinary connected rooms unless their purpose changes.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Add controlled ambush rooms and trap lanes to an otherwise readable route. Specify projectile origin/direction, activation policy and safe standing area in the manifest; a decorative launcher mesh does not provide spell logic.

## Follow-up review

Test each paired door, trap activation/repetition, projectile collision and safe recovery. Verify the two bound chest slots and any secret faces. Explicit lights here have nonzero 384-unit radii, useful for a small-room placement comparison in engine.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
