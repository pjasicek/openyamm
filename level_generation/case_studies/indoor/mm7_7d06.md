## Layout and visual reading

Winding caves and branching pockets lead toward an ordered temple with a circular focal chamber and rectangular side rooms. The contrast between the rough approach and built shrine is stronger than a single material or room shape.

lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Violet torch sprites repeat along built passage walls and room corners. Modeled tables, shelves and pillars provide the furnishings. The adjoining natural corridor has a different treatment, so a single global decoration scatter would blur the distinction.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Design the shrine as a landmark reached through a natural route. Preserve distinct cave and masonry surface roles. Use this as a manageable hybrid reference before attempting large castles or heavily stacked puzzle maps.

## Follow-up review

Review the cave-to-temple transition at eye height, test all grouped door endpoints and low/high Perception on intended secrets, and verify chest approach/return routes. The four explicit imported lights have zero radius, so validate lighting in the engine.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
