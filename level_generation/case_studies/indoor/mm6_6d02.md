## Layout and visual reading

A long bent sequence of irregular chambers and connectors spans a large elevation range, with branches and a small architectural section at one end. The route changes width repeatedly. A chain graph with carefully chosen side pockets captures its progression better than uniformly scattered rooms.

lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Standing braziers follow the tunnel edges and bends; the central walking strip remains readable. Some additional props and rock sprites occur elsewhere in the native inventory. On sloped cave walls, sample the actual floor and inspect the full sprite silhouette instead of placing every light at a fixed world Z.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Use room purpose and sightline changes to pace a long dungeon. Keep the organic tunnel cross-sections varied while preserving walkability. Import and review scene overrides as part of runtime state rather than copying only static geometry.

## Follow-up review

Walk the complete route in both directions, verify slopes/steps at narrow joins, and test cage rewards before/after their state change and after save/load. Capture separate close views for the distant architectural section and cave branches.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
