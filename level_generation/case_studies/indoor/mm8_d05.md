## Layout and visual reading

Large chambers bookend a long sequence of built spaces, including a dense cluster of small puzzle rooms. The overall map is modest compared with VARN, but its local mechanism density is high. It is not an appropriate 'simple starter dungeon' compiler test.

The plan shows source geometry/terrain and selected bound POIs. The Blender view uses neutral study
lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Apply to a new map

Represent control-to-mechanism dependencies, face flags and visual state explicitly before modeling each module. Do not equate one button with one door. Reusable puzzle-room geometry still needs unique semantic/event/state identities.

## Follow-up review

Test correct/wrong button sequences, resets, repeated plate contact, monster/object activation policies, spell trajectories and secret detection. Validate collision and restored light/texture states after save/revisit. Review a close cutaway of the dense puzzle cluster.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
