## Layout and visual reading

A small low-poly cavern has two connected sectors and a broad irregular interior. Angular upper wall bands remain visible after ceiling removal, producing apparently floating strips in some cutaway angles. Those review artifacts should not be mistaken for the complete player's view.

The plan shows source geometry/terrain and selected bound POIs. The Blender view uses neutral study
lighting, excludes indoor ceilings and omits actors/decorations and scripted state. Outdoor water/shore
transparency is not composited in Blender; magenta edges are a preview limitation. Neither image is a game screenshot.

## Decoration, architecture and terrain study

Bones cluster close to the entry-side edge of an otherwise open dragon chamber. This sparse placement reads as evidence of occupation and leaves the main creature space clear. Native sprite count is only six; adding many torches or crates would change this reference’s character.

This reading is an interpretation of the measured arrangement, not a claim about the original authors’ documented intent.

## Apply to a new map

Use this to test a single portal between a pair of organic spaces, texture repetition and large-creature clearance. Pair it with d48 when adding a house or chest, since this reference does not demonstrate those bindings.

## Follow-up review

Verify portal traversal/culling and inspect two opposite cutaways plus complete-ceiling eye views. Determine actual in-game illumination from ambient/decor/party sources; do not accept the neutral Blender sun as evidence.

Scope runtime checks to new mechanisms or unresolved behavior using the [review policy](../../REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md).

These are review suggestions, not gameplay tests executed by this survey.
