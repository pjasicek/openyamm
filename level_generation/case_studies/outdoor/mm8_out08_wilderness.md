# Ravage Roaming — focused wilderness contrast

This follow-up supplements the 33-map main survey. It does not claim a complete event/geometry walkthrough.
Use it with [wilderness calibration](../../WILDERNESS_CALIBRATION.md) when a grassland brief needs a more open palette
than Ravenshore or the dense woodland of Murmurwoods.

- [Native encounter/scenery plan](../../evidence/wilderness_calibration/out08.png)
- [Decoration inventory and provenance](../../evidence/mm8/out08_decorations.json)
- [Spatial measurements](../../evidence/wilderness_calibration/measurements.json)

The initially visible native decorations include **166 trees, 85 rocks, 8 lights and 7 other props**, with **no low-plant
sprites** in this inventory. This is not proof of an empty-looking surface: terrain art and BModel detail contribute.
Ravenshore has 271 trees and 269 low plants, much of the latter close to BModels. Do not transfer that garden layer
across the entire Ravage Roaming countryside.

In four fixed coordinate windows, tree nearest-neighbor medians range from approximately 1,939 to 2,554 native units.
These are coordinate observations, not matched semantic districts or density normalized to traversable land. Inspect
water, slopes and building footprints in the plan before comparing them with another map's woodland or town.

Generation lesson: a green terrain palette can support open grassland, scattered tree groups, rocky shoulders and long
bare stretches. Choose coverage from the intended district and biome. The goal is coherent, purposeful scenery, not
maximal foliage or a universal minimum number of plants.

The encounter plot shows native **spawn markers**, separately from fixed actor records. Resolve marker count ranges,
conditional actors and map events before turning it into a combat headcount. The detailed population accounting and
entrance-guarding comparison in this follow-up use Ravenshore; they are not automatically facts about Ravage Roaming.
