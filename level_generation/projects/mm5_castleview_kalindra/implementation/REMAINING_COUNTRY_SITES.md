## Reference and review sequence

Actually inspected Garrote [tent part 7](../../../catalogues/mm8_garrote_gorge/models/007.md) and its native render.
Its measured 992×624×432 form uses `t06a10`, `t06c11` and `GNecClg2`; the canvas wings and timber framing provide
a reference vocabulary for new temporary shelters. Verify the individual bitmap roles and native scaling before
adding them to the MM5 palette. The reference has no direct house destination and supplies no resident mapping.
Do not copy its mesh or treat the unusual overlapping cloth arrangement as the required new structure.

Follow [outdoor decoration calibration](../../../OUTDOOR_DECORATION_CALIBRATION.md): first establish the principal
form and clear approach, then add a small purposeful group. Camps use supplies/activity clues; sanctuaries use
natural framing; mine/drain entrances use construction and bank context. Keep intentional clearings and do not
repeat the same flower/rock ring at every destination.

Build/review these as separate understandable groups: temporary human sites; mine/drain entrances; natural
clearings/descent/rope sites. For each, retain source occupancy and future routes, author new geometry, inspect
renders, export native geometry, and verify representative real clicks and ordinary access. Resolve the unsupported
river-bank outfall and sloping sanctuary explicitly before assigning final pad heights or reporting them complete.
