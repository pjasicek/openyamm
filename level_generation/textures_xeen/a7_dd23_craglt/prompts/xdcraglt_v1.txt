The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Darkside of Xeen in Might and Magic's style: D4's high snowy crags, seen from a gargoyle lair's cave mouth high on the mountain under the Darkside's dim amber-grey sky (two reference pictures are game screenshots of D4, for the sky's colours, the haze and the snow; the third is the lair's dark brown-black cave rock).
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
