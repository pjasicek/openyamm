Asset: a flat repeating surface albedo texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Pirate Outpost of Might and Magic VIII: dark reddish-brown hand-painted wooden planks, carved panelling and braced plank doors, warm and slightly stylised; and the sewer and cellar of Might and Magic VI: red brick, mossy grey-brown block stone and dark old planks, lit green by slime.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: a seamless repeating texture that fills the whole 1024x1536 (portrait) canvas edge to edge, features spread
evenly with nothing centred, no border, no frame, no darker or lighter edges. Shelves of Joe's stock seen straight on, repeating: four rows of dark old plank shelves, each holding a row of stoppered glass bottles and squat jars of bright green tonic and grey-green powder with small paper labels, corks and twine, the dark wall behind. Every shelf row the same height, the shelves running off both side edges.
