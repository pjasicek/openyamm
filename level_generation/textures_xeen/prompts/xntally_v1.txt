Asset: a flat single-picture surface texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: Snergle's Caverns, the dwarves' mine of Might and Magic VI: red and dark-brown rough-hewn rock, blue-grey cave stone, dark timber posts and plank lagging, dwarf-dressed sandstone blocks.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1536x1024 (landscape) canvas edge to edge. A slab of dressed sandstone seen straight on, filling the whole canvas, the same stone and colour as the first reference, covered with rows of chalked tally marks in white chalk: groups of four strokes crossed by a fifth, in neat rows, with a column of simple angular dwarf numerals and rune-like marks down the left, some rubbed out and written over. Chalk only, no letters of any real alphabet.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
