Asset: a painted sign texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Pirate Outpost of Might and Magic VIII: dark reddish-brown hand-painted wooden planks, carved panelling and braced plank doors, warm and slightly stylised.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition (Reference 1 is the layout guide: the board covers exactly its flat brown rectangle, at the same position,
size and proportions; the grey around it stays plain grey): on a perfectly flat plain mid-grey (#808080) background, one flat rectangular board seen exactly
straight on, its outline perfectly straight and axis-aligned, centred, 160 wide by 128 tall (aspect 1.25 to 1),
filling about 92 percent of the canvas height. Nothing else on the grey: no shadow, no wall, no nails outside the board.
The board: Joe's notice board: a small board of dark reddish-brown planks like the references with a plank frame, and pinned to it with nails one large sheet of yellowed paper, hand-lettered in black ink: the heading "JOE THE EXTERMINATOR", below it "PESTS GONE OR YOUR MONEY BACK!" and below that, much smaller, "(money not back)". Two small scraps of paper pinned at the corners, one with a few illegible scribbled lines.
