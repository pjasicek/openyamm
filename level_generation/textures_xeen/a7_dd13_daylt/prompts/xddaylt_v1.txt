Asset: a flat single-picture surface texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Darkside of Xeen in Might and Magic's style: D2's Ogre Pass, a dirt road over green downs between dark forest and grey rock, seen in the game under the Darkside's dim amber-grey sky (the reference pictures are game screenshots of this pass, for its colours and light).
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1536x1024 (landscape) canvas edge to edge. The view out of a dark gateway onto a mountain pass at eye level, filling the whole canvas: a beaten red-brown dirt road running away from the viewer and bending out of sight, on its left the edge of a dark forest of dense, almost black-green trees, on its right grey rock knolls and boulders, green grassy downs behind them, far hazy brown mountains low on the horizon, and above it all a dim overcast sky of amber-grey and dull ochre clouds, exactly the colours and the soft, dim light of the two references (the same pass in the game). The light is that of a failing sun: dim, muted, slightly hazy, no bright sunlight, no blue sky, no sun disc, no strong shadows. The horizon about one third down from the top. No palisade, no logs, no fort, no people, no frame or dark edges.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
