The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: Castle Gryphonheart of Might and Magic VII and the Castle of Air of Might and Magic VIII: dark grey cobble and dressed stone, black iron, woven heraldic hangings, a grim knightly order's tower.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge. A square dark grey stone wall panel seen straight on, filling the whole canvas, like the references' stone, with a large stone face of a knight carved in relief out of the wall: a closed great helm's shape with the visor raised, and under it a stern bearded face with its eyes shut and its mouth firmly closed. Symmetric and centred, weathered, dust in the carving. No letters.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
