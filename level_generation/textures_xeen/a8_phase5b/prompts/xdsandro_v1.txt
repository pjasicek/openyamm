Asset: a flat single-picture surface texture for Might and Magic VI-VIII (a 1990s first-person fantasy RPG), in the remaster's high-resolution art.
The reference images are finished textures of the same place in the game: the Tomb of Ethric the Mad of Might and Magic VI: dark grey-green carved crypt stone, bone and skull niches, cold dim light, a lich's crypt over a sewer.
Paint the new texture as if by the same artists for the same set: the same palette, saturation and contrast, the same
scale of detail (one texel is about one centimetre), the same hand-painted, slightly stylised realism (painted, not a
photograph), the same soft even lighting. Flat orthographic front view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no
vignette, no baked highlights, glare or cast shadows, no watermark or signature.
Composition: full bleed, the subject fills the whole 1024x1024 (square) canvas edge to edge. A square carved stone seal panel seen straight on, filling the whole canvas: a slab of the references' dark grey-green crypt stone with a plain raised square rim, and carved deep in relief in its middle a human heart pierced right through by a long bone laid diagonally, the heart and bone in pale bone colour against the darker stone, faint dark grooves, cold and old, a little chipped. Symmetric framing, centred. No letters, no words, no skulls, nothing else.
Every edge row and column is the material itself: no dark outline, frame line or vignette along the canvas edges.
