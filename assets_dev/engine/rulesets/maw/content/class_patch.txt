Seraphim	Class description	Seraphim are holy warriors who fight with sword and spear and draw on Light, Spirit, Mind and Body magic. Mind adds holy damage to their blows, every melee hit heals the most wounded companion by their Body skill, Light speeds their attacks and lets them wield two-handed swords in one hand beside a shield, and Spirit turns aside part of every hit. Once every five minutes Divine Protection saves a Seraphim from a killing blow. Seraphim never wield two swords.
Angel	Class description	Angel is the first Seraphim promotion: more hit points and spell points per level, and Master ranks in Spirit, Mind and Body magic.
Archangel	Class description	Archangel is the second and last Seraphim promotion: the most hit points and spell points of the line, and Grandmaster ranks in Sword, Spear and Light magic.
Death Knight	Class description	Death Knights are armoured warriors of Frost, Blood and Unholy magic. Their spell points are a small fixed pool that their own melee hits refill, and their spells are blows of the weapon in hand. Frost and Unholy add damage to every swing and Frost speeds it, Blood shrugs off physical damage and drains life, and Unholy wards against magic. Their auras reserve no mana, and spell books cannot teach them.
Death Lord	Class description	Death Lord is the first Death Knight promotion: more hit points, a larger spell point pool and Master ranks in Frost, Blood and Unholy.
Lich King	Class description	Lich King is the second and last Death Knight promotion: the most hit points and spell points of the line, and Grandmaster ranks in Sword, Axe, Plate and their three schools.
Shaman	Class description	Shamans are warriors who use all seven schools of magic (Fire, Air, Water, Earth, Spirit, Mind and Body) to strengthen their weapon. Fire burns away part of the target's health, Earth and Spirit add melee damage, Mind and Body restore spell points and hit points with each blow, and Air and Water reduce the damage they take. Every school point also makes their spells and heals stronger.
Sekhem	Class description	Sekhem is the first Shaman promotion: more hit points per level and Master ranks in every school.
Marabou	Class description	Marabou is the second and last Shaman promotion: the most hit points of the line and Grandmaster ranks in every school.
Elementalist	Class description	Elementalists master Fire, Air, Water and Earth by using them: every hit of their own spells on a hostile monster advances that school, and new spells of each element open as it grows. Each offensive cast builds a stack that makes the next spells stronger and faster; stacks fade when they stop casting, and a melee or bow hit clears them. Their combined elemental skill is their Ascension. Spell books cannot teach them.
Elemental Adept	Class description	Elemental Adept is the first Elementalist promotion: more spell points per level.
Master of Elements	Class description	Master of Elements is the second and last Elementalist promotion: the most spell points of the line and Grandmaster ranks in all four elements.
