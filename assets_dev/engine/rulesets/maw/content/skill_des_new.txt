Cover	The character steps in front of blows aimed at companions. Every point adds 1% to a base 10% chance to take the hit instead, up to 40% at 30 points; of the companions whose roll succeeds, the one with the most hit points takes it. Alone, a successful cover halves the blow. Mastery comes by itself at 6, 12 and 20 points (8, 20 and 30 on Insanity and Madness), and reaching 30 points teaches Retaliation. The gem beside its name on the skills screen (or P) turns it on or off.	Covers physical attacks	Also covers elemental attacks	Also covers spells	Hitting a monster raises the next cover chance by 15%
ManaShield	While it is on, mana soaks up part of every hit the character takes. Each spell point absorbs 1 + skill^1.4 / 60 damage, 5 at 50 points, where the skill stops. Mastery comes by itself at 6, 12 and 20 points (8, 20 and 32 on Insanity and Madness). The gem beside its name on the skills screen (or M) turns it on or off.	Absorbs 25% of each hit	Absorbs 50% of each hit	Absorbs 75% of each hit	Absorbs all of each hit while mana lasts
Enlightenment	Deepens the character's mana and lightens the mana that buff auras hold. Mastery comes by itself at 6, 12 and 20 points.	Mana +0.5% per point; auras reserve as if the class had 0.5 more mana per level	Mana +1% per point; auras reserve as if the class had 1 more mana per level	Mana +1.5% per point; auras reserve as if the class had 1.5 more mana per level	Mana +2% per point; auras reserve as if the class had 2 more mana per level
Retaliation	After a successful cover, every point gives a 1% chance to store a counterattack for 10 seconds. The character's next hit spends it: extra damage that grows with the skill, melee power and vitality, a 25% chance per stored counterattack to stun the monster for 2 seconds (1 second on bosses), and 30% less recovery per stored counterattack. Learned when Cover reaches 30 points; mastery comes by itself at 10, 24 and 40 points, and the skill stops at 50.	Stores one counterattack	Stores one counterattack	Stores one counterattack	Stores up to three counterattacks
