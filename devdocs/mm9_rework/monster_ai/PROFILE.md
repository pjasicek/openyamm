# MM9 AI profile and placement fields

Status: phase 1 of [the plan](../MM9_MONSTER_AI_PLAN.md) (data), 2026-11-04. Every MM9 monster row has a profile and
every placed MM9 AI actor its placement fields; both reach the AI facts. Since phase 2 (2026-11-04) the walkers
(crawler, skirmisher, ranged holder) read them: `attack_reach`, `melee_sweep`, `alert_radius`, `wince`, `ranged`,
`throttle`, `always_evade`, and the placement's `sight`, `hearing`, `runaway_chance`, `ranged_type` and
`hiding_places` (the plan's "Phase 2"). Since phase 3 the fliers, swimmers and turrets (but the Dragon King) read them
too, with `ranged: flier`, `melee_window_seconds` (it marks a Banshee) and `pod_launch`; `pod_cooldown_min/max` stay
unused, because lobber.scr's own wait never runs (the plan's "Phase 3"). Since phase 4a every loop archetype reads the
placement's `wander`, `path`, `path_random`, `wander_leash`, `wander_wait`, `wander_time`, `hiding_places` (now only
those out of the target's sight count) and `can_be_resurrected`, and the row's `alert_radius` (alerts), `resurrecter`,
`cower`, `raise_seconds` and `resurrect_spell_seconds`/`resurrect_key_seconds` (the plan's "Phase 4a"). MM6-MM8
monsters carry neither.

Research behind every field: [MM9 data and scripts](MM9_AI_DATA_AND_SCRIPTS.md) (columns, base scripts, §4.1 family
hooks, §4.2 placement patterns) and [MM9 native RE](MM9_AI_NATIVE_RE.md) (classes, the AttackReach/AttackRange swap,
wince, perception defaults).

**Units.** Distances and speeds are ours: MM9's LithTech units × 2.56 (`tools/mm9_import_discovery/mm9_units.py`, as
the table generator's speeds and the converters' positions). Times are seconds. Points are the map's native frame.

## Data flow

| Step | Row profile | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generated by | `tools/mm9_import_discovery/generate_mm9_monster_modifiers.py` (MONSTERS.txt, the default script's include chain, the class table, model dims) | `tools/mm9_import_discovery/mm9_ai_placement.py` `placement_ai()` from the DAT object's properties and `ScriptName`/`ScriptParams`; called by the BLV converters (`mm9r_common/dungeon/content.py` `Content.actor`) and the ODM transcoder (`transcode_mm9_dat_to_odm.py`) |
| File | `worlds/mm9/modifiers.yml`, modifier `mm9_ai` | scene actor map `mm9_ai` (`<map>.scene.yml`) |
| Parsed by | `game/tables/MonsterModifiers.cpp` (catalogue, ranges, consistency) → `Mm9AiProfile` | `parseMm9AiPlacementNode` (`game/tables/Mm9MonsterAi.cpp`) from `IndoorSceneYml.cpp` / `OutdoorSceneYml.cpp` → `MapDeltaActor::mm9Ai` (saved from save version 147) |
| Runtime | `ActorBehaviorCatalog::mm9AiProfileForMonster` (kept apart from the tactics: a profile alone gives no tactics) | both world runtimes copy `mm9Ai->settings` into `ActorMovementFacts::mm9Placement` |
| AI facts | `ActorTacticsFacts::mm9Profile` = `placedMm9AiProfile(row, placement)` (`ActorBehaviorRuntime::applyMm9AiProfile`, every step, MM9 monsters only) | `ActorMovementFacts::mm9Placement`; MM9's defaults for an actor with a profile but no record (a summon, an old save) |

The vectors and strings of a placement (path and summon points, summon class, script and its params) stay in
`MapDeltaActor::mm9Ai` for the world to resolve (as `patrolPoints` → `patrolTarget` today); the facts carry scalars.

**Placement over row:** `placedMm9AiProfile` applies the fields both name: first the placement script's `profile`
(below: MM9 runs the placed `ScriptName`, not the row's), then a placed dormant start replaces the row's, and a placed
`ranged_type: 2` turns a crawler or skirmisher whose script has `Range.inc` (`ranged: walker`, the placement script's
when it has one) into a ranged holder (MM9's Range.inc reads the placement's `RangeAttackType`; without Range.inc it is
ignored, and the Evil Sorcerers are holders whatever their placement says). Both runtimes' AI facts carry the placed
profile; indoors the world's own reads (refuges, guards, calls, dormant clips) use it too
(`IndoorWorldRuntime::mm9ActorProfile`).

## Row profile (`mm9_ai` modifier)

| Field | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `archetype` | native class + default script include chain | see the rules below |
| `movement`: walk, fly, swim | native class | `AISwimFly` rows fly (or swim for swimmers and the Fish), whatever `Fly` says |
| `ranged`: none, walker, flier | include chain: `Range.inc` → walker, `FlyRange.inc` → flier | `lobberpod.scr` none (its range attack is switched off). A row with nothing to shoot keeps `walker`, as MM9 does: `CanRangeAttack` is false at run time. The Ghoul (`baserange`) does shoot: its `concat` row casts its second spell, Disease (native RE section 7.2.2) |
| `attack_reach` | `AttackReach` (column U) + 1.25 × (√(x²+z²) − z) of its model's half extents × `Scale`, × 2.56 | MM9's `AttackRange` **stat** (`0x1000dd40`): the distance it decides to attack at. The column names are swapped in MM9 |
| `melee_sweep` | `AttackRange` (column V) × `Scale` × 2.56 | the melee hit's sweep beyond the attacker's radius (plus the target's radius) |
| `alert_radius` | `AlertRadius` × `Scale` × 2.56 | `SendAlert` radius (default 3 072 = 1 200 LT) |
| `walk_speed`, `run_speed`, `fly_speed`, `lunge_velocity` | the velocity columns × 2.56 | written when not 0; MM9 applies twice `lunge_velocity` × the `lunge` key's factor |
| `wince`: low_health, never | `Always Wince?` = 1 → never | **MM9 quirk reproduced:** the column reads as its opposite. Otherwise MM9 rolls 20-50 % once its HP ≤ 20 %; `WinceThreshold`/`WinceTrackTime` are never read |
| `resurrecter` | `Resurrect` = 1 | it raises dead friends (16 rows) |
| `head_turn: false` | `HeadTurn` ≠ 1 | its model's look turns toward nothing (51 rows: the beasts, fliers and other non-humanoids; [ambient animation](MM9_AMBIENT_ANIMATION.md) §2.4); every MM9 row's look turns only toward the target its AI holds or the party talking to it |
| `cower` | its MM9 model (`ModelName`) has a `Cower` animation | BaseRun.inc's CanCower (phase 4a): it may cower instead of fleeing |
| `raise_seconds` | the length of its MM9 model's `Resurrect` animation | AICommon.inc's CanBeResurrected needs one (with the placement's `can_be_resurrected`); the raised monster's clip |
| `resurrect_spell_seconds`, `resurrect_key_seconds` | its MM9 model's `ResurrectSpell` animation and its `DoResurrection` key | a resurrecter raises others only with one (phase 4a assumption); the corpse rises at the key |
| `boss` | `dragon.scr`, `yanmirbase.scr` | a Lua behaviour (phase 5: `worlds/mm9/behaviors/`) |

| `throttle: false` | `dagrell.scr` | BaseMelee without SpeedThrottle |
| `always_evade`, `evade_min_seconds`, `evade_max_seconds` | `lobberpod.scr` (1.8-2.2 s) | evades after every attack, for a seeded 1.8-2.2 s (LOBBERPOD.scr DoEvade `GetRandomFloat 1.8, 2.2`); the table generator also drops the pods' projectiles (they only melee: `g_bIsRangeAttacker` is never set, MM9_AI_NATIVE_RE.md section 7.2.3) |
| `ranged_weapon` | an `AttachProp` in the script chain whose model `WEAPON.txt` lists as a Bow, CrossBow, Throw, Wand, Staff or Scroll | its `Attack` in reach becomes `RangeAttack` (section 7.2.3); without one a monster in reach always strikes. **No MM9 row has one** (the scripts attach hammers, swords, hoes and pamphlets; no placement's `Weapon` property is set) |
| `pod_launch`, `pod_cooldown_min/max` | `lobber.scr` (5-10 s) | Lobber → Lobber Pod (9084), Ice Lobber → Ice Lobbercicle (9105): the family's own pod row (assumed; the clip spawns natively) |
| `melee_window_seconds` | `banshee.scr` (5 s) | stat `Undead` during each melee pass |
| `spawn_in` | `greaterdemon.scr` | MM9 runs it only for spawns with p0 = 1 (the 1000 Terrors spawners); placed demons never |
| `death_spawn` | `lesserdemon.scr` → 9242 | likewise p0 = 1 only |
| `dormant_until`, `dormant_pose` | `FlyingIcky.scr` → disturbed, hang | not the Airborne Icky (row 87) |
| `guard_refuge` | `NPCBase.inc` in the chain | a fleeing townsperson runs to a town guard (phase 4b) |
| `wake_seconds`, `dormant_fidget_seconds` | its MM9 model's `Launch`, `HangFidget` | hanging rows only (phase 4b) |
| `spawn_seconds`, `aware_seconds` | its MM9 model's `Spawn`, `Aware` (else `Taunt`) | spawn-in rows only (phase 4b) |

Beside the profile the generator writes the `reposition` modifier (an OpenYAMM addition, not MM9;
[MONSTER_MODIFIERS.md](../../MONSTER_MODIFIERS.md), the plan's "Repositioning casters") for rows 156-158, the
`EvilSorcerer.scr` ranged holders, with its defaults. It is a row modifier, not a profile or placement field: a
placement's `ranged_type: 2` makes a holder without it (MM9's holder), and a placement script that resolves to another
archetype leaves it unused.

Not written (and not read by MM9's AI): `Fly`, `Move`, `Target Pref`, `ConcatAttk`, `Hostility Group`, `Special`,

`skirmisher` modifier, generated as before for every row whose script includes `BaseMelee.inc` (it keeps working
unchanged; the profile does not repeat it).

## Placement scripts: the placement's `ScriptName` wins

MM9 runs the script a placement names, not its row's default (the row's `ScriptName` stat is only read by scripts that
hand over to it). The profile fields a script decides (the archetype, the ranged add-on, the family hooks and BaseMelee's
footwork) therefore come from the placement's script; the rest of the profile (reach, sweep, alert radius, speeds,
wince, resurrecter, model clips, spell recoveries, pods, boss rows) stays the row's.

**Generator.** `mm9_ai_placement.placement_ai(..., row)` (the BLV builder's `Content.actor`, with the placed object's
own MONSTERS.txt row by its class, as the ODM transcoder resolves it, also for an NPC placed on a stand-in engine row;
the ODM transcoder with its replacement's row) writes `profile` from
`generate_mm9_monster_modifiers.placement_profile`, which resolves the placement script with the row generator's code
(`script_chain`, `classify`, `script_traits`) and compares it with the row's default script:

1. `effective_script`: a script that **starts a fight loop** is its own: its chain has BaseCrawl, BaseMelee, BaseFly,
   BaseSwim, PoliceMan or Base.inc, and a file of its own (the script or a hostility include, not the base library
   that defines the init) calls `BaseInit`, `BaseCrawlInit`, `BaseFlyInit` or `BaseSwimInit` (`gosub` or `goto`), or
   it is a turret script. A base include alone only brings the labels (NPC109.scr fights, Svenarena.scr only walks).
2. Otherwise a script that **hands over** (`RunScript`) runs the script it hands to: the row's own when it reads the
   `ScriptName` stat (AutoResurrect.scr, Jumper.inc, Thorgard_Actor.scr, IsleOfAshesActor.scr: no override), else the
   first literal name (CoffinMummy.scr, KlusosSleeper.scr, KlusosHidden.scr: baseMelee.scr; YanmirHidden.scr: titan.scr;
   WizardMaster.scr: EvilSorcerer.scr).
3. Otherwise a script whose own file starts a loop that **only moves** (NPCBaseInit, BaseWanderInit, BaseRunInit, the
   farm animals' inits) is a civilian (no ranged add-on, no footwork, `guard_refuge` only with NPCBase.inc).
4. Otherwise the script **runs no AI** (dialogue, shop, set-piece and cut-scene scripts): `profile: {passive: true}`.
   A boss placement (its Lua behaviour stands in for its script, `BOSS_SCRIPTS`) gets no `passive`.

The override lists only the fields that differ: `archetype`, `ranged`, `ranged_weapon`, `guard_refuge`, `throttle`,
`always_evade`, `evade_min_seconds`, `evade_max_seconds`, `melee_window_seconds`, `spawn_in`, `death_spawn`, `passive`,
and `skirmisher` (`false` drops the row's footwork; `{strafe_attack_chance, evade_chance, taunt_seconds}` from the row's
columns and its model's taunt gives it to a script that adds BaseMelee). The row's `movement` (its native class)
stays.

**Runtime.** `parseMm9AiPlacementNode` reads `profile` into `Mm9AiPlacement::profile` (`Mm9AiPlacementProfile`, saved
from version 149; an older save's placements are marked `fromScene` and take it from the map's scene, same index, same
source object and script, when the map is next entered: `refreshSavedLegacyMm9PlacementProfiles`).
`placedMm9AiProfile` applies it; `ActorBehaviorRuntime::applyMm9AiProfile` applies the footwork to the tactics.

**A script with no AI (Sturmford's prison guard).** Sturmguard.scr includes only `globals.inc`: its `Main` registers
`Use` (gives a key, `DoRude 425`) and the dialogue's exit handler (the jail door, Hatlati's arrest). No base loop, no
`OnDamage`, no target, no hostility rule: in MM9 the guard stands, talks and can be killed without fighting back or
fleeing. `passive` keeps his row's loop and class (a guard: townsfolk still run to him as to any GuardBase, his relation
to monsters stays the guards'), but he never takes a target, answers no hit or call (no police count, no help) and never
attacks; a placed wander, a dormant start and the native wince still run. The civilian archetype would be wrong: it
flees when hit (NPCBase.inc's OnDamage, which Sturmguard.scr does not have).

**Conversion status (2026-11-04).** The generator change is in the shared converter code (`mm9_ai_placement.py`,
`generate_mm9_monster_modifiers.py`, `mm9r_common/dungeon/content.py`, the ODM transcoder): **every map gets its

City, Guberland, Guberland City, Hall of the Gods, Inventa Storca, Lich Lab, Lindisfarne, Lindisfarne Monastery,

## MM9 quirks the data reproduces

- `Always Wince? = 1` means never wince (`wince: never`).
- The `AttackReach` and `AttackRange` columns do the reverse of their names (`attack_reach`, `melee_sweep`).
- Fliers and swimmers by class, not by `Fly` (Banshees fly with `Fly 0`; Jellyspores swim with `Fly 1`).
- The Lobber Pod's range attack is switched off (`ranged: none`); the Ghouls' `baserange` has nothing to shoot.
- Only spawned demons (p0 = 1) spawn in or split on death.
- Sight and hearing double after first contact (phase 2 applies it); only placed runners (`runaway_chance`) can flee.

## Factions (`worlds/mm9/relations.yml`, phase 4b)

The generator also writes the MM9 world's relation add-on (devdocs/ENERGY_SHIELDS_AND_RELATIONS.md): factions
`mm9_guards` (the GuardBase rows 107-110) and `mm9_monsters` (every row whose native class is an AIBase but not an NPC,
Animal, Cat or GuardBase: the guards' AddFriend list), hostile (`long`) to each other both ways (PoliceMan.inc AddEnemy
AIBase). Every other pair keeps the engine table's relation, which leaves MM9 rows friendly to each other (MM9's
IsFriend default). The scene's `mm9_civilian` (BLV converters, phase 4b) gives a townsperson on a stand-in engine row
`mm9StandInCivilianProfile`; the scene's `dormant` is written for the starts the AI wakes by itself
(`mm9_ai_placement.starts_dormant`).

## Open questions

- The placement-script resolution reads the scripts' text (includes, init calls, `RunScript`), not their control flow:
  a script that starts its loop only on an event counts as fighting (MonkGuardBasic.scr once hostile, NPC109.scr in
  its cinema), and a script with several hand-overs takes the row's or the first. MM9's object classes that carry
  native behaviour without a script were not checked against `passive`.
- The Lobbers' pod rows are assumed from the families; the `LaunchPod` clip spawns natively.
- `attack_reach` takes MM9's body term from the model's sizing dims at the row's scale; a placement with its own
  `Scale` changes it in MM9 (not carried per placement).
- `Att%` is resolved (the chance a monster in reach strikes rather than shoots or casts; the plan's "Phase 2");
  `RangeAttackRange` and the wince threshold columns stay unresolved.
