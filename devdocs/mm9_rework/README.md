# MM9 rework

Rebuilding all 45 MM9 maps as native OpenYAMM ODM/BLV content with MM6–MM8 density, our terrain, skies, water,
x4 textures and 3D creatures. Start with the [plan](PLAN.md): scope, decisions, geometry and creature routes,
phases and pitfalls.

**Taking over the rework?** Start with the [orchestrator handoff of 2026-11-07](HANDOFF_20261107_ORCHESTRATOR.md):
current state, the rebuild/promotion procedure, in-flight work, user decisions and open items.

**MAW in MM9** (the MAW ruleset on Chedian): [devdocs/maw/MM9_SUPPORT.md](../maw/MM9_SUPPORT.md) and
[MM9_BALANCE_DESIGN.md](../maw/MM9_BALANCE_DESIGN.md). MAW's MM9 numbers are its own
(`rulesets/maw/worlds/mm9/balance.yml`); this folder's balance is the Standard ruleset's.

Documents in this folder:

- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): the rework plan and the user's decisions (2026-10-29).
- [INVENTORY.md](INVENTORY.md): Phase 0 inventory, generated by `tools/mm9_import_discovery/inventory_mm9_rework.py`
  (about 5 s; rerun after importer or table changes): every map's size, monsters, NPCs, quests, chests, doors, exits,
  content and terrain textures; the world graph; textures; all 50 monster bodies with rigs, clips and events; and
  the MM6–MM8 starting-area baselines.
- [CHECKS.md](CHECKS.md): feasibility checks before the Isle of Ashes pilot (party scale, terrain resample,
  dragonfly rig and clips), with images in `checks/`.
- [BALANCE_PLAN.md](BALANCE_PLAN.md): MM7 difficulty on MM6–MM8 rules: items, monsters, loot, shops, density, the
  loot-source setting and the comparison report (decisions 2026-11-05, revised 2026-11-06).
- [GROUND_TREASURE_PLAN.md](GROUND_TREASURE_PLAN.md): MM6–MM8-style treasure markers (items on the floor at the
  location's treasure level) in the rebuilt maps: MM7's measured density, levels and placement (draft, 2026-11-08).
- [PLACED_ITEMS_PLAN.md](PLACED_ITEMS_PLAN.md): MM9's pickable props (quest items, keys, skill books, scrolls) as the
  scenes' world items with pickup events (`dungeon/pickups.py`), and Hjordisa the Hag's lost items (2026-11-08).
- [MM9_CUTSCENES.md](MM9_CUTSCENES.md): MM9's cutscenes and camera shake (research, per-map inventory) and the shared
  in-engine cinematics (`<stem>.cinematics.yml`, `evt.PlayCinematic`, `evt.CameraShake`), with the Isle of Ashes pilot.
- [QUEST_XP_AUDIT.md](QUEST_XP_AUDIT.md): every MM9 quest XP award, what it is for, and its balanced amount (decided 2026-11-06).
- [BALANCE_REPORT.md](BALANCE_REPORT.md): generated by `tools/mm9_import_discovery/mm9_balance_report.py --gate`, the balance
  checks on the generated MM9 tables (items, monsters, shops, economy, the engine balance probe).
- [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md): everything still to do and in what order; [OVERNIGHT_MONSTERS.md](OVERNIGHT_MONSTERS.md) and
  [OVERNIGHT_DUNGEONS.md](OVERNIGHT_DUNGEONS.md) are the briefs for unattended runs.

Since 2026-11-06 `assets_dev/worlds/mm9` holds the 44 rebuilt maps (BLVs) and none of the old import's ODMs
([promotion](FINAL_REBUILD_WORK.md), item P1); conversions still install into the review roots first.
Since 2026-11-09 its surface textures are cooked only (`textures_x4/*.oytex`, and `textures/*.oytex` for faces without
an x4 master); every BMP and PNG of them is a source in `assets_source/worlds/mm9/` (see
[ASSET_COOKING_PLAN §15](../ASSET_COOKING_PLAN.md#15-mm9-size-pass-2026-11-06-user-decision)). A promotion copies no
`textures/*.bmp` or `textures_x2/*.png` from a review root into `assets_dev`: put new natives in `assets_source` and let
the build's surface cook make them.

**State check, 2026-11-10**: every map's converter was rebuilt into a scratch folder and compared with `assets_dev`;
all match. The same day installed the converters' pending changes (an empty `pickup_qbits` on 13 maps' world items,
the camera gate in Arslegard City's and Thronheim City's cutscenes), the Tomb's growth at 2x and Thjorad Mine's at
1.4x (BALANCE_PLAN.md 3.6), and the Isle of Ashes' Skeletoid teleport fix (its project README). Every lighting bake
is current (`tools/lighting/stale_bakes.py`): Sturmford's was rebuilt (its BLV of 2026-11-08 had left 19 765 faces
without a lightmap), the other 36 needed only their scene hash. MAW's pack reserves are current
(`tools/maw/mm9_balance.py --check`).

([review_root.py](../../level_generation/projects/mm9r_common/review_root.py)):

of `level_generation/creatures/mm9_dfly_claude/`; humanoids wait for the humanoid route piloted on Forad Darre
(`level_generation/creatures/mm9_forad_claude/`). MM9's own visual properties (translucency, glow masks, texture lights, animated textures, skin alpha) are
inventoried in [MM9 visual properties](MM9_VISUAL_PROPERTIES.md) (current state and queue: [status](STATUS.md)): carry them over (match or gain, lose nothing), and

decisions, are on one review page: `level_generation/creatures/claude_shared/mm9_overview/index.html` (served at
`http://127.0.0.1:8912/level_generation/creatures/claude_shared/mm9_overview/`).

New rework documents (pilot records, per-region notes, creature routes) go here. The existing
[`devdocs/mm9/`](../mm9/) records belong to the earlier integration, which imported MM9 maps as generated
`bmodel_world` ODMs; the triage below says which of them still hold.
