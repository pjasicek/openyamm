## Phase 0 work, in order

1. **The town format** (TOWNS_FORMAT.md) and `gen_towns.py`. Per house:
   - a new house id (4001+);
   - type, name and hours;
   - residents (1, sometimes 2 or 3; new NPC ids 4001+, each with portrait, profession or role, greeting; topics
     filled by A3 under the same NPC id);
   - teacher offers;
   - shop stock tier and kinds;
   - guild spells;
   - training cap;
   - transport routes.

   It writes `house_data`, `house_animations`, `house_exits`, `house_movies`, `npc`, `npc_greet`, `teacher_topics`,
   `trainer_places`, `transport_index`, `transport_locations` and `roster` rows, in the formats of A1's
   `devdocs/xeen/lanes/TABLES.md` (start from the engine tables' formats).
2. **Vertigo (X1) and Rivercity (X2) in full**:
   - every service and residence the built maps have;
   - Vertigo's Xeen names and people (WORLD_DESIGN §5.1, the X1 and X2 NOTES, MAPS's original lines: Joe, Gunther,
     Thylon, Myra, Oslo, Celia, Valia, the drunk, Vern; Rivercity's skill row Arnold's Gym, Martial Training,
     Lindon's Temps, Flint, Barok, Nystor);
   - their teachers and the training caps (Vertigo 10, Rivercity 25);
   - stables;
   - Nystor's boat;
   - the town mirrors.

   Put the old→new house id mapping for each door in your lane file, "House ids for the map lanes".
3. **The world's teacher plan**: `source/towns/teachers_plan.yml`, about 120 offers, three per skill in three towns,
   Grandmasters late or remote (WORLD_DESIGN §5.3). `towns.py` checks it and every town file against it.
4. **The Adventurer's Inn roster** for Vertigo (WORLD_DESIGN §5.3).
5. **Town Portal**: the destination list (12 towns, two faces) and the page art (Codex, the Obsidian UI style of the
   current Town Portal screen), for A1's W4. **The new-game card art** for A1's E2.
6. **First portrait batch**: Vertigo's and Rivercity's named NPCs.

## House ids for the map lanes

Fixed on 2026-11-20; these ids do not change. A door is named by the map project's building id (`#n` is its
`index`); the old row is what the built map opens today. Map lanes: replace the old row with the Xeen house per door
(X1 opens rows 1122 and 1126 at two doors each: the new ids are per door). "new" doors are buildings that today run a
message or an event; they become ordinary house doors. The topic texts of every NPC are A3's (`source/npcs/<map>.yml`,
keyed by these NPC ids); the town files are `assets_dev/worlds/xeen/source/towns/x1.yml` and `x2.yml`.

per town map in the order of TOWNS_FORMAT.md §2. NPC portraits are `npc<10000 + NPC id>.png` (npc14001 …).
