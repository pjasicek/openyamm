## Rules for every fix agent

  build → private hard-link root → checks → playtests → own screenshots → install into `assets_dev` → bake straight after),

  commands), and your decisions' full answers. Do what the decision says; where it is unclear or proves wrong in
  practice, take the option cheaper to undo, say so in your section, and do not ask the user.
- **Fix at the source, then every site of its kind, with a check** (generators and shared tools, never only an
  installed file; generated tables and Lua are never edited by hand: `tools/xeen/build_world.py --only <module>`).
- **Never** `git add/commit/stash/reset/restore --staged`. Never edit `world.yml`, `source/maps.yml` or DECISIONS.md
  (the orchestrator's). Never kill a PID you have not just checked with `ps -o pid,ppid,cmd -p <pid>`; never a parent.

  `output/xeen_locks/cpp_build.lock` (only the engine agent builds C++; everyone else uses the released
  `build-xeen/game/openyamm` and `openyamm-studio/build-xeen/oymap`). Heavy jobs through `tools/heavy.sh <cap>` (game
  run or scenario 6, oymap build 8, bake 16, C++ build 24 with -j12). Game runs headless with
  `SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy OPENYAMM_START_MINIMIZED=1`, your own `--user-data` under `output/xeen_fb/p6_<you>/`.
  Scratch in `output/xeen_fb/p6_<you>/`.

- Shared files you may both touch (`tools/xeen/regressions.sh`, `tools/xeen/check_wild.py`, `tools/xeen/door_check.py`):
  small targeted edits, re-read just before editing.
- End checks before you report: `python3 tools/lighting/stale_bakes.py --check` 0 for your maps,
  `python3 tools/xeen/build_world.py --check` 0, your maps' playtests, `tools/xeen/regressions.sh` gates you touched.
- Report in your own section under "Results" below (what changed, files, checks with numbers, pictures you looked at,
