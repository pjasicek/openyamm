# Xeen: design decisions

Owner: the orchestrator. The user's instruction (2026-11-20): every design question a lane or the orchestrator has is
routed to a new Opus decider agent, given the context it needs, which decides what is best for the circumstances; the
user is not asked. The orchestrator records each decision here and relays it to the lanes that act on it. The user
can overrule any entry; it then stays here, marked as overruled, with the user's answer.

Decisions taken before this rule (the user's of 2026-11-20 and the orchestrator's provisional ones) are in
[STATUS.md](STATUS.md) "Decisions". The provisional ones are re-decided by a decider (D-1 below) and recorded here.

## How a question is decided

1. A lane writes the question in its report under `DECIDE:`: what is to be decided, the options it sees, its evidence
   (measurements, files, pictures) and what depends on it. The orchestrator collects its own questions the same way.
2. The orchestrator gives each question, or a group of closely related ones, to a **new** Opus agent (the decider).
   Its brief is self-contained: the question and the lane's evidence; the documents to read (README's decisions,
   ROADMAP §0 and §2, the design documents of the area, the user's known preferences in
   `devdocs/map_studio/HANDOFF_LEAD.md` §2 and §5 and this file); the constraints (MM6/MM7 quality, the MM6–MM8 power
   curve, the per-map table, the texture budget, the dressing rule, no riddles the game does not answer, no stand-ins);
   and what each option would cost the lanes.
3. The decider reads, may measure or look at the shipped games and the built maps, but edits nothing. It answers with
   the decision, the reason in a few sentences, what each affected lane must do, and whether the decision is cheap to
   reverse later.
4. The orchestrator records the answer below (numbered D-n), relays it, and moves the item off the waiting lists.
