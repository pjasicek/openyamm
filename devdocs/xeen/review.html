OpenYAMM · design proposal · 2026-11-12

Xeen World Atlas

A new MM6–MM8 style world that tells the story of World of Xeen on new maps. It is one flat world with two inhabited faces, joined by Ancient pyramids, and its edge is a cliff above the stars.

Two faces of one world

North is up on both faces. Each region is one surface map: the source's 2×2 groups of squares, laid out fresh at MM6–MM8 scale. Plate 4 · Map plans (MU-3, first pass)

The two arrival maps, to scale

For the user

Questions before production

  1. One world with two faces, or two worlds? Recommended: one world.
  2. MM6–MM8 Fly (restricted on sky maps and story castles), or MM9's Fleet Foot and no Fly? Recommended: Fly.
  3. Open Darkside from about party level 12, or play the faces in sequence? Recommended: interleave.
  4. Full plan or lean variant? Recommended: decide after the Act I slice.
  5. Light/Dark promotions per character or per party? Recommended: per character.
  6. Artifacts: MM9's black-chest rule or MM6–MM8 random level-6 drops? Recommended: MM6–MM8 plus fixed quest artifacts.
  7. May restored Xeen faces become NPC portraits?
  8. Gems as a second currency (skulls, the dragon tax, gemsmiths)? Recommended: yes.
  9. Add Xeen's Barbarian class and Gnome race? Recommended: no.
  10. Music: MM6–MM8 tracks by area, or new music?
