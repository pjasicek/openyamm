# MAW gaps

Every known difference between OpenYAMM's MAW ruleset and MAW `ef23ee17` that is not a recorded deviation: what is
missing or wrong, its priority and its status. This is the one list to work from. Deliberate differences live in
[DEVIATIONS.md](DEVIATIONS.md); [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) §6 records what each phase built.

Sources: the 2026-10-24 gap audit (nine reviewers, then verified item by item against both repositories; ids A, B,
D, E, H keep the audit's numbering), the "Left" notes of the HANDOFF phase rows (ids L), and later findings (ids N).
MAW paths are relative to `Maw-Mod-MMMerge/Scripts/` (DM `Modules/MawCore/Damage.lua`, IT `General/zzMaw-Items.lua`,
SP `General/zzMaw-Spells.lua`, S `General/zzMAW-Skills.lua`, MON `General/zzMaw-Monsters.lua`, CL
`Modules/MawCore/Classes.lua`).

Priority: **P1** wrong rules in a MAW game; **P2** missing MAW behaviour a player notices; **P3** small, edge case,
text. Status: **open**; **done** (with the date); **dev N** (became deviation N); **decide** (the user's call, the
current behaviour stays until then); **phase 9** (challenge modes and randomizer); **no port** (decided, with the
reason).
