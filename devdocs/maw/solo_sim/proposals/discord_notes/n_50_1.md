# MAW #balance-discussion, 2026-08-02 to 2026-08-20 (chunk_50_1): notes for the solo-BM goal

Scope note: this slice is the 5.0 *development and open beta* ("We're in an open beta", Archmage1, 08-19). The name
"Beyond Madness" never appears; the top tier discussed is **Madness**, with **RTI** (Road to Insanity) called second
highest, then Insanity, Doom, normal. Rawsugar does not post in this slice. Malekith (dev) posts often; heavy
contributors: Carta (solo player, lvl 1800 solo saves), catles (caster/party, also solo shaman), Freeday005, Krishh,
Keyen (party, early 5.0 tester), VictorDragonslayer.

## 1. What 5.0 changed, and why

- 08-04 Malekith (dev): "I might go for a massive de-power creep"; "just keep numbers down a bit... without making
  progression to straight up feel bad"; "reducing numbers is not the only goal"; "I'll go with math heavy". Reading:
  5.0 is a number squish plus rebalance, not a pure nerf.
- 08-08 Malekith: "Number squish also increase the amount of content around your difficulty"; "you could go higher and
  not be punished as much... And go lower without being faceroll"; "higher difficulties are still clutch around your
  level". Reading: intent is a wider band of level-appropriate content.
- 08-08 Malekith: "you have no ideas about the amount of nerfs I'm doing for 5.0". "melees are basically done, I need to
  nerf casters and then balance monsters". Showed damage-taken range "from 1% damage taken to 8.3%, 8.3x damage" (a DK)
  then "**set hard cap to 90% now**". Reading: damage reduction hard-capped at 90 % (so >=10 % of raw damage always
  lands). This is the root of the user's "10 % floor".
- 08-05 / 08-09 Malekith: legendaries lose the +50 % base stats: "+50% stats will go away, it's going to be baked into
  primordials"; "that alone is responsible for almost 10x damage". Legendary = epic stats; primordial/ancient become
  "stat sticks". Strength may stop giving % damage.
- 08-08 Malekith: All Seven now +20 %. Alchemy temporary buff potions turned into % (divine power potion = **15 %**
  stats, 08-14); permanent potion stats unchanged ("20 every 50 power"). Potion buffs and the matching spell buffs no
  longer stack (meditation still stacks; stoneskin does not) (08-13/14).
- 08-10 Malekith: "removed completely bolster resistances" (monsters keep native resists). "removing items being
  rolled the first time you enter a map". Weapon recovery reduced to offset slower animation: "basically same speed,
  less damage". Luck nerfed twice; accuracy crit damage nerfed (Carta: "6K accuracy is now 175% crit damage"). Speed
  regains dodge. AC "is a % like armors"; skill AC halved.
- 08-10 Malekith: old Madness item level cap was **720**.
- 08-13/14 Transcendence potions deleted ("making 50, 100 or 200 trans pots is a design aberration", Carta); Malekith
  replaced them with a **skill-point potion**: "each 50 [potion power], you get +20, 50, 90, 140, 200, 270 etc..."
  (cumulative skill points, like permastats). Freeday: "trans pots are skillpots".
- 08-14: SP-on-kill legendary cut to **5 % (was 10 %) of unreserved remaining mana** (Malekith: "so yeah, it's a big
  nerf"). Enlightenment nerfed; regeneration "changed, along with meditation" (reserved pool reduces regen: 50 %
  reserved = -20 % regen, 75 % = -35 %).
- 08-15: Skill caps added: mana shield capped at **skill 50**; alchemy cap "50 o 100 looks good"; cover/retaliation
  already capped. Malekith: "there are too many skill points it is very hard to balance. If game would end at lvl 100 no
  cap would be needed." Krishh: caps freeze midgame power at a static level.
- 08-15 Malekith (XP curve): "Now it's lvl*1000, I want to make it exponential. The idea is to make normal game go still
  to level 100, all 3 games around 200-250, madness around 500. or even lower." 08-17: "I changed exp required to level
  up"; "RTI is lvl 250"; "madness goes up to 500". Skill levels at end: "**around 80 in madness**", Doom "250 level, so
  around 40"; "at level 500 you will have around 80. at level 300 you have around 50". Mastery thresholds proposal
  "10-24-40" (catles wants GM at 30-35). Malekith: "I like people keep progressing into skills until later on".
- 08-15 Malekith (item scaling): "instead of leveling up and getting weaker, you just find less strong items"; "it has a
  consequence on accessibility, aka you can push monsters much higher than your level"; items get "both" lower stats
  and lower stat limits. 08-16: enchant max "150 is max in new mode, 120 base, 1.25 for primordials". Higher item
  level requirements make blue items "fairly unusable" (Carta 08-13).
- Gems: 08-15 "it changes everything for gems which I'm probably going to remove/limit"; 08-16 "Current one yes, I'll
  rework them"; "**new gems will have flat stats to customize stats in your items**"; "I removed duplicated enchants, so
  this will be a way to stack a single stat (no more + might + might)"; 08-18 "only gems, adding them as stat sticks
  probably". Other crafting mats (cubes, hourglass, eye, mirror, emeralds) stay.
- Cubes: 08-16 Malekith "I'll make crafting cubes to increase a weapon/armor base stats by **up to 20%**". 08-18 "once I
  balance the healing spells and rework cubes, 5.0 has the least minimum features to be enjoyable".
- Loot: 08-15 "I also want to make epic the standard, not everyone being always full primordial"; "a lot in the game
  depends on legendaries, which is not fun if I make them too rare"; celestials and artifacts excepted. 08-17 new loot
  table: celestial about 1 per 125 bosses from level 300 ("around 2x, as intended"); mob loot chance ~15/30/60 % for
  A/B/C tiers. Keyen: "you have a zone lvl 300 with 600 mobs and 20 bosses... 94% chance to get a celestial".
- 08-18 Malekith: "boss nerfed and density multiplier removed" (density multiplier gave ~3x damage, broodlord 3x more);
  "monsters, loot, spells and melees are balanced"; monk/DK/seraph/shaman/MAW classes still to check. Artifacts nerfed
  (Keyen 08-20: "lost 60% dps on artifact update"; Malekith: "you are supposed to get online at 70 skill levels").
- Shrinking Ray nerfed "to half, and damage reduction to 30%, same as DK" (08-15). Debuffs "already hit 50% vs bosses";
  CC reworked "reliable, with smart targeting" (08-08).
- Leech (Carta, confirmed by Krishh): heal = own max HP x (damage / monster max HP) x leech %, overkill counts, no cap
  except your max. Krishh: "it really discourages taking hard fights" (higher-level monster = more damage taken, less
  leech). Malekith 08-15: may remove the spell-leech legendary and "make it a weapon effect".
- Mana shield: Malekith 08-04 idea "make it as a buff that reduces damage taken" (casters build HP too), shelved 08-05
  ("changes fundamentally what casters are"). 08-15: "I definitely want to keep some classes having 100% mana shield".
  Carta numbers: "381K mana x 30 (manashield) is already 11,3 million EHP"; "in 4.0, casters with over 600M
  toughness". Malekith: "on paper a sorcerer has around 2x vitality compared to a knight, but it's all unhealable";
  endurance limited "at level 900", mana shield skill up to 1000 (pre-cap).

## 2. Solo play

  damage by 40% and count as cover" ("40% chance to do 40% reduction it's just a 16% reduction"). catles: "you gonna

- 08-11 Valkus: "Is solo still going to be viable?" Malekith: "**Yes ofc**".
- 08-13 Carta: potion/spell buff non-stacking "is a huge equalizer for solo, where having access to buffs was a huge
  advantage over classes who didn't".
- 08-14 Carta/Krishh: solo has far fewer gold problems ("solo does seem to intentionally have infinite money as one of
  its perks. See also them getting lloyd's for free", Krishh); "in solo 100% you can GM everything" (Don Q.). Carta:
  solo alchemy stops paying around skill 50 (later "20-30").
- 08-15 Carta: old solo runs finish at "**1600-1800**" (elementalist and knight lvl 1800 full celestial shown).
  Malekith: "Benchmarks are up to lvl 1000".
- 08-15 Malekith: "**I'll probably remove exp bonus from solo btw**" -> solo "exactly same level as a party? Yes, but more
  skill points"; "you would get double skill points"; gryz: "only 1/5 of the xp... when you are Solo?" Malekith: "yes,
  but more stuff per level"; "I want to see if new changes make it feasable". Malekith's concern: "the extra
  personality multiplier and solo having much higher skill level".
- Push-back: Don Q. "wouldnt this make solo essentially unviable?"; Valkus "Please don't... losing that would make solo
  unplayable". Carta: "lvl 1800 is 170K skillpoints, lvl 1200 is 77,5K, double would be -12%", and the new skill pot is
  "much, much, much stronger for parties", so "close to a triple" nerf. SP per level: 5 at 1-9, +1 per 10 levels

- 08-18 Carta (Madness, "a solo character with skill points x2", 5.0 beta numbers): cannot tank a devil king ("not

  boss for literally 100 seconds". Malekith: devil king "would do 180k damage, with reductions around 45k" (his build
  ~450k average one-type hit); density multiplier then removed and bosses nerfed.
- 08-18 Carta: "the average solo experience if you [start without prep] is just dying in two hits by green pirates";
  Malekith: "you have starting gold and levels for a reason". catles: strongest solo start = 50k gold, rush Kriegspire
  well (XP not counted toward the party-XP penalty), then Dragonsand altar.
- Solo opinions: catles "solo game is actually easier in maw... I proved it" (endgame solo shaman that still couldn't
  afford all skills); Archmage1 "solo is harder early, easier late"; catles: hard part is "dungeon entrance ambushes".
  Don Q. soloed sorcerer on Madness (~130-150 h), skipped wasp nest for lack of DPS, cheesed troll tomb with doors.
- VictorDragonslayer 08-18: earlier "2x CC duration for solo" was announced but never worked; CC at 1.2 s duration vs
  1.2 s recovery is weak for solo.

## 5. Endgame and farm loops

- Dimension maps: 08-10 Malekith "I'll try first to remove them alltogether, with crunched numbers it might work";
  08-11 **why**: "Because after you get maps it becomes a grindfest, not a mode where you need to venture places higher
  than your level". Alternatives: "give a map every 50 levels", fixed level; "I'm fine having players to minmax their
  map, specially if limited". 08-11 money: "You choose your priorities carefully". 08-16 "It's more about maps being
  limited" (reason for higher training costs). 08-17 "now in madness there are no maps atm"; "**I'm probably going to
  add maps, I just need to do some calculations**"; "map updates, if it comes, it will be with some loot nerf"; "what's
  dangerous is increased rarity in maps".
- Pre-5.0 maps were effectively infinite (map drop pity, maps from bounties/arena, "level 1000+ maps drop maps");
  Krishh: endless grindable resources "are very clearly against Malekith's design goals for MAW". catles ran maps with
  "800 bosses" / "between 1000 and 1100 bosses" with 3 omnipotents in a party.
- Arena gold: lord-difficulty arena oozes = "six digit payout for 20s of work" (Carta, "600K gold"). Malekith 08-15:
  "Yeah I need to remove it, but there are some quests related". Krishh calls it bug abuse.
- Gold/economy: skill training costs scale with number of skills ("to master 5 skills is 7.5mil gold, to master 8
  skills is 18 million", catles); Malekith 08-16 "training cost should go up instead" (of prices). VictorDragonslayer:
  "I'm against money sinks becoming money bottlenecks."
- Crafting philosophy, 08-17: catles "you are making poe or d3/d4 out of the game"; Malekith "why would you think I want
  to make it as diablo"; "having already all gear ready and just needing crafting items to make it good isn't fun...
  end game you have a bunch of eye/hourglass you are never going to use". VictorDragonslayer: "Players want good gear
  and will do anything in order to get it. Game designer's job is not to stop them but make these actions interesting."
- Post-game: Krishh "Post 1000k is irrelevant, that's postgame after you've won if you want to keep leveling forever."
- 08-14 Malekith "you gave me a nice idea" on "imagine if you needed 150 repair to handle legendaries" (Archmage1: repair
  skill to use enchantment-upgrade items). Possible skill-gated crafting.

## 6. Ideas fitting the solo power goal, and rejections

Proposed / dev-positive:
- Solo cover/retaliation as a passive (40 % chance, -40 % damage, counts as cover): Malekith "I guess yeah" (08-05).
- Solo XP bonus traded for **double skill points** per level (Malekith 08-15): a solo-only power lever via skills,
  not levels. Players say x2 is not enough (Carta's -12 % maths).
- Flat-stat gems "to customize stats in your items" and stack one stat (08-16); cubes +up to 20 % base stats (08-16).
- Limited, fixed-level maps ("a map every 50 levels") as controlled farm (08-11); maps may return with loot nerf (08-17).
- Legendary effect transfer into primordials ("you might want to transfer legendary effect into primordials", 08-05).
- Legendary "increase mana regeneration by 1% per 1% mana reserved" (Malekith 08-04, legendary idea).
- Krishh: single transcendence pot to a cap rising with potion power; Carta: quest XP = one level each.
- VictorDragonslayer: move affixes to high skill levels; berserk affixes onto artifacts; artifact rework sheet.
- Archmage1 "Predator"/hoarder boss; Malekith "Predator v2: kills monsters around maps, getting their loot".

Rejected / dev against:
- Monsters scaling with the player: Malekith 08-17 "**I promised myself I will never ever ever do 'monsters scale with
  you' stuff**"; "if everything in the game progress with you, then you are not progressing"; "my goal in games is to
  minmax and become strong. if playing makes you weaker then it's a no for me"; "Monsters scaling with you is a lazy way
  to balance stuff". (Bolster exists as an option; Carta favours global scaling.)
- Endless grind loops (maps as grindfest, arena gold, transcendence spam, pyramid/trading cheeses) removed.
- Unlimited legendary dependence: "I don't want to make legendaries the main source of mana" (08-10).
- New systems now: "I'm delving deep into KC class design, I have no space in my head to create new systems here.
  I'll just tune what is there" (08-16). Ranger light/dark: "naah". Seraph dual-wield spear: "never". Bow-only button:
  "no bow melee is intended".
- Robes slot: too much art work (08-07).

## Most relevant for the solo-BM goal (top 10)

1. 08-08 Malekith set the damage-reduction **hard cap to 90 %** during the 5.0 squish (DK showed 1 % -> 8.3 % taken).
   That is where the 10 % floor comes from, so a solo layer has to add HP/EHP or avoidance, not more reduction.
2. 08-15 Malekith: "I'll probably remove exp bonus from solo"; solo gets the same level as a party but "**double skill
   points**", "more stuff per level". Players: this makes solo unviable (Carta: about -12 % SP versus old lvl 1800 solo,
   nearer a triple nerf with the party-favoured skill pot). The 5.0 solo power deficit is a design choice to rebalance
   around, not a bug.
3. 08-15/17 New XP curve is exponential. Madness ends around **lvl 500** with main skills around **80** (lvl 300 about
   50; Doom 250, about 40). Skill caps were added (mana shield 50, alchemy 50/100) because "too many skill points".
   Before 5.0, solo ran to 1600-1800; dev benchmarks stop at 1000.
4. 08-17 Malekith: "**never ever ever** do 'monsters scale with you'"; "my goal in games is to minmax and become strong".
   A PoE-style solo power path fits his philosophy. Monster scaling to the player does not.
5. 08-11 Maps removed because "it becomes a grindfest, not a mode where you need to venture places higher than your
   level". Then 08-17: "probably going to add maps... with some loot nerf"; alternative "a map every 50 levels",
   fixed level, min-maxable but limited. An opt-in, bounded map farm is acceptable to the dev.
6. 08-18 Carta, solo with x2 SP in Madness: cannot survive one devil-king hit (needs about 600K HP) and needs about 100 s
   to kill one. Malekith removed the density multiplier (about 3x) and nerfed bosses in response. Solo was already
   walled against top-end bosses during the beta.
7. 08-05 Malekith accepted a solo-only cover/retaliation stand-in ("40% chance... reduce damage by 40%", about 16 %
   average). This is precedent for solo-exclusive defensive mechanics.
8. 08-15/16 Item power was tied to drop level ("instead of leveling up and getting weaker, you just find less strong
   items"), with lower enchant caps (150 max: 120 base x1.25 primordial) and +50 % legendary stats moved into
   primordials. Gems became flat-stat customisers, one stat can stack, and cubes add up to +20 % base. These are the
   crafting knobs a solo power path can extend, for example solo-only gem tiers or extra cube uses.
9. Leech heals (own max HP x damage / monster max HP x %), with overkill counting. Krishh: it "discourages taking hard
   fights", because high-level monsters leech less. With the 10 % floor and BB-capped HP, solo sustain against
   lvl-321 BM mobs falls off. A solo talent could scale leech differently.
10. The economy assumes no infinite gold. The dev wants the arena exploit removed and endless resources are "against
    Malekith's design goals". Yet solo was seen as having "infinite money as one of its perks" (free Lloyd's, can GM
    everything). A solo gold or skill sink, such as talent purchases, fits better than raw stat farming.
