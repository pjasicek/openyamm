# MAW #balance-discussion, solo / insanity slice 1 (2023-08 to 2025-05, before MAW 5.0)

Source: `out/chunk_old_solo_1.txt` (2814 lines, read in full). Dev = Malekith (lead), Rawsugar (co-dev, spells/classes,
author of Austerity mode). "Beyond Madness" is never named in this period; the top difficulty here is
**Road to Insanity (RtI / "insanity")**, added 2024-10-21. Versions named: 2.5.0 stable (mid 2024), 3.0 (Dec 2024
rework), "MAW Redone" (DaveHern's map variant, Dec 2024), Austerity (Jan 2025).

## 1. Solo runs and the developers' stance on solo

- 2024-01-12 dev Malekith: "difficulty is the same, but you get 5x experience and you can focus all the good loot and
  barrels on 1 character". Solo = same monsters, all XP and loot on one body. Reading: the original solo deal.
- 2024-01-22 dev: "I had lot of fun playing troll solo ... regeneration skill is so strong now ... continuously".
  Troll regen is the dev's own solo template.
- 2024-03-01 dev: "Knight can't because has no self sustain"; "any Class with healing spell/regeneration can solo. Even
  better if they have alchemy"; "maw custom classes ... kinda good in many fields ... Making them best for solo,
  balanced at most for 5 man". Self-sustain is the solo gate; hybrid custom classes were knowingly solo-favoured.
- 2024-03-01 player MR___TJ: "everyone dies solo vs same lvl stuff ... besides of 100% immune vampire ... you get 1 shot".
  Same-level fights were already lethal for solo in early 2024.
- 2024-03-03 dev: misc skills no longer party-shared, highest used automatically, "when playing solo, all misc skills are
  learnable up to GM" ("Master of all"). First explicit solo-only rule. Later (2024-04-22) MR___TJ: "Solo was adjusted
  to give everyone buffs" (ID monster etc.); 2025-02-02 alchemy auto-trains in solo; 2025-03-06 solo gets permanent
  Torch Light + Wizard Eye buffs; 2024-08-26 dev: Fly/TP/Beacon "it's unlimited in solo with scrolls".
- 2024-03-11 dev: Nightmare is party-balanced, solo is "ultimate challenge"; "Solo it's even harder early, but eventually
  you get so much good gear and experience it becomes around the same difficulty"; "With DK I'm soloing it fairly easily".
- 2024-04-09/11 Hagnak solo druid lvl 229, 6M gold; "How is solo spellcaster?" - "Very easy". Aku solo shaman, 16M gold,
  bow, "at this stage they were no challenge at all". Gold stops mattering mid-game (trade triangle).
- 2024-04-24 Aku solo druid: melee "10k+ a hit", Immolation 300/tick.
- 2024-06-03 dev: "I myself have been easily able to clear [DOOM] with solo DK with 0 grind".
- 2024-06-13 dev: "You can play solo directly btw, you don't really need 5 party members to Clear the game".
- 2024-06-25 dev (key): "Party is going to be much stronger early and late game. Solo has a power spike mid, where you
  can upgrade weapons and armors with cubes and get full legendary. After that solo falls off. A well build party will
  always outperform a solo player". The dev's own model: solo plateaus after it is fully geared.
- 2024-06-25 Hagnak: "why do you think I started playing solo classes? because healing sucks ... I can just focus on
  mitigating damage". Ark Tolei (Doom party): non-boss hits for 150 % max HP; minotaur king 14-20k vs troll 8000 HP;
  "how anyone is touching the third continent on doom without just going solo deathknight".
- 2024-07-17 dev: buff rework (mana reservation) hurt solo, "but with the Oracle orb changes I'm pretty sure that solo
  players can easily break the game".
- 2024-08-26 dev: "DK early game is godlike (and intended to, hero class)"; catles: "you keep forgetting what makes dk
  solo: blood leech"; "solo knight is possible, but you gotta vampire your weapon".
- 2024-09-03 dev: "Any class can solo tho ... I have a solo dk save ... he's immortal". catles: water/mind druid "strongest
  class in the game"; 2024-09-16 catles quit a solo druid challenge "too boring and easy".
- 2024-10-19 dev: "Solo basically gets 2.2 times levels". Rawsugar: "it really does look like solo is too easy vs party
  lategame". Hagnak lvl 540->600 "in 15 minutes with just killing mobs in maps".
- 2024-11-15 Hagnak finished RtI with a vampire-magic caster spamming Life Drain + Rock Blast, "able to level up by 140".
- 2024-11-17 catles: solo XP is mostly quest XP, "heavily penalized by monster experience cause usually you outlevel them".
- 2024-11-27 catles: "solo is in a sense easier ... only harder on entrances"; "mages can steamroll solo ... getting all

- 2024-11-29 dev: "The reason solo it's easy I bet it's because you can abuse crafting cubes".
- 2024-12-14 Rawsugar (key): "starting out you are super super weak, just 1 guy instead of 5, in a game that is balanced

- 2024-12-15 dev: "insanity is designed so that you might not be able to Clear it if too unoptimal"; solo MM7 start on

## 2. Difficulties: intent and changes

- Ladder seen: Normal / MAW, True Nightmare (2023), Nightmare (packs 1-6), Hard/Hell, Doom (packs 1-9; ID item and disarm
  mandatory, 2024-04), Road to Insanity (2024-10, packs 5-9), Austerity (Rawsugar, 2025-01), Challenge renamed Speedrun.
- 2024-03-11 dev on Nightmare: "too hard early and too easy as you go on ... it's too much" (powercreep).
- 2024-06-03 dev on Doom: dungeon reset made more accessible, "before an unoptimized party wasn't able to clear doom. Now
  every party can eventually grind out their way. If your party isn't bad you don't need any of that."
- 2024-10-21 dev, RtI announcement: "only pushes the game to the extremes ... Nerfs are very likely NOT to happen".
  Food per dungeon limited; E/M/GM at 8/20/32; rising mastery costs; no dungeon reset; maps never reset; items/enchants
  +30 %; packs 5-9; monster HP and damage up; legendaries +50 %; gems, cubes, emeralds, mirrors x2 "so you have much
  more freedom with crafting". catles: no resets means you may run out of XP; dev: "Yes, that's the whole point".
- 2024-10-21 dev: RtI monster HP "not crazy high, as I expect players to build more defensive".
- 2024-10-26 dev: magic maps locked behind lvl 300 in RtI "because otherwise free farming ... intended for you to clear all
  the games before ... was ok for doom, but not for insanity". Maps drop from lvl 100 on Doom, 300+ on RtI.
- 2024-11-03 catles (key): "monster bolster faster than players: in doom that powercurve starts at 550 and by 750 you are
  relatively equal ... in insanity that point is 250 and it goes downhill much faster; gear doesnt catch up to monster
  bolster". 2024-12-28 repeats: Doom ~600, RtI ~200; "at the same level, item lvls are designed to have max stats", so
  fights become "sucker punches of infinite save/load". Dev: "Once you manage clearing maps you should be fine".
- 2024-12-01 dev, big nerf pass (3.0): RtI item power 66 % -> 40 %; all enchant power -37 %; RtI loses better-enchant
  odds; Doom/RtI density 5-7 -> 4-5 per pack; map-completion XP much lower under high bolster; RtI XP was 40 % too high.
  Reason: "too many monsters, too much loot, AoE too OP, allow me to make single enemies st[ronger]".
- 2024-12-05 dev (key): "scaling went a bit too exponential previously. This means that as long as you could fight lower
  level monster insanity was actually very very easy, and higher level monsters were too hard." 2024-12-06: "with latest
  changes, pacing is closer to what's my vision". catles objected: "you get your build together and its supposed to get
  easier if done right".
- 2025-01-05 Rawsugar on Austerity: weaker items, far fewer crafting mats, stronger monsters, "the idea is precisely for
  hell to be near impossible"; "past level 300 the whole game should be level 300 content"; RtI "might be fixable as ...
  a harder doom that doesnt spiral out of control ... making it more forgiving past level 300 would work as well".
- 2025-01-05 catles: finished RtI at 346; his maps were 320-350 (bolstered 350+); Malekith needed an hour for the easiest.
- 2025-02-23 Rawsugar: below the top modes "items follow the stronger monsters. the clear exception is insanity and
  austerity where this is absolutely not the case".

## 3. Power curve, scaling, items, crafting, farming

- 2024-04-12 dev: dodge cap is multiplicative, "0.99^1000 if you have unarmed 1000" - never 100 %.
- 2024-04-15 dev: "the hardest part comes whenever you end up in a dungeon higher level than you. As long as you can farm
  lower level dungeons it isn't very hard".
- 2024-05-09 dev: "at level 200 getting +4 AC is nothing"; crafting cubes upgrade armour.
- 2024-05-28 Aku: dungeon reset "like D2 farming ... would make Doom solo more feasible ... Want to farm and create a
  monster of a party? Play Doom solo and farm". Dev: "I already added that" (later removed for RtI).
- 2024-06-03 new XP formula: less XP for lower-level kills, more for higher. Aku: "very bad for solo play ... One of the
  advantages of solo is being overleveled"; Hagnak asked for a 75 % XP floor; Rawsugar: "Previously grinding allowed
  player to basically bypass difficulty setting, the new xp system makes this much harder". catles 2024-07-07: "10-20%
  if you start outlevling em". Dev kept it ("level taken in account isn't character level, but level gained").
- 2024-06-03 Pythia (solo DK, Doom, lvl 155): gem tier per dungeon; "stats from eq upgrade seems more worth it than
  higher levels".
- 2024-07-01 dev: "item power is capped btw, you have up to 22 item tiers".
- 2024-07-15 dev: MM8 arena leaderboards, "we can also separate solo with full party category". MR___TJ idea: monthly
  bounty board that respawns one dungeon at your level.
- 2024-07-19 dev: "Poe is for math nerds actually".
- 2024-09-16: legendary effects named: +ascension, 10 % HP/MP on kill, a resistance one, 10 % less damage, double item
  stats. Rawsugar: "seems a big jump to be able to kill level 600 when you are 416. but might just be compounding bonuses".
- 2024-11-01 catles: in RtI/Doom "you just pump your defenses not to get oneshot", the HP/MP-on-kill legendary is "the
  only way to boost your damage by boosting defenses ... not OP but mandatory". Hagnak: RtI maps meant to be post-game.
- 2024-11-02 Rawsugar: "cant quite find a way to tank and dps ... best i can do is one third damage and triple toughness
  ... there is as much compounding stats as there is diminishing return".
- 2024-11-03 dev: "mages are currently OP in insanity ... the fact is that damage is OP"; HP/MP-on-kill legendary nerfed
  (one proc per AoE). catles: "you absolutely cannot face tank anything ... only saving grace is ranged damage".
- 2024-11-27 dev: monster-dropped maps exist for outdoor maps too (e.g. a New Sorpigal map); random monsters planned.
- 2024-11-29 dev: "I think there is another layer of issues with stats going so high".
- 2024-12-28 dev: maps "only spawn from level 300 ... so clearing fully all the m&m is needed".
- 2024-12-27 anon: trainer cap 1000 (Balthazar Lair); route "reset Nagas ... then Endless Arena ... wait for the Random
  Map - then grind some gear". 2025-02-05 Rawsugar: "farming was never neccesary ... current insanity might warrant it".
- 2025-02-23 Albedo suggestion: Doom/RtI loot level = max(map level, party level/2) so low dungeons still pay.
- 2025-02-24 Albedo: "AC became a slot machine ... you stay at about the same 75% mitigation with 30% block. Just that
  enemies start to hit stronger, so you need to compensate with hp pool"; wants status-immunity and -10 %/near-enemy DR
  legendaries; mirrors never seen.
- 2025-04-09 Albedo: MAW is "not PoE/Last Epoch with sheer amount of loot"; solo crafting chain (usable base, right 2 bases,
  right special, good rolls, gems) is too RNG; "It can work at around 500 level, with maps ... but at that time it is
  usually already 3 weeks, and you continue only for numbers sake"; lowest RtI map seen 320, tank 1-4 shot; proposes a
  stat reroller. Dev: "in PoE/le crafting is super random, here you can easily get what you want"; crafting drops scale
  with Party.High^0.5; "legendaries can still roll between 50 and 166%"; offers a pity system; gems in shops for RtI.

- 2024-09-19 dev: "GM regen has to be a troll exclusive mechanic"; Dragon may get GM cover/BB, "I would need to run a solo
  dragon". 2024-10-21 dev: dragon "just fixing dps works". 2024-11-02: dragon tank does little damage, blast nerf talk.
- 2024-10-19 dev: cap shaman water reduction at 75 % (solo shaman near-invulnerable); Hagnak 2024-12-14: shaman was "the
  easiest class for soloing ... best entry class"; rework made water DR scale with XP gained, broke early solo.

## 5. Ideas fitting the goal, and rejections/reverts

Ideas:
- Solo-only rules already accepted by the dev: GM misc skills, auto alchemy, unlimited utility scrolls, halved debuffs.
- Dungeon reset / D2 farming for solo Doom (Aku; dev added it, later disabled in RtI).
- Monthly bounty-board dungeon respawn at your level (MR___TJ).
- Solo/party leaderboard categories in the MM8 arena (dev).
- Loot level max(map level, party level/2) (Albedo); stat reroller and pity system for crafting (Albedo; dev open).
- Rawsugar: past lvl 300 everything should be lvl-300 content / forgiving post-300 curve.
- Dev's own next game (2025-03-06): talent tree + "maw like itemization (a bit more deep and less power creeping, similiar
  to POE but up to 4 enchants)". Shows he likes talent trees and PoE-style items, with less power creep.
- Solo thief/"summoner" classes (dev: thief with combo points and stealth "more like a solo Class"; summoner "could be fun
  but I'm afraid it gets boring quite quickly").
- MR___TJ: shield-type spells (Celestial Armor, overheal shield) against one-shots.
Rejected or reverted:
- Old XP formula for solo (asked by Aku/Hagnak, 2024-06): kept the new one; aim is to stop out-levelling difficulty.
- Free map farming in RtI: maps gated to lvl 300 (2024-10-26).
- Dungeon resets and map respawns in RtI; monster resurrection removed (2024-10-22, "It is called Insanity").
- Big loot/density/AoE nerf of 2024-12-01 because "scaling went a bit too exponential".
- 100 % dodge (multiplicative cap), mistform immunity, uncapped shaman water DR (75 % cap): no full immunity.
- GM regen for non-trolls; regen for knight ("for solo you can drink regeneration potion").
- HP/MP-on-kill legendary nerfed to one proc per AoE.

## Most relevant for the solo-BM goal (top 10)

1. 2024-06-25 Malekith: solo has a mid-game power spike (cubes + full legendary), "After that solo falls off. A well
   build party will always outperform a solo player." The plateau our simulation sees is the dev's known model, not a bug.
2. 2024-12-27 tinywhitecat (solo RtI finisher): endgame dragons/devils one-shot "any character ... including with a
   dragon"; won only "with much endgame cheese". 2024-12-30 lvl-2400 solo shaman: "monsters wins no matter what I do".
3. 2025-02-23 Albedo: solo death spiral = incoming damage / self-heal > 1 with one action instead of five; "we take same
   damage as party"; "what is the point of level stacking then, if you never ever feel powerful?". Self-heal and DR, not
   damage, decide solo.
4. 2024-11-03 / 2024-12-28 catles: monsters out-scale players from ~lvl 550-600 on Doom and ~200-250 on RtI; "gear doesnt
   catch up to monster bolster"; item levels max out at the same level, so the curve flips.
5. 2024-12-05 Malekith: "scaling went a bit too exponential ... lower level monster insanity was actually very very easy,
   and higher level monsters were too hard"; he flattened loot (item power 66->40 %, enchants -37 %) and kept the top
   mode punishing on purpose (2025-02-24: "barely keep up with monster level pacing ... intended by design").
6. Solo-only rules are an established pattern the dev accepts: GM misc skills, auto alchemy (GM at 10), unlimited
   utility scrolls, permanent Torch/Wizard Eye, halved disease debuff, Party.High^0.5 compensation formulas.
7. Self-sustain gates solo: Malekith "Knight can't because has no self sustain"; DK blood leech, Troll GM regen, Darkness
   (leech+speed) weapons are what made solo work; "GM regen has to be a troll exclusive mechanic".
8. Farming history: dungeon resets added for Doom ("every party can eventually grind out"), then removed for RtI; maps
   gated to lvl 300 "because otherwise free farming"; Rawsugar: farming is an option to make it "a bit easier". An
   opt-in solo farming path fits past dev intent for Doom but conflicts with the top-mode stance.
9. 2024-06-03 XP formula change cut XP for out-levelled kills (Rawsugar: grinding "allowed player to basically bypass
   difficulty"); bolster counts solo XP /5 and "solo gets 2.2 times levels". Solo level advantage was deliberately damped.
10. No-immunity rule: dodge capped multiplicatively, shaman water DR capped at 75 %, mistform immunity removed. A solo
   power path should raise effective HP, sustain and DR inside caps (Albedo: stuck at ~75 % mitigation + 30 % block)
   rather than grant immunity; Malekith's own design taste is talent trees plus PoE-like items "less power creeping".
