# Notes: #balance-discussion slice 50_3 (2026-09-12 .. 2026-11-14, after MAW 5.0)

Source: chunk_50_3.txt (3640 lines, read in full). Dev = Malekith (MAW author); Rawsugar = dev. Others are players.
Regular posters: Kyx4578 (powergamer, party/solo, critic of 5.0), Freeday005 (melee party on BM, about 40 levels
behind), Archmage1 (caster-heavy BM party, level 250+), Ribald (BM parties at 266, 381 to 416, analyst), catles

## 1. What 5.0 and the 5.x patches changed, and why

- 09-12 catles: 5.0 "lost its gems and maps" ("shrinkflation"). Crafting gems and dimension maps were removed at
  release and came back later.
- 09-18 Malekith on the endless-arena potion drop rate: "I limited the endless brainless sustain". Freeday: arena
  monster levels went up (cannibals 50 -> 72; another +20 jump from wave 11 to 12).
- 09-19 Malekith, why gems were removed: "1: it was kinda of a chore, you get shittons of gems ... 2: once you get the
  legendary with right stats, you would upgrade that forever, making harder to get better items from loot / 3: I
  wanted to rework this to add gems sockets to items." Also "I liked the D3 gems much more ... simple but fun".
  Reading: the dev dislikes one item upgraded forever and wants new loot to stay relevant.
- 09-30 Hagnak/Freeday: removing crafting "kills melee builds" (no way to roll armsmaster/unarmed/dodge gear); Freeday
  says weapon-skill dps was halved. Legendary transfer orbs are expensive (about 9M for more orbs). Malekith: "I intend
  to fix it and give a proper way" (celestial rerolls).
- 10-04 Malekith: "I'm restoring gems and crafting cube ... gems will be slightly different." 10-13: "New gems are
  weaker, meaning that they help keep up older gear while keep the new loot relevant. You can still Max out items, but
  they only drop up to tier 16 gem. Others need to be merged to be obtainable." 10-09: the cube "is mostly valuable on
  weapons and body armor".
- 10-14 Ribald, armour-skill change (seraph physical damage reduction): before, Chain 1 = 56.47 %, Chain M50 = 65.69 %;
  after, Chain 1 = 47.51 %, M50 = 60.28 %. Armour skills are worth more, but the ceiling went down. Malekith: "monsters
  hit for less tho".
- 10-23 new boss affixes: Berserker (`damage *= 2 - hp/maxhp`, up to x2 at low HP), Unstoppable (CC immune), Cryptic
  (hidden), Skilled ("horseshoe boss": counts your dodge as 0, 100 % hit, drops 4 horseshoes = skill points; 11-12
  they teleport to the inventory). The catles "Coward" boss was rejected: "they can be unfair and are hard to balance".
- 11-01 Malekith on buffs no longer stacking (alchemy plus spell): if they stacked, "madness becomes trivial / less
  viable party compositions / people who follow the meta are forced into alchemy". "I prefer to have it balanced with
  lot of nuances and no clear winner." "I don't see any logical argument to have the same buff stacking twice."
- 11-03 Malekith: "increasing resistances will also make monsters deal more damage of that type, same for ac. I'll
  probably revert to 4.5 or close, which felt good". Monster damage is scaled against player defence.
- 11-04 Malekith found the big bug: "Melee had a bug in estimator calculation doubling the actual damage later on
  (almost no changes early), and I balanced monsters and casters around that ... monsters were balanced around melee
  having +50% skill bonus as enchant. Except there is no +weapon skill." Fixed, and casters rebalanced. Also "monster
  stats are balanced around melees, so any buff I do to melees need a couple of further steps". Reading: MAW tunes
  monster HP and damage from an estimator of a player's damage and defence at a level.
- 11-04 Malekith: the new damage formula "separates better the physical/magic damage accounting for player phys/magic
  resistance, instead of doing an average". "Mana shield ... has an higher pool than hp, but it's much harder to
  restore." "Note that new monster hp nerf make leech much stronger." Ribald saw elemental damage after the patch at
  +100 % (+150 % with a resistance ring).
- 11-08 Malekith (Ribald's XP and gear-level complaint): "with the new update should be good". 11-10 Freeday's summary
  of the state then: "Cubes are back gems are back / More lega drops / Monster hp is lower / Xp is fixed density is
  fixed". Kyx: "with the exp change, biggest issues were fixed ngl".
- 11-09 Malekith: "the issue with crafting materials is that I can't be too generous on them, as they don't have any
  level restriction".
- 11-10 Malekith agrees +magic skill should raise buff power up to its cap ("It wasn't there because buffs used to not
  have a cap"); "doing a balance rework on spells". 11-11: fire spike to get AoE, rogue an immobilise.
- 10-22 Malekith refuses dragon as a race for other classes ("too messy, it's a whole another layer of balance").
  Dragon gets Regeneration at Novice (leech for spells "as usual with half rate").

## 4. Melee and ranged, tankiness, damage floors, one-shots

- The longest debate in the slice (10-23, 11-03 to 11-06). Consensus: melee cannot stand in melee on BM. Monsters do
  much more damage in melee than at range; kiting is too easy; casters get AoE, CC, range and mana-shield toughness.
- Concrete numbers: Freeday's minotaur has 10,200 HP and 54 % mind resist; one Psychic Shock from a level 240 boss put
  him at -1k. Don Quixote's DK: 20 cover, 55 % res, 2k HP; a single broodling arrow knocks out the thief. Freeday's troll
  has 1,100 base regen at GM plus 3 % max HP heal on cover (about 1,000/s), 41k HP, and 90k "toughness" at 215.
  Archmage's seraph: 181k at 252, where about 40k damage is a kill. Kyx: "a single T3 can destroy a melee party".
- 11-08 Kyx on speed: in 5.0 speed gives "a (very very large) chance to dodge every attack"; casters stack it for "40-50%
  chance to dodge any damage, including any potential one shot". Melee gets nothing from it.
- 11-13 Kyx: monster non-physical damage is "several fold bigger than physical"; tanks hit the elemental resistance cap
  by about level 350 even without rings (Freeday: only with a shield above Expert). He proposes higher resistance caps
  from armour mastery (M leather +1 %, GM mail +2 %, M plate +3 %, M shield +1 %). Freeday: "2 dbreath kill an almost
  max res party" at about 85 % all resistances; "1 incinerate knocks out 3/5 party members".
- 11-13 Kyx: mana shield toughness exploit. A death/eradicate legendary ignores reserved mana and mana-shield mastery:
  a cleric dies after about 200,053 damage with M mana shield switched OFF against 76,548 for a life build. "the
  toughness multiplier it gives is gigantic".
- Strongest builds in 5.0 (Kyx 11-13): light magic (prismatic light, sun ray), dragons ("resistance agnostic, can't
  miss, too high a toughness for their damage, need very little resources in crafting mats"), bows (+40 % from the
  monster HP cut; carnage bow plus the %current-HP legendary counts several times).
- 11-03 Krishh: "'1 or 2 characters emerging alive' is just not really an outcome that maw's combat system allows for.
  Either your sustain can keep up with the damage ... or it's a rout".
- 10-01 Freeday: leech bosses are percent-based "supposed to punish tank builds"; Berserker punishes tanks, Unstoppable
  punishes CC.
- 09-30 Malekith (nobody one-shots past level 100): "it's because of the speed being too much".

## 5. Endgame and farm loops

- **Dimension maps** (Ribald 11-07): drop from about level 80. They can be held and run later (a map that dropped at 80
  was run as a level 240 map at party level 190). Up to 4 modifiers. Players reroll the area for high density (Mire of
  the Damned, Castle Darkmoor) and push them 30-50 levels above the party. Emeralds of Power raise the level; with 26
  emeralds a Murmurwoods map reached level 1700, and a cleric gave about 4k XP at his level, enough to "jump from level
  410 to 500 in just one map". Monsters reroll with each emerald. Malekith: "Limit are emeralds"; Ribald: the arena
  gives infinite gold, so emeralds are infinite. Malekith 11-01: maps start dropping after the main questlines are done;
  "I probably need to lower the level which maps start dropping in other difficulties". Simpelicity: "resetting a map
  is not nearly the same fun as maps".
- **Gear wall** (Ribald 11-07): "after a certain point, you can no longer upgrade your gear with loot because the level
  requirements become impossible to reach". Being under-levelled means waiting to equip the best finds. Archmage agrees.
- **Crafting** (Kyx 11-08, 11-13): pearls of memory (enchant reroll) are too rare and shops stop selling them past level
  200. "the correct way to gear yourself is getting items with the right stats, even if they are turbo low level ...
  and upgrade them all the way to endgame". Items above tier-16 materials cannot be rerolled (2-3 tries). Celestial
  rerolls are "awful", worse now that Transcendence gives no XP. He asks for a common reroll material or a
  seed-reroll NPC. Raphael: shop crafting-item rolls scale with carried gold: 20M+ = 100 % chance per slot, 10M = 60 %;
  pearls about 10 % of rolls; alchemy shops are best.
- Players ask for a pay-to-pick enchant NPC (Raphael and Sparten 10-12/13: gold plus gems instead of reroll
  save-scumming; Krishh: legendary-orb-style transfer for the 3 regular enchant slots).
- **Legendaries** used: 3 % damage reduction per nearby enemy up to 50 %, status immunity, +1 % damage, mana leech,
  double AC (adds to skill instead of multiplying), a meditation boost. Celestial rings can be mirrored for "+50% power
  and vitality".
- **Gold**: the normal arena gives about 1M in under 5 minutes (Freeday) or 150k per trip (Don Quixote). Malekith
  knows ("I can scale monsters I guess"). Archmage gave himself 30M with cheats; Don Quixote uses Cheat Engine for
  reroll gold. Players see gold as the bottleneck for masteries (M Mind 1.8M) and crafting.
- **Level cap**: mordormordor3 (09-24) says the hard cap is just over 2,000 (the 4B XP cap) and his mod raises it to
  32,000. Malekith: "Nice, I had the same idea but I didn't have time to implement it."
- **Alchemy**: weaker in 5.0 and no longer stacks with spells. Kyx: "alchemy is in a balanced state, while it was wildly
  overpowered before". Permanent potions and Transcendence (power 150 with alchemy 20 plus a booster) are still worth it.
- **Artifacts**: catles proposes D2-style class sets ("inferior artifacts by itself but strong as completed set").
  Malekith: "sets then become mandatory ... Once you find them you never replace that item slot."

## 6. Proposals that fit the solo-power goal, and dev rejections

- Fit: the 32k level-cap idea (dev liked it); dimension maps with emerald scaling as an optional farm (the dev's
  limit is emeralds); a reroll NPC or common reroll material (Kyx); pay-to-pick enchants (Raphael, Sparten); legendary
  orbs to move regular enchants (Krishh); D3-style gem sockets (the dev's plan); +magic skill raising buff caps (dev
  accepted); higher resistance caps from armour mastery (Kyx); a skill-tree-like "techniques" layer for martials
  (Raphael and Archmage, "a significant undertaking"); a reverse-piñata boss whose loot grows (Malekith: "could be fun").
- Treasure-goblin and "Moneybags" bosses: Malekith says "the issue is that you scale fast and you are rewarded for going
  in low level dungeons, not killing the big one".
- Rejected or resisted by the dev: stacking buffs (alchemy plus spell); class artifact sets (become mandatory); the
  Coward boss; dragon as a race for other classes; generous crafting materials ("they don't have any level
  restriction"); an endless gem upgrade path (the reason gems were cut at first).
- Dev stance on power: overpowered is fine up to Doom; BM is the minmax challenge. Malekith: "maw changed so much
  through the time, I'm not particularly attached to anything".

## Most relevant for the solo-BM goal (top 10)

1. **The XP penalty hits solo hardest** (Kyx 11-14): level x 1000 plus a penalty past level 100 (quoted as +0.5 % per
   level, but his 150->151 = 225K example fits +1 %). Solo's early level lead does not survive it. Check
   zzzMaw_Experience.lua.
2. **Only a dragon has soloed 5.0 BM** (Archmage 11-14); the same player quit a knight solo. Kyx: other classes get
   "randomly one shotted" even with 70+ Bodybuilding, and solo cannot absorb a one-shot the way a party can. This
   matches the sim (Dragon, Troll, Seraph only).
3. **Monster stats come from an estimator of player power** (Malekith 11-04: balanced around melee; a bug assumed a
   +50 % weapon-skill enchant). Raising player resistance or AC also raises that monster damage type (11-03). A solo
   power path must sit outside the estimator, or the estimator must ignore it, or monsters will scale it away.
4. **Magic damage and resistance caps make the one-shot wall**: about 85 % all resistances and still 2 dragon breaths
   kill a party; caps reached by about level 350. Kyx proposes higher caps from armour mastery. This is a direct
   solo-only lever.
5. **The dev's intent**: overpowered is fine up to Doom, BM is "absolute minmax, push your builds to the limit"; buffs
   never stack ("madness becomes trivial"). Solo-only power is not in MAW's design, so OpenYAMM should label it a solo
   variant.
6. **Dimension maps are the only real power farm**: held maps, 4 modifiers, area rerolls, Emeralds of Power up to level
   1700, about 90 levels in one map. Malekith: "Limit are emeralds"; arena gold makes emeralds infinite. Even so, the
   best BM party ended at about 416 of a presumed 500. A solo farm loop needs its own rate.
7. **The gear wall**: past some point loot level requirements exceed the player's level (Ribald); reroll materials are
   scarce (pearls), and items above tier-16 cannot be rerolled. The rational play is to upgrade low-level items forever,
   which the dev wanted to stop. Solo crafting additions (seed reroll, pick-an-enchant, uncapped cube tiers) target
   this directly.
8. **Mana shield, speed-dodge and leech outscale HP**: speed gives casters 40-50 % dodge; mana shield plus the
   death/eradicate legendary gives about 2.6x effective HP (200k against 76k); the monster HP cut made leech "much
   stronger". These are the existing multiplicative defence levers a solo tree could build on, or must cap.
9. **Players asked for, and the dev was open to**: a 32k level cap (dev: "I had the same idea"), +magic skill raising buff
   caps (accepted), D3-style gem sockets (dev plan), a reroll NPC or legendary-orb enchant transfer. The dev rejected
   class artifact sets (mandatory BiS) and generous materials (no level restriction).
10. **Strongest archetypes are ranged and self-sufficient**: dragon (resistance agnostic, can't miss, high toughness,
    cheap to gear), light magic (prismatic light, sun ray), carnage bows. Troll GM regen (about 1,100/s plus 3 % max HP
    on cover) is the best melee sustain. Melee solo is "in the gutter in the second half". Solo tuning should lift the
    melee and sustain classes most.
