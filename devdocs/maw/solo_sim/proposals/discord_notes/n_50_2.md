# MAW #balance-discussion, 2026-08-20 to 2026-09-12 (chunk_50_2), notes for the solo-BM goal

Context of the slice: MAW 5.0 is in public beta/balance phase ("feature complete, balancing phase", Keyen 08-24).
Almost all play reports are on **Madness** (and RTI). **Beyond Madness is mentioned only once, in passing**
(no clears, no walls reported for BM in this slice). Roughly a third of the slice is a flame war with a troll
(mordormordor3); his claims are not used here except where a dev answered them.

Roles: Malekith = MAW developer. Keyen = veteran tester (about 500 h, "first person to deathless madness", soloed
Madness deathless on Dragon). Freeday005, Hagnak, catles, Archmage1 = experienced players. Rawsugar does not post in
this slice.

## 1. What 5.0 changed, and why

- 08-21 Malekith (dev): **the XP curve changed.** "before solo level was a constant of 2.25 compared to party, with an
  average of 84 monsters to kill to level up from level 100+ (constant)"; "now monsters to kill are around 84 at level
  100, 168 at level 200, 252 at level 300 and so on, so solo level gets increasingly closer to a full party level."
  Reading: the kills needed per level now grow linearly with level, which removes most of solo's level lead late on.
- 08-21 Malekith: "solo now is much harder. Levels requiring increasingly more experience it's a big nerf for solo specially."
- 08-21 Malekith: "getting to level 500 requires 50% more monsters killed than old 1000", and "level 1000 it's 5x than older
  1000"; "I think I'll just make level 1000 the cap." 08-26: "**madness cap at 500**" (the map unlock at 450 is a temporary value).
- 08-22 Malekith: "for experience, I want to change the system and make it so that when you level up, previous experience
  is removed. this should basically remove the cap." Reading: he plans per-level XP instead of a cumulative total, so levels stay open-ended.
- 08-20 Malekith: "Mastery learning are calculated in the Monster progression curve, so if I make it earlier, it means
  monsters gets stronger earlier." Reading: monster power is keyed to *expected* player progression, not to the player's actual power.
- 08-27 Malekith: "technically 5.0 is more balanced, as monsters follow the player progression closer. The issue is that if
  monsters are on the hard side, people get stuck, if they are on the easy side then it's all good." Stated design intent.
- 08-29 Malekith: **regeneration formula**: `hpRegenRate = {0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.10}` by mastery;
  `hpRegenPerSec = rate * sqrt(expected * fullHP)`, `expected = getPlayerEstimatedHealth(skill^1.4)`. The estimate is
  "how much health you are supposed to have"; at that health you regenerate 10 %/s, at 4x that health 5 %/s, at 1/4 of
  it 20 %/s. "it's an estimate I use **also to calculate monster damage**"; "at level 100 it's around 1500 HP, level 200
  6700 HP"; it "changes based on difficulty", and not with bolster level. Reading: monster damage is pegged to an
  expected player HP per level and difficulty, so HP above that estimate is the lever for out-scaling.
- 08-29 Archmage1 (screens from the dev build): the Regeneration spell's bonus is capped at about +3 % of max HP per
  second, even at 500 regeneration, level 500 and 100 GM Body. Keyen: Dragon regenerates about 7–7.5 %/s.
- 08-29 Malekith: "healing has been reduced as well"; "in 5.0 you don't get as much damage".
- 08-23 Malekith: "Water reduction is capped to 75%"; "With current BB giving no % health, I think I might need to increase
  it to 0.5% at novice"; 08-21: "respeccing and getting to BB M is +50% vitality, I'm seeing that armor investments is a
  bit too weak tho", "resistances are a bit low". Freeday (08-22): "Putting a hardcap on resistances mean that after
  maxing out on them the only defensive option is to put more hp and mana or force melees into cc casting".
- 09-05 Malekith: "ring resistances is purposefully very strong and doesn't stack, meaning you ideally want one of each";
  for a player hurt by Nighon warlocks: "investing into shield more would help greatly".
- 08-23 Malekith: **density cut**: "insanity had 5-9 monsters per spawn node. now it should be 3-6"; 08-29: "current one,
  with 4.5 monsters per pack is a better spot than the previous 7". 08-28: the cut also means about 33 % less gold and
  pity progress, "but I nerfed monsters accordingly to the less loot". Keyen: "the density nerf made the game a lot better".
- 08-20/22 **Gold and loot**: Keyen: "gold income was more or less slashed by 2 across the board" (lower area levels mean
  lower gold); Malekith: "I didn't nerf gold directly", "training cost is reduced tho". Loot quality: many white
  items ("I loot only white items", gryz 08-21); Malekith: "Enchants a consequence of loot nerf".
- 08-27 Keyen's list of party *and* solo pain points: (1) "Monsters scaling faster than you do"; (2) "Crafting is in a
  ditch. Crafting materials are much rarer (got only one leg orb up to nighon tunnel), you can no longer upgrade items
  over time, you can no longer aim for a specific enchant ... as the only rerolling mats are the emeralds" (so resistance
  rings are hard to get and keep); (3) gold: "no way a party lvl 250 can pay for 4 M and 1 GM on each character (unless
  abusing arena)". **Malekith: "yeah that's likely. Still need to add the gem system."** Old gems were removed and new ones
  were not yet in ("With old gems removed and new gems not implemented", Victor 08-20).
- 08-27 Keyen: hourglasses gave stats above the item's current stats, so he crafted "items stronger than caped
  celestials in a gutted crafting system"; Malekith: forgot to set the hourglass to 1:1, "I believe it's like 1.1x".
- 08-26 **Maps**: removed from Madness progression. Malekith: "map questline will start at any oracle, maybe around
  level 60 or 100, and give your progressively harder maps"; 08-21: "You are given a set map and you need to clear it to
  get to next step ... after clearing all the questline you can drop maps freely", "with level steps". The Breach
  questline that requires items "can go away". 08-29 Keyen: "madness is without either respawns or maps in 5.0".
- 08-22 Malekith (leech/enchants): "giving everything means no actual choice"; he considered removing the Darkness enchant
  and the "1/n" skill-point rule (dual wield). Dual wield nerf (08-23): "the goal was to make other setup viable in party
  play, **in solo it's still busted due to the big amount of skill points**".
- 08-26 Malekith: considered nerfing alchemy buffs 20 % ("0.75 - 1.5 to 0.6 - 1.2 ... or even straight 0.5 - 1") or
  limiting potion buffs to 2 active; on solo: "**for solo it might be ok to have them all, but in a full party probably not**".
- 09-05 Weapon speed per mastery halved: dagger 1/1/1/2 → 0/0/0/1 (Malekith: intended), axe 0/1/2/2 → 0/1/1/1. The
  meditation rework and Ascension tuning are mentioned (Keyen 08-24). Monsters past the first two areas of each continent
  got ranged attacks to stop kiting abuse (catles 08-26); Malekith: "the bow attack is relatively weak", "arrows being
  strong is intended".
- 08-22 Malekith: artifacts get bonus damage again ("+10-20 light damage"); an artifact rework is in progress.

## 6. Ideas that fit the goal, and what the dev rejected

Fits:
- A solo skill-point multiplier at character creation, with solo at party level (Keyen; **Malekith agreed to work on it**).
- XP that resets each level, which "basically remove[s] the cap" (Malekith).
- A map questline with level steps from about level 60–100, then free map drops (Malekith); Freeday wants random enemies and
  maps "set to bolster level so you can't cheese it w party".
- Cover/Retaliation available solo (Malekith, done).
- Solo keeps all alchemy buffs while parties get limited ones (Malekith).
- Monster stats tuned for solo (Malekith, "might").
- Bounties as a small gold and boss-loot loop (Freeday).
- A gem system for crafting is still to come (Malekith).
- 09-01 mordormordor3 (troll, but the idea stands): "Grinding can resolve a lot of those issues. Class imbalance ... bad rng";
  Don QuixotEevee and Keyen answer that "Grinding is not challenge".

Rejected or pushed back:
- Minotaur crowns ("busted").
- Making masteries available earlier (it would make monsters stronger earlier).
- 08-22 Keyen: an XP multiplier "maybe it's not the kind of thing this mod is trying to endorse" (Malekith only explained how to edit it).
- Keyen: more S-rank skills.
- Leaving a dungeon resets it, and saves are limited, by design. Keyen: "The point is not be able to save after every pack".
- catles 09-05: "if a class has to be boosted to be able to clear madness it would make it op in party settings". This is the
  exact tension that a solo-only power path avoids.

## Most relevant for the solo-BM goal (top 10)

1. **Malekith (08-27): "game is balanced around party play, not single player"** but "I might ... change monster stats to
   account for solo a bit more". Solo-only tuning is a gap the dev acknowledges and has not filled.
2. **The 5.0 XP curve kills solo's level lead (Malekith, 08-21):** kills per level used to be a constant of about 84 from level
   100, with solo at 2.25x party level; now they are about 84/168/252 at levels 100/200/300, so "solo level gets increasingly
   closer to a full party level". Level 500 needs 1.5x the kills of the old 1000. "solo now is much harder."
3. **Malekith agreed to a solo mode at character creation with a skill-point multiplier**, solo at party level (08-22, 08-24).
   This is the dev-sanctioned route for solo power, and it fits a talent-tree or multiplier design.
4. **Monster damage is computed from an expected player HP** (`getPlayerEstimatedHealth(skill^1.4)`, about 1500 HP at level 100
   and 6700 HP at level 200, difficulty-dependent), and regeneration is pegged to it (10 %/s at the estimate). HP or
   mitigation above the estimate is the only real way to out-scale. The 10 % floor in our simulation makes HP the binding
   stat, which matches.
5. Monsters "follow the player progression closer" by design; masteries are part of the monster curve, so out-scaling
   "is not possible anymore" (Keyen). A solo-only multiplier outside that curve (talents, crafting tiers) does not get
   cancelled by monster scaling.
6. **A maxed, cheesed solo Knight was walled on 5.0 Madness** around level 160–228: level-180 liches averaged 33 % max life
   per hit and one-shot him; he had to split-pull every pack. The cause was no crafting, white shop items and few resistance
   rings. Our simulation's BM wall is consistent with this, one tier lower.
7. **Crafting and farming were gutted in 5.0**: no item upgrading over time, no targeted enchant rerolls (only emeralds),
   rare orbs, half the gold, no maps and no respawns on Madness. Malekith: "Still need to add the gem system". The
   planned map questline (from about level 60–100, then free maps) and gems are the dev's intended farm loop, and they map
   onto dimension-map farming and crafting additions.
8. **Classes that do well solo have self-sustain or flat reduction**: Dragon (about 7 %/s regeneration, deathless solo
   Madness, "you can actually tank shit in melee now"), Shaman (Water reduction capped at 75 %, Air/Water flat reduction
   of about 80 % against packs), Seraph or Knight with Cover/Retaliation (about −20 % damage). Dagger, bow and caster classes without them
   fall behind. This matches our Dragon/Troll/Seraph result.
9. **Malekith's solo concessions so far**: Cover/Retaliation working solo ("very strong, not op"), alchemy buff stacking
   "ok for solo", dual wield "still busted in solo due to the big amount of skill points", a solo quest-XP change ("massive
   difference", Keyen). Precedent: solo-specific multipliers on skill points, XP and buffs are acceptable to the dev.
10. **A perfect character needs about 8 legendaries and 8 primordials (Malekith)**, plus a pity system, legendary transfer orbs and
    resistance rings that are "purposefully very strong and don't stack" (one of each element). A solo endgame loop should
    focus on reaching and then exceeding that kit, for example targeted rerolls (Zage's removal-gem celestial rerolls in
    4.0), more transfer orbs and power past the item caps, rather than raw level.
