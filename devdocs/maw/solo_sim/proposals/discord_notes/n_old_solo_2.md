# MAW #balance-discussion, solo / Madness slice 2 (2025-05 to 2026-07, MAW 4.x)

Source: `out/chunk_old_solo_2.txt` (3369 lines, all read). Malekith = MAW dev, Rawsugar = dev. Others are players.
Naming in this period: difficulties from low to high are roughly Normal/MAW, Nightmare, Doom, Road to Insanity
("RTI", "Insanity"), then **Madness** (new in Aug-Sep 2025; players also call it "Beyond Madness"/BM by Oct 2025).
Important scale note: 4.x Madness used *zone levels up to ~1000* (Dagger Wound 2, Castle Ironfist 37, Regna 450-550,
The Pit 625, Escaton's Palace ~950-1000) and players finished at level 1650-2025. Numbers are not directly
comparable to 5.0's BM (monsters ~321).

## 2. Difficulties: intent and changes

- Insanity skill thresholds 8/20/32 (E/M/GM); Doom 6/12/20 (2025-05-02, 2025-08-06). Insanity: no dungeon respawns,
  "supposed to be difficulty that should not be completed by not optimized parties" (Hagnak 2025-05-15).
- 2025-05-15 Malekith: Insanity treasure maps "start at level 300+ (instead of 100), so you are not supposed to beat
  them during the campaign"; "insanity isn't supposed to give an easy way of farming"; bounties limited to 1/month.
- 2025-08-30 Hagnak: "Even road to insanity was meant to be uncompleteable with some characters or parties. In the end
  it was able to complete with any combination." -> design intent drifted toward "all can finish".
- 2025-08/09 Madness launch: bolster off, fixed zone-level roadmap ("x*3, where x is the range of the map level"),
  no XP/loot from bounty monsters or any arena ("you farm endless for the gold, bounty for gold and exp from quest"),
  dimension maps drop from ~level 100, hold limit (3, later removed), pity system for legendaries/maps/materials,
  death counter. BootsandCats: "literally designed to be only for the most optimal parties"; "no one has even beaten
  it yet". Freeday 2025-10-18: "Madness is ment for over 1k levels".
- 2025-09-12 Malekith "madness gatekeep": quests scale off map level not bolster, quests don't count toward bolster
  ("no punishment at all clearing them early on"). Map-access gating was argued against by players.
- 2025-10-07 Malekith hit formula: hit chance = (5+lvl*2)/(10+lvl*2+AC), "except in AC is divided by bolster amount,
  which is 1 baseline, 6 for madness". Offered "make chances up to 50% at most ... and everything else is added as
  extra reduction". Malekith: "diablo lost control over his numbers ... numbers kinda lost relevance, and it became only
  order of magnitude".
- 2025-10-16 Malekith: will release 4.0 once Madness is stable.
- 2025-11-07 Malekith: "with maps considered, if you can get kinda precisely to level 1000 I'd say madness is ready
  for official release" -> level target ~1000 at end of campaign.
- 2025-12-02 Malekith: further difficulty would be a "game mode, there is no point in increasing numbers further when
  you have madness" (limited-resources mode, randomizer in Jan 2026).
- 2026-01-20 Malekith: Doom vs normal MAW "Much harder, but close to nightmare".
- 2026-02-07 Malekith: "madness becomes easier once level 100 / with players not even needing maps"; "monsters becomes
  easier and easier, which is counterbalanced by density multiplier". 2026-02-16 Freeday: "scaling is back to hardest
  version ... too dmg sponge without % dmg"; Malekith: "ok, that's bad".
- 2026-02-03 Keyen: "Average party does not clear madness / Optimised party clear madness with a high deathcount";
  only 4 people had cleared it with a party (as of 2025-09). VictorDragonslayer: "Highest difficulty is about figuring
  out some unholy minmaxing strat."

## 3. Power curve and scaling

- Exp formula: 2025-07-29 ohanar: monster XP scales quadratically on monster/player level ratio, proposed linear.
  Erbse: curve should "Never close in to 0 ... Never take off towards infinity". 2025-09-14 Malekith Madness ideas:
  more XP for first map completion; "remove cap of experience gained from higher level monsters (but change the
  formula)". 2025-10-05: Madness XP simplified to monster base exp divided by party members.
- XP hard cap: 2025-09-30 Malekith: experience clamped to 2^32 "at level 2700ish ... or it goes negative".
- Gold is the solo/late bottleneck: training cost (gryz lvl 902: 5M gold ~30 levels; Freeday at 560: 10 party levels
  cost 4M). BootsandCats proposed cheaper Master/GM skill cost increments for Madness; Malekith "this could work",
  "+50% instead of +100% cost might work", "I'm happy with people having a goal even after getting all the skills".
  Keyen 2026-02-05: "solo is barely held back by money"; party problems "99.99999% ... gold".
- Damage reduction: 2025-10-07 Malekith: "150 AC reduce the same amount of 100 res". fortego: AC much harder to stack
  than resistances (one ring + potion + Shield solves magic); evade chance "too random". Dragon/players: physical late
  damage "bonkers". Malekith 2025-10-05: immunities may become "-75% damage taken, preventing some cheesing".
- Resist reduction: 2025-10-04 Malekith: "from 900 to 800 is the same than from 100 to 0" (multiplicative logic).
- Leech: 2025-08-24 base leech halved (double-coded bug), spell leech nerfed heavily, darkness+vampiric weapon don't
  stack. 2025-09-03 Malekith on Madness vampiric: "monsters are much higer level ... I will think of something to make
  fighting higher level monsters less punishing"; idea "leech to always heal at least leech%/5 of your max HP".
  2025-12-02: "leech to grant at least 1 remaining HP ... and poison hit the mana shield". 2026-06-24: "The strong
  thing about leech is more about it not requiring heavy skill points investment, same for mana on kill".
- Leech/poison as % max HP: 2025-05-16 fortego (lvl 291 vs 144) took 25 % HP per leech tick; proposed level-difference
  protection or % current HP.
- Mana shield math (Ark Tolei 2025-08-18): lvl 300 Elementalist 2464 HP vs 21924 SP; each SP worth 5.1 HP at GM mana
  shield, effective 111,812 HP; HP gear loses >=90 % of its value vs SP gear; legendary that bases death threshold on SP.
  catles 2025-10-09: at ~500 Madness "90% of mana is consumed by mana shield".
- Spells: 2025-12-25 Malekith: spell damage x 1.015^ascension. 2026-02-08 catles: ascension ~75 % flat / 25 %
  scaling -> 60/30. 2025-09-08 Malekith: Intellect -> spell damage and crit damage, Personality -> spell damage and
  cost reduction (Insanity and Madness only).
- Items/legendaries/crafting: Crafting Cube ring needs char level 235 (fortego); gem caps per slot (weapon 2x,
  chest 1.5x, rings 0.75x). Legendary pity system: 2025-11-18 Malekith "you are kinda guaranteed to have all of them
  (in madness)"; "maybe a bit too much on the heavy side, but I think it's ok". VictorDragonslayer: "The fact that some
  legendaries are needed means that balance leans too heavily towards them." Must-haves cited: +50 % resistances,
  +50 % base stats (multiple copies), +% base HP damage, mana leech, 3 double-resist rings with "base enchants 50 %
  stronger". Krishh: mirroring a celestial with a good legendary (1 celestial orb + 1 legendary orb + 3 mirrors) gave
  4 identical +50 % staffs. 2025-10-16 Malekith on late crafting materials: "I think they are too much".
- Artifacts: 2025-09-12 "extremly weak after level 100" in Madness. 2025-12-07 Malekith idea: "make artifacts much
  stronger at the moment you drop them / but not let them scale with level". 2026-07-29: artifacts made
  deterministic/rerollable.
- Dimension maps (Madness endgame farm): 2025-10-02 fortego: maps "painful and unrewarding" (regen, resist, slow
  modifiers); catles: map modifiers add 10-20 % levels; density and on-death-spawn mods are beneficial. 2025-10-02
  Malekith: undecided, "could remove map rewarding with other maps and give exp+crafting instead". 2025-10-09
  Malekith: more gold from maps "specifically for madness". 2025-10-18 BootsandCats: "my run is pretty much at a dead
  end ... level 280 / no maps ... no way of getting more resources". 2025-10-24: maps customisable, rerollable, no
  hold cap. 2026-02-07 Keyen: "make it so maps can drop maps / and people will farm it". Freeday: "map level chance
  [start] 50 / all enemies receive dmg and hp buff ... / maps are infinite again"; no same map twice in a row.
- Farming sources and limits: endless arena (gold only in Madness), Lord arena 80k/week, bounties 1/month (no XP in
  Madness), Kriegspire well (+30 levels temporary, 50k XP for 50k gold/30 days), Kriegspire energy drakes (~600 cap),
  monster spawns stop after 500 generated. Krishh 2026-06-23 wished Madness "designed more around RTI's no farming".
  Keyen: "you are not allowed to farm at all in early game while not farming past mid game is only a choice".
- Difficulty curve: Keyen: "maw is by far the hardest in early mid game"; "Madness is all about staying ahead of the
  curve ... at some point, you can just lack damage or be one shot, with no obvious way to progress". catles solo
  curve: "a bit harder 1-20, a lot easier 20-80, a bit harder 80-140, a lot easier 140-1000".
- Bosses: Broodlord 2x HP 1.5x damage, Omnipotent gets all traits; fortego lvl 781 in a 373 zone lost 80 % HP per
  Broodlord hit; Omni Titan killed only by tanking with a wisp. Freeday: "Its not meant to be tanked".

## 4. Melee vs ranged/caster, tanks, classes

- 2025-11-07 Malekith: "melees are king of single target damage / caster can compare to melee only with shotgun
  spells". 2025-10-30 Malekith: "attacking fast in solo is very important indeed". 2025-11-18: casters "roasted into
  oblivion without the legendary" after ~250 (Freeday).
- 2025-08-29 Malekith: new monster damage formula "reduces the gap between a tank, a bruiser and a glass cannon".
- Archmage1 2025-11-12: "tanking is not really an option in a solo game"; Keyen: sustain (leech) = power x tankiness.
- Keyen 2025-10-27 solo Knight: "you absolutely need the spear (any setup with spear will absolutely crush anything
  else)". Knight best for solo Insanity (fortego): master bow early, GM plate/shield, M Bodybuilding, GM axe.
- Dragon: Jrneli "amazing early game decent mid terrible late"; dragon scaling uses bolster level; no leech, no dodge,
  few items; Malekith 2025-12-19 "No idea how, for solo regeneration is the only way to go".
- Seraphim: Light damage adds flat amount, scales with sword %, might, crit (Malekith 2025-05-01); attack bonus falls
  behind with levels (Reiska 2025-12-02).
- Shaman: many mitigation sources, best solo class by several accounts; Malekith wants shaman nerf.
- Thief: post-rework could "freely clear madness" (catles); best bow user per Keyen.
- Troll: regeneration 3.4 HP/s at start; Hagnak solo Madness troll finished.
- Vampire: leech early; "everyone solo is forced into vampire" (Keyen); vampire leech formula was damage^0.7*0.25.

## 5. Ideas that fit the solo-BM goal, and rejected/reverted items

Fits:
- Malekith 2025-06-16: per-completion reward, "an unenchanted ring with a legendary effect of choice when doing a new
  game", coefficient "3-2-1" by difficulty, progression "first 2-3 easy, and then takes increasingly more time".
- Infinite map farming where maps drop maps, with harder monsters (Keyen, Freeday 2026-02-07).
- Malekith: leech floor (leech%/5 of max HP per hit), leech never takes you below 1 HP, poison hits mana shield.
- Leech/sustain via skills not gear (VictorDragonslayer 2026-06-23: "Too many things are tied to gear and legendary
  affixes"); Dragon leech via Dragon Ability skill (gryz).
- Cheaper late skill/training costs in Madness (BootsandCats, Malekith "this could work").
- Hit chance capped at 50 % with remainder as extra reduction (Malekith 2025-10-07); always-hit physical for control
  at high numbers (BootsandCats).
- Artifacts strong at drop but non-scaling (Malekith 2025-12-07).
- Solo archer "disengage" skill (Malekith 2025-10-23).
- Auto promotions by level (150/300, or by bolster for solo) (VictorDragonslayer 2026-02-02; Malekith "sitting on 1st
  promo for most of the game sucks").
- catles: monster ID skill lowering monster effective level, "solo use only".

Rejected / reverted / nerfed:
- Trading (infinite gold) removed 2026-02-05 ("trading is entirely deleted in practice").
- Bounty and arena XP/loot removed in Madness (2025-08-30); bounties 1/month.
- Solo x3 debuff duration reverted 2026-01-08.
- Dodge for Dragon: "not going to add dodge" (2026-06-23).
- Automatic promotions in Madness: Malekith "Nah" (2025-10-31), preferring a dungeon-level table for promotions.
- Invisibility scrolls: debated removal; Malekith "from lvl 100+ sounds good" (2025-08-30); "not sure invis is going to
  make it in 4.0".
- Solo celestial/artifact power: Malekith wants it nerfed (2025-12, 2026-02).
- Madness beatable by any party: "never said such thing".

## Most relevant for the solo-BM goal (top 10)

1. 2026-01-20 Malekith (dev): "solo madness is very hard on an absolute scale / but party is harder than that"; "in
   solo you are much tougher, but you can't escape oneshots" and damage "only reference level is monster level, not
   player". Outleveling never reduced incoming damage, so solo needs explicit mitigation tools.
2. 2025-11-07 Malekith: Madness was tuned so players end "kinda precisely" at level 1000; real solo runs reached
   1650-2025 with dimension maps (Keyen). Endgame was meant to be far above zone level, and maps were the lever.
3. 2025-10-06 fortego solo Seraphim lvl 1079 (378k HP, 95 % resists): physical damage from monsters 400 levels lower
   still took 10-20 % HP, bosses 2-3 hits. Resists were easy to cap, AC/physical was not. Same failure as our floor.
4. 2025-10-07 Malekith: hit chance (5+2L)/(10+2L+AC) with AC divided by 6 in Madness; offered "chances up to 50% at
   most ... everything else is added as extra reduction". A dev-endorsed shape for a late physical-mitigation path.
5. 2025-09-03 / 2025-12-02 Malekith: leech floor (at least leech%/5 of max HP), leech cannot drop you below 1 HP,
   poison hits mana shield; "Regeneration is the way to go for solo"; leech "would really make sense" for solo Dragon.
   Sustain that scales with max HP was the dev's own fix for fighting higher-level monsters.
6. Solo concessions already existed: extra skill points ("almost make you as strong as a full party"), free GM misc
   skills, infinite TP/Fly/Torch/Wizard Eye, faster solo Dragon recovery, CC recovery halved in solo (proposed),
   x3 solo debuff duration (reverted). Precedent for solo-only rules.
7. Successful solo BM-tier builds: Shaman (most mitigation sources, 0 deaths), Knight with spear ("hidden op"), Troll
   with only the %-HP-damage legendary, Archer/Vampire with leech. Common thread: %-HP damage, leech/regen, stacked
   mitigation, fast attacks ("attacking fast in solo is very important").
8. 2026-02-07 Keyen/Freeday: "make it so maps can drop maps and people will farm it"; maps infinite again with
   harder monsters. BootsandCats stuck at 280 with "no maps means no way of getting more resources". Farmable dimension
   maps were what unblocked late runs.
9. Gold and training cost were the solo late bottleneck (gryz lvl 902: 5M gold = ~30 levels; Malekith "a common solo
   issue"); proposals for cheaper Madness skill costs got "this could work".
10. Dev intent against solo power creep: Malekith wants solo artifacts/celestials nerfed ("OP stats until level 1000
   ... not intended", "2.2x levels ... double the power") and never promised every build beats Madness. A solo power
   path should come from earned or ground progression (talents, crafting, maps) and not from level-scaled loot alone.
   His NG+ idea fits: a legendary-effect ring per completion, scaled 3-2-1 by difficulty.
