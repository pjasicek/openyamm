# MAW deviations

Every place where OpenYAMM's MAW ruleset deliberately differs from MAW `ef23ee17`: what MAW does, what we do, why, and
where it is specified. Unit tests encode our behaviour, not MAW's. See [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) §4.
