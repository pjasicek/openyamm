## L0 Pitch

A warlord's mercenary army has dug into a walled stronghold near Silver Cove. The front door opens
only into a guard tunnel: the real way in is through a blank wall at its end into an empty cellar of false doors
that drag the party between cells, then up through the store cellars and barracks into a vast brazier-lit muster
hall packed with soldiers. Beyond the hall is the warlord's own wing, where a drawer in his study holds the key to
the small vault with the Eagle Statuette, one of the statuettes the party carries to a pedestal for a continent-wide
quest. Upstairs, a long gallery of barracks leads to a throne room held by the fortress's elite knights. A switch at
the foot of a back stair lifts a portcullis that opens a short way back to the entrance. Play is room-by-room
melee against human soldiers in three ranks (thugs, men-at-arms, plate knights), in great numbers, with chests in
every barracks. Mood: martial, crowded, torchlit stone and timber, a lived-in garrison rather than a ruin.

## L1 Plan

### Identity and purpose

- Held by the warlord's soldiers: monster slot 1 leather-clad thugs (low), slot 2 chain-mail soldiers (mid),
  slot 3 plate knights (high; the top variant is a strong late-game knight). No named boss actor; the throne room
  and the warlord's antechamber carry the strongest knights instead. Map difficulty high for all three slots,
  groups of 2–4.
- Why the party comes: to recover the **Eagle Statuette** (quest item; finding it sets the quest bit and the seer's
  hint bit), and for loot: the fortress is a rich treasure map (top treasure tier, about 20 containers).
- Map card: treasure tier 6 of 6, lock 5, trap 6, refill 672 days, random encounters 10 % (mostly thugs).

### Entrance and exits

- One exit: a large double door in the gate tunnel back to Silver Cove; the party arrives just inside it. No other
  exits or drops. The entrance is the **0 band**: the door sits at about +128 in a ramped rock tunnel whose floor
  runs from about +370 down to 0, then keeps descending to the cellar band.

**Legend.** Size by floor area (units²): closet < 0.15 M, small 0.15–0.5 M, medium 0.5–1.2 M, large 1.2–2 M,
great > 2 M. Bands are floor heights relative to the entrance: cellar −256, entrance 0, ground +256, upper +512,
dais +768. Ceilings 256 above the floor in most rooms; the great hall rises to +768, two upper rooms to +896.

Layout at plan level: a stepped fortress about 19 × 14 grid cells that climbs from south to north. The entry
tunnel runs along the west edge; the cellar sits in the south-west, the ground-floor barracks along the south, the
great hall fills the east side, the warlord's wing is its north-east corner, and the upper floor covers the
north-west half. Wings sit side by side rather than stacked; only the back stair and gallery pass over lower rooms.

### Critical path

E1 → E2 → E3 → E4 → (secret wall) → C1 → C3 → C5 → (door) → G1 → G2 → G5 → H1 → (door) → W1 → W3 (take the
Warlord's Key and the first Storage Key from the drawers) → (key door) → W4: open the chest, Eagle Statuette.
About 15 rooms and 4 doors, through the two heaviest fights (H1, W1). Way out: back through H1 → H2 → U1 → U2 → U3,
throw the switch, portcullis up, E2 → E1.

### Loops and shortcuts

- **Big ring**: entry → cellar → ground floor → hall → upper gallery → back stair → portcullis → entry. Closed until
  the switch at the foot of U3 is thrown from the inside; afterwards it is the fast way in and out.
- **Storeroom triangle**: H1 and H2 are open to each other; H3 sits between them behind two locked doors. Opening
  both closes a small loop. Every other branch is a dead end (barracks, cells, closets, treasury, vault).

### Locks, keys and switches

- **Warlord's Key**: chest of drawers in W3 (with Storage Key A). Opens the W3 → W4 vault door; consumed.
- **Storage Keys A and B**: A in W3's drawers, B in the throne room's drawers (U15). The two storeroom doors are
  "double locked": each needs both keys in the pack; the keys are spent when the second door is opened.
- **Switch** (wall button at the foot of U3): raises the portcullis between U3 and E2; stays thrown across visits.
- **Secret walls (4)**: E4 → C1 (rising wall, entry route), G3 → G4 (sinking wall, treasury), U5 → U6 and U15 → U16
  (rising walls, closets). Plain wall look, clickable, no hint text.

### Set pieces and puzzles (as rules)

- **Cellar of false doors**: five door panels set into cellar walls (C1, C2, C4 twice, C5) and a few unmarked click
  spots on the tunnel walls, floor and ceiling (E1–E4). Input: click. Effect: the party is moved to one of five
  landing spots spread over C1, C2, C4 and C5, chosen at random, never the clicked door's own; message "you are pulled
  through the door". No state; harmless disorientation. The tunnel spots are a second, accidental way into the cellar.
- **Lure door**: E4 has a door that only says it won't open; the secret wall is beside it.
- **Hidden axe**: one of H1's braziers, when used, gives a single good axe (treasure tier 6) once.
- **Statuette chest**: the first opening of W4's chest gives the statuette and sets the quest and seer bits.

### Population (groups of 2–4; 43 groups, about 130 soldiers)

- none: E1, E3, E4, all of the cellar C1–C5, G4, G5, H2, H3, W2, W3, W4, U1, U2, U3, U5, U6, U10, U13, U14, U16.
- pack (2 groups): E2, G1, G2 (thugs and soldiers); U4, U7, U8, U9, U12 (soldiers, most with one knight group).
- heavy: G3, G6, U11 (4 groups each, G3 and U11 with a knight group), H1 (8 groups incl. a top soldier and a mid
  knight: the densest room), W1 (3 groups incl. a top-rank knight). Boss-class: U15, 4 groups incl. a mid and a
  top-rank knight. Corridors, stairs and the cellar are empty; fights grow toward the hall, wing and throne room.

### Treasure

- G4 hidden treasury: 6 chests (one of them a shared footlocker, below) and 3 loose items: the richest room.
- Footlockers: 9 small chests in G4 (1), G3 (2), G6 (3), U4 (1) and U8 (2) that all open one shared container.
- Distinct chests: G3 ×2, G6 ×1, U4 ×1, U7 ×1, U9 ×2, U16 ×1; cabinets: W2, U6, U15 ×2, plus one cabinet type
  shared by W3, U5 and U15.
- H3 storeroom: 3 loose items (mid tier), no chest. Single loose items in G1, U11, U12.
- Quest and keys: W4 statuette chest; drawers in W3 (two keys) and U15 (one key). H1 brazier: hidden axe.

### Lights and dressing

Wall torches in nearly every room: 1–3 per room, 9 in the great hall, none in W4, U2, U3 or the closets (75 lights).
Braziers in H1 (8) and U15 (2). Barrels and sacks fill G1 and H3; bunks and beams in every barracks and W2.

### Verticality

Five bands over 1,152 units: cellar −256, entrance 0, ground +256, upper +512, throne dais +768. Every band change
is a stair or ramp (tunnel ramp, G1, G2, H2, U3, the W2 bed step, the U15 dais); no lifts, no drops, no pits. The
critical path climbs from 0 down to −256 then up to +256; the return goes up to +512 and down U3 to 0.

### Totals

38 rooms (about 32 M units² of floor, compact for MM6): 1 great, 5 large, 16 medium, 13 small, 3 closets.
19 doors: 11 ordinary, 1 single-key, 2 double-locked, 4 secret walls, 1 switch portcullis; plus 5 false teleporting
doors and 1 lure door.
About 20 container records (30 physical containers), 9 loose items, 43 monster groups, 2 loops.

## Designer notes

(a) Left for L2: kits per wing (rough cave tunnel, plain stone cellar, timber-beamed barracks, richer stone hall
and throne room); door kinds and sizes (small sliding doors, wide doors for the storeroom); the switch, chest and
cabinet models; false doors identical to real ones; room shapes (mostly rectangles, U1 a long gallery); light colours
and radii; loot tier per chest.

(b) Brief-gap candidates:
1. **Shared containers**: many chests opening the same inventory (nine footlockers, three cabinets) has no field.
2. **Random teleport sets**: "one of N landing spots, not your own" is a mechanism rule with no preset yet; landing
   spots need named anchors in rooms.
3. **Double-lock rule** (two items, spent when the second of two doors opens) needs a condition with item counts and
   a consume-on-condition effect shared by two doors.
4. **One-way-until-switched shortcut**: the flow graph must know the portcullis is opened only from the U3 side.
5. **Click spots on floors and ceilings** (not doors) carrying events: no way to say "unmarked clickable patch".
6. **Hidden item in a decoration** (the brazier's axe): the dressing list needs an "interactive decoration" entry.
7. **Population by rank**: a per-room field naming slot 1/2/3 and variant a/b/c (or any) per group, not just a count.

## Corrections from the L3 pass (2026-11-12)

Measured from the original while sketching L3; these override the body above, and `plan.yml` at L1, L2 and L3
carries them.

1. **No stacked rooms.** No room lies over another anywhere in the fortress, the upper gallery (U1) and the back
   stair (U3) included; "only the back stair and gallery pass over lower rooms" is wrong.
2. **U15 Throne room has no dais.** Its floor is flat at +512. The +768 surfaces are cabinet tops; the corner piece
   is a canopied throne seat (or bed). Band +512, no "dais up one band", no dais band.
3. **W2 Bedchamber has no raised floor.** Floor flat at +256; the +464 is the canopy of a four-poster bed (mattress
   about +320). It is furniture, not a bed step.
4. **E1 Gate tunnel** floor runs from 0 at the junction up to about +128 at the exit door, not from +370 down to 0.
5. **H3 Storeroom and W1 Antechamber** ceilings are at +640: clear height 384, not 256.
6. **H2 Hall stair** is a single 256-wide flight, not wide stairs.
7. **U1 Upper gallery** is about 9.5 grid cells long and half a cell wide (a 256-wide corridor, about 4,860 long),
   not 9 × 1 cells.
8. **G5 Guard post** lies south of the great hall and opens into its south wall, not west of it.

**Verticality, corrected:** four floor bands, cellar −256, entrance 0, ground +256, upper +512 (no +768 band).
Every band change is a stair or ramp (tunnel ramp, G1, G2, H2, U3); no lifts, drops or pits. The 1,152 units are
the span from the cellar floor (−256) to the highest ceilings (+896, U11 and U15), unchanged.
