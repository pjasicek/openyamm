## L0 Pitch

A ruined moon temple half swallowed by a cave system on the island where new parties first land. The way in is a long,
dark, winding cave of rough rock: two broad caverns with rock pillars to walk round, side pockets that climb to high
ledges or sink into a spider pit, and only bats, rats and spiders in small packs. At the far end, steps cut into the
rock become masonry and drop into the temple: a sunken octagonal nave under a tall open dome, a raised apse with the
moon altar, colonnaded side aisles, and two mirrored wings beyond, a dormitory and a library, held by a handful of
human temple guards far tougher than the vermin. Every few moments a pair of fire bolts sweeps the lane in front of
the altar from both wing corridors. A blank wall in the library lifts to reveal a hidden study. Three of the island
scavenger hunt's six items are here: the moon floor tile in the hidden study, the wealthy hat in a dormitory chest and
the longbow carried by the guard captain. Mood: damp natural cave giving way to violet torchlit stone; a first
dungeon, short to walk, easy to fight, with one sharp step up in danger at the temple doors.

## L1 Plan

### Identity and purpose

- Starter dungeon. Encounter slots: 1 bats, 2 rats, 3 spiders, all at the lowest tier, groups of 2–5. The temple
  wings hold four fixed human guards (three swordsmen and one stronger guard captain), several levels above the
  vermin; they are the dungeon's real fight and its only humanoids. No named boss.
- Why the party comes: the Emerald Island scavenger hunt (return six items to the judge). This dungeon holds three of
  them, each fetched in a different way: a floor item in a secret room, a fixed chest item, and an actor's drop.
  Secondary: first spell scrolls and two spellbooks from bookcases, a few low chests.
- Map card: treasure tier 0 (lowest), disarm difficulty 0, perception difficulty 0 (secrets easy to spot), one trap
  die, refill 672 days, encounter chance on rest 10 %, cave music.

### Entrance and exits

- One exit: a clickable mouth face at the top of a short rising ramp just south of the arrival (the cave runs north
  from the arrival; the exit is up the slope behind it), back to Emerald Island beside the dungeon entrance. No other
  exits, drops or teleports.

### Critical path

Mouth → entry tunnel → descent bend → south cavern → north cavern → temple stair → nave → apse → south passage →
south corridor → south door hall (double door) → anteroom → library → secret wall → hidden passage → hidden study.
15 rooms on the path (14 links), about 17,000 units walked; the nave is about 10,500 from the arrival. The
dormitory (hat chest, captain with the longbow) is the mirror branch, also about 17,000 away. Every scavenger item is
at the end of a wing; the cave branches are optional.

### Loops and shortcuts

- No loops or shortcuts in the room graph: a branching tree with three cave and two wing dead ends.
- Local circuits only: four rock pillars (two in the south cavern, one each in the north cavern and west descent),
  big enough to walk round and break line of sight. They matter for play and look; they are not graph loops.

### Locks, keys and secrets

- No keys, no levers, no locked doors. Both double doors open on a click from either side (each pair of leaves slides
  apart, about 160 each, with a small linked seam piece).
- Secret 1 (on the critical path): the library's east wall, flagged secret, lifts about 256 on a click from either
  side, opening the passage to the hidden study. Nothing else hints at it but the room's shape.
- Secrets 2 and 3: a secret-flagged rock face that is itself a chest, in the south cavern and the spider sinkhole.

### Set pieces and puzzles

- Fire-bolt crossfire: a timer every 2.5 game minutes fires two novice fire bolts at floor height in opposite
  directions along one north–south lane, from the far east end of the north corridor to the east end of the south
  corridor. The lane runs through both wing passages and across the apse between the altar and the nave steps, so
  anyone going between the nave and either wing crosses it. No switch disables it. A torch marks each emitter end.
- The descent into the temple: rough rock turns to cut stone on the stair, a straight flight down the nave's axis,
  the dome and its glow ahead; the nave sits lowest, the apse and wings one step-run higher. No stateful puzzles.

### Population

Spawn points by slot (each a group of 2–5): south cavern bats 1 (light); high east pocket rats 2 (pack); northeast
chamber rats 1 (light); northeast pocket bats, rats and spiders 1 each (mixed pack); spider sinkhole spiders 3 (heavy
for this map); nave rats 3 in the sunken centre and bats 3 round the apse and aisles (heavy); hidden study rats 1.
Fixed guards: library 2 swordsmen; dormitory 1 swordsman and the guard captain (toughest room). Empty: mouth, tunnel,
descent bend, east climb, north cavern, west descent and neck, and every temple corridor, aisle, door hall and
anteroom. Total 17 spawn points (about 60 monsters) plus 4 guards; density rises at the pockets and peaks in the nave.

### Treasure

- Dormitory: 5 chests in the far alcove, each 3–4 random low items; one also holds the **Wealthy Hat**.
- South cavern and spider sinkhole: 1 secret niche chest each, 3 random items (tiers 1–3).
- High east pocket 3 and northeast pocket 4 random floor items (tier 1–2).
- Library: 11 one-shot bookcases, each a first-circle scroll (Torch Light, Wizard Eye).
- Hidden study: 5 one-shot bookcases (two spellbooks, Torch Light and Awaken; Fire Bolt, Fire Resistance and Poison
  Spray scrolls) and the **Floor Tile with the moon insignia** lying on the floor.
- Guard captain: drops the **Longbow**.

### Verticality

Height range about 2,000 (deepest floor −900; highest ceiling about +1,100 over the high east pocket). Bands: high
(+300 to +700: high east pocket, top of the northeast pocket), arrival (0: mouth, tunnel), middle (−300 to −450: both
caverns, apse, aisles, wings), low (−550 to −900: nave, lower west descent, sinkhole). Slopes in the caves, steps in
the temple; no lifts, ladders, drops or pits. Clear heights: caves about 400–500, taller in the pockets; temple
corridors and wing rooms 384; nave about 640; dome about 1,350.

### Whole dungeon

30 spaces: 19 rooms, 9 corridors or passages, the apse (sub-room) and the dome (void). The original also has 30
sectors, grouped differently (the apse shares the nave's sector; the lifting wall's slot is its own). Floor about
38 M units² (caves 24, temple 14): small for MM7 by area (canonical median 73), near the starter-group median in
spaces. Footprint about 23 × 25 cells; the temple about 12 × 8 cells in the north-west, the caves south and east of
it. 7 door movers (2 double doors with seam pieces, 1 secret lifting wall), 3 door events, 51 clickable faces, 4
secret faces, 1 timed trap.

## Designer notes

### (a) Left for L2

- Theme per room: rough rock walls and floors for rooms 1–12 (one wall material dominating), masonry for 13–30 with
  the temple's furniture (beds, bookcases, tables, pillars), and where the material change sits on the stair.
- Mechanism detail: door leaf speeds and travel, the seam pieces' role, the lifting wall's travel and sound, trap
  spell level and timer period confirmed in game time.
- Lights: 21 violet torches, all in the temple (aisles 2 + 2, apse 1, corridors 3 + 3, anterooms 1 + 1, dormitory 4,
  library 2, hidden passage 2), caves unlit; red point lights, three up the dome's column, one in the hidden study.
  Also decoration, the altar's look, the guards' family and level, and item tiers per chest.

### (b) Brief-gap candidates

- **Circuits inside a room**: rock pillars give walkable loops the room graph cannot see. The format needs "pillars
  or islands per room" or the loop metric will call any build with a pillar ring a mismatch (or miss their absence).
- **Void rooms and sub-rooms**: the dome is a space with no floor above the nave; the apse shares the nave's space
  but plays as its own room. Needs a "void above" kind and a "zone within a room", or room counts drift by one each.
- **Mirrored wings**: one shape, different contents; a "mirror of room X" field would carry it cheaply at L1.
- **Traps that cross rooms**: the fire-bolt lane spans five rooms. The trap needs a path (from room A to room B),
  period, spell and strength, not a room.
- **Secret containers**: a wall-face chest differs from a chest in a secret room; needs "secret container".
- **Quest items by delivery kind**: floor item, named item in a chest, actor drop; each tied to a world-level quest
  (QBits on another map). Needs the world `story.yml` link from a room.
- **Fixed actors versus spawn points**, with strength relative to the slots; **one-shot bookcases** (one item each,
  from a list) as a container kind.
- **Floor relief inside a room** (slopes of 300–700 within one cave) versus flat masonry; and the **arrival–exit
  relation** (exit behind and above the arrival): a per-room relief field, arrival facing and exit offset.

## Corrections from the L2 pass (2026-11-12)

The YAML briefs (`mm7_7d06/L1`, `L2`) follow the original data where it differs from this prose:
- The library has 10 clickable bookcases, not 11. There are 15 in total, as 5 Torch Light and 5 Wizard Eye, not a random draw.
- The dormitory alcove is not raised. Its floor is level with the room, and the five chests are cabinet blocks around it.

## Corrections from the L3 pass (2026-11-12)

Measured from the original during the L3 sketch; the YAML briefs (`mm7_7d06/L1`, `L2`, `L3`) carry these, the prose
above does not:
- Dome (15): its top is about 1,400 above the nave floor (about +864 against the arrival datum), not 1,350; the
  cupola rises from a ring about 768 above the nave floor. Its three red lights sit low in the nave (about 224 above
  the floor), at the dome's mouth just above the nave ceiling, and in the dome's lower part about 500 below its top;
  "up the dome's column" overstates how high they reach.
- Moon nave (14): the rim round the sunken octagon is level with the aisles and the apse at −416; only the octagonal
  well is sunken, 128 lower at −544.
- Nave to apse (14–16): the 5 steps take only the middle of the side, on the axis (about 320 wide, within the middle
  512). The rest of that side is an open 128 ledge with no wall above it.
- West descent (10): floor about −470 where it leaves the north cavern down to −640, not −320 → −640.
- South cavern (4): floor −320 to about −150, typical −320; it never dips below −320 (not ±130).
- Entry tunnel (2): it heads north-north-east from the mouth (about 1,100 east over 1,800 north, about 2,100 long),
  not north.
- Footprint: left at about 23 × 25 cells. The original's floor spans about 22.5 × 24.6 cells (the dormitory reaches
  256 north of the L3 sketch's outline); the sketch's 23 × 24 comes from snapping to the grid.

## Corrections from the in-game comparison (2026-11-12)

- There are **no red lights.** The four light records read from the original (colour 31,0,0, radius 0) give no
  visible light in the game. The nave takes the violet glow of the aisles' torches. The hidden study is dark.
- Every room has the original's minimum ambient light, level 24.
