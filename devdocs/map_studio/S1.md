## Done

- **Link closures instead of include closures.** `openyamm-studio/tests/link_closure.py <source ...>` reads the
  game's own build objects and prints which sources a file pulls in at link time and where SDL, bgfx or bimg come in.
  It changed the picture of the cuts (below).
- **Cut G1** (unstaged, for review): the door distance function moved out of `game/events/EventRuntime.cpp` into the
  new `game/events/MechanismDistance.{h,cpp}`. `IndoorMovementController` now links with 8 sources instead of 83.
- **Cut G2** (unstaged, for review), in two moves: the Lua source helpers (script path candidates, overlay names,
  joining overlays, support and world scripts, the two text readers) out of `game/data/GameDataLoader.cpp` into the
  new `game/data/MapScriptSources.{h,cpp}`; and scene overlay discovery out of `game/maps/MapAssetLoader.cpp` into
  the new `game/maps/SceneOverlayPaths.{h,cpp}`.
  All three moves are move-only; the functions left the anonymous namespace and got a header. The new files are in
  `game/CMakeLists.txt` and `tests/CMakeLists.txt`.
  Checked after each: the game and the unit tests build; event, script and door unit tests pass; the scenarios
  `shared_fx/mm6|mm7|mm8_map_lifecycle`, `release/save_load_roundtrip`, `release/rebuilt_map_save`,
  `mm7/grand_temple_moon_cell_exit` and the slice walk pass. Not run: the full unit suite as a whole, MM9 scenarios,
  a load-time comparison. `GameDataLoader.cpp` also carries another session's unrelated pending edits (house table,
  MM9 dialogue rows); three assertions of `Mm9RudeDialogueTests` about the house table fail with them and are not
  from these cuts.
- **The studio builds, natively and for wasm:** movement and pathfinding (`IndoorMovementController`,
  `OutdoorMovementController`, both path map builders, `PathMap`, `PathPlanner`), `MapRegistry`, `SpriteTables`,
  the engine's `AssetFileSystem` with PhysicsFS, `Localization`, Lua 5.4.8 and the game's `ScriptedEventProgram`.
- **A loaded map in the core** (`core/MapLoad`): geometry, the scene with its overlays applied, the starting state,
  and the event script with its overlays, world scripts and support script, all through the game's own code.
- **`oymap audit <map> --world <w> --assets <root>`** (`core/Audit`): overview and wiring checks: events called by
  faces or decorations without a handler, handlers nothing can run, doors the script moves that do not exist or have
  nothing to move. All 247 shipped maps load and are audited in 38 seconds (`openyamm-studio/tests/audit_all.sh`).
  It finds the documented issues: Goblinwatch's events 38, 54, 57 and 58 without handlers; Gharik's Forge's door 53
  without vertices; the Mad Necromancer's Lab's handlers moving doors that do not exist.
- **Schema v0 extended** for the 60 gaps the four L1 briefs found (`briefs/SCHEMA_GAPS.md`), and the validator now
  checks every id reference and every named quest bit and item in all project files, indoor and outdoor. The briefs
  are at L1 and L2 in `briefs/<map>/L1|L2/` (openyamm-fe, now on L3); a second list of gaps (round 2) is open.

- **Reachability** (`core/Reachability`, `oymap audit --reach`): a flood over the game's path map from the party
  start, taking each step as the game's planner does, with the doors as they start and with every door out of the
  way (a door has two poses and which one clears its passage differs per door, so door faces are removed as
  obstacles and kept as floors in both poses). Slice 2 then 6 of 6 rooms; Temple of the Moon 22 then 29 of 30
  sectors; Warlord's Fortress 4 then 38 of 39; Goblinwatch 1 then 45 of 58 (the rest lie behind its teleporters);
  Gharik's Forge 5 then 22 of 47. Three to fifteen seconds per map, so it stays behind `--reach`.
- **`oymap probe <map> --from x,y,z --to x,y,z [--doors-open]`**: why the party can or cannot walk between two
  points (the failing sample, the reason, the blocking face with its texture, attributes, sector and event) and
  whether the planner finds a route.
- **`oymap metrics <map> [--reach]`** (`core/Metrics`): CALIBRATION.md's comparison metrics as JSON: room graph
  (sectors, links, components, loops), scale (floor area, sector areas and heights, doorway widths and heights,
  floor levels; outdoors building footprints, heights, nearest gaps, relief), mechanisms by kind (from the handlers'
  text, as the Xeen census does), density per floor area, palette by area, and the walked share. The four
  calibration originals are in `openyamm-studio/calibration/originals/` (evaluator only). Temple of the Moon agrees
  with its brief and the Xeen census (30 sectors, 29 links, no loop, 37.4 M floor, 7 doors, 51 clickable faces, 17
  spawn points, 4 fixed actors).
- **`oymap flow <map> [--event n]`** (`core/EventFlow`): every handler run in a Lua sandbox whose `evt` only
  records, once for each way its tests can come out (at most 48 paths and 24 tests a handler, an instruction limit
  against endless loops). Per path: the tests with their answers and the effects, written readably
  ("when [item 2177]: door 4 open"). The audit uses it for map variables that are tested but never set, and set but
  never tested.
- **`oymap solve <map>`** (`core/Reachability`): the lock-and-key solve. Walk from the start, use every event bound
  in the reached part (and the map's load and timer events), let the doors they move stand out of the way and
  their teleports carry the party, walk again, until nothing new opens. Prints the steps with what opened each and
  what it needs. The slice opens exactly as designed (hall; the two click doors; the lever's gate; the strong room,
  "needs item 2177") and `tests/run_slice.sh` checks that. Temple of the Moon: 22, 26, 29 of 30 sectors in three
  steps. Warlord's Fortress: 38 of 39 in five steps, needing items 2185 and 2191 (the two Storage Keys) and then
  2184 (the Warlord's Key), as its brief says. Goblinwatch: 45 of 58; the other 13 sectors are not reached and
  want a look with `oymap probe`.
- **Import** (`core/Import`, `oymap import <map> --to <folder>`): a shipped map as an overlay project: the map
  card with `base` and the neighbours its exits lead to, a derived plan (a space per sector with a floor to stand
  on, a link per pair joined by a portal, door or open, totals) and `ids.lock.yml` with the map's own sector, door
  and event numbers under stable ids. The geometry stays in the base map.
- **Scene writers.** The plan asked for writers "at parity with the parsers" (the editor's indoor one emits about
  150 of 279 keys). S1 takes the route that cannot lose a key: a scene is kept as its YAML tree and written through
  `core/CanonicalYaml`; `oymap scene-roundtrip` proves, for every shipped scene and overlay, that the written text
  says the same, is stable, and is accepted by the game's parser. Writers from typed data exist only where the
  studio makes scenes itself (a new indoor map, S0) and grow with authoring.
- **Renders** (`core/Preview`, `oymap render <map> --out file.png [--view plan|iso]`): drawings from map data with
  labels, PNG written by the core itself (no dependency, same bytes everywhere). Indoor: each sector's floors in
  its own colour with its number, portals green, door faces red with door numbers, the start, spawn points;
  isometric shows floors and far walls. Outdoor: terrain by height, water, building footprints with numbers,
  clickable faces, spawn points.
- **The solve waits for keys and reports faults:** a path that tests an item the map itself gives is used only once
  the party can have it; an event that can be reached but whose item lies only in parts that stay closed without
  it is an issue ("key behind its own lock"); an item no handler or chest of the map gives is noted as coming from
  elsewhere. Unreached sectors too small to stand in (Goblinwatch's 13 key slots) are listed apart. The flood no
  longer tests a step into a cell already stood on (Goblinwatch's solve: 35 seconds to 4).
- **`oymap build-map`**: the editor's `--headless-build-map` through the same `EditorDocument`, compiled in place
  after cut G5. `tests/build_map_parity.sh` builds against hard-link copies of the asset roots, because the build
  (like the editor's) writes `map_stats` and `map_navigation` rows into the root's editor folder.
- **Round 3 tool items** from the L3 sketches: overlapping rooms are refused; a project without an objects.yml
  start takes it from the plan's arrival; `diagram.file` must exist; `map_stats` got `alert_days` and `steal_perm`.
- **Round 2 tool fixes** for the briefs: item names in treasure, drops and chest contents are checked; `fmt` writes
  a text of several lines as a literal block; `oymap install` writes lock difficulty, music, environment and the
  full encounter slots and works without a reference row; `map_stats` rejects unknown keys.

## Known limits, carried forward

- **Mistake made and repaired:** the first `build-map` parity run built against the Hesper stage root itself and
  so rewrote its four outdoor rows in `map_stats.txt` (losing the world column) and `map_navigation.txt`, as the
  editor's build does there. The rows were regenerated with `hesper_stage/stage.py`'s own rules (35 columns,
  `hesper`); copies of the files as the run left them are in the session scratchpad only. The same runs upserted
  the `out01.odm` row in the repository's git-ignored `assets_editor_dev/engine/data_tables/` (what
  `terrain/mm8/prepare_land_fixtures.py` also does). If Hesper's outdoor maps misbehave, run `stage.py` again.
- **"Goblinwatch's key E checks key D's variable"** (research/TEST_MAPS.md) is not a fault: in GrayFace's
  decompiled original, events 20 to 25 test MapVar0 to MapVar5, one each. The note is corrected.
- **Flow graph:** the solve and `oymap flow` give the order, conditions and faults; the graph is not yet written
  into a project's plan as conditional links, there is no critical path to named goals, and one-way traps are not
  detected. Tests on quest bits and awards are listed as needs, not decided. Outdoor maps are not walked.
- **Import** derives a plan and ids; it does not turn handlers into wiring presets, and an overlay project is not
  compiled back to overlay files (S2).
- **Renders** are painter-sorted (isometric views of stacked maps can overlap wrongly) and unlit; no camera sets.
- **`oymap audit` and the other map commands under WebAssembly:** PhysicsFS does not start under Node. Asset
  access for wasm is S5's design; until then they are native.
- **Audit noise on shipped maps:** MM8's generated scripts move about 2 000 doors that do not exist, and MM7 and MM8
  have about 4 000 handlers nothing binds: true, but in need of grouping.
- **Schema:** the L2-depth gaps R2-G1 to G19 and the sketch gaps R3-K1 to K15 (paths with width, non-convex
  rooms, slopes, stairs, ceilings, pillars) are open in `briefs/SCHEMA_GAPS.md`; they belong to indoor and outdoor
  authoring (S3, S4), as does the proposed outdoor sketch format.

## The cuts

Four changes outside the studio, all unstaged, each a move of code with one exception noted:

| Cut | Files | What |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | `game/events/EventRuntime.cpp`, new `game/events/MechanismDistance.{h,cpp}` | The door distance function out of the event runtime. `IndoorMovementController` links with 8 sources instead of 83 |
| G2 | `game/data/GameDataLoader.cpp`, new `game/data/MapScriptSources.{h,cpp}`; `game/maps/MapAssetLoader.cpp`, new `game/maps/SceneOverlayPaths.{h,cpp}` | Lua source helpers and the two text readers; scene overlay discovery. Functions left the anonymous namespace and got headers |
| G5 | `engine/ImageAssetLoader.cpp`, new `engine/ImageAssetPaths.cpp`; `engine/render/CookedTexture.{h,cpp}`, new `engine/render/CookedTextureHeader.{h,cpp}` | Image path lookups and the header readers apart from the decoders (SDL, bimg); the cooked texture's signature, version and size constants into a header free of bgfx. **Not move-only in one place:** `findSurfaceTextureLogicalSize` (used by the editor only, never by the game) now reads the size from the file's header instead of decoding the image; a cooked texture of another version gives "no size" instead of an exception |
| build lists | `game/CMakeLists.txt`, `engine/CMakeLists.txt`, `tests/CMakeLists.txt` | The new files |

G3 (table loader) and G4 (collision-set builders) turned out not to be needed for S1: the table classes and the path
map builders link cleanly.

Checked: the game builds; the scenarios `shared_fx/mm6|mm7|mm8_map_lifecycle`, `release/save_load_roundtrip`,
`release/rebuilt_map_save`, `mm7/grand_temple_moon_cell_exit` and the slice walk pass; the game's unit tests for
events, scripts, doors, images and cooked textures pass; `build-map` parity proves the header-read sizes equal the
decoded ones on every texture those eleven packages use. Not done: MM9 scenarios, a load-time comparison, and a
comparison against a clean tree. The full unit suite has 7 failing cases of 2 897 (house table and MM9 dialogue rows:
3; saves from before rulesets and the save list: 3; the staged Hesper missiles: 1). Other sessions have pending
edits in those areas (`GameDataLoader.cpp`'s house table and dialogue rows among them) and the Hesper case fails on
"isleofashes.blv is not a BLV map" in the stage root, which was reported before this work; but none of the seven
was checked against a tree without these cuts.
